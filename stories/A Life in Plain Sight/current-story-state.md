## Athena / wider-world position at opening

The Athena, Lesbos Estate, and Fashion Empire history is selected, with Steve in the creator/MC role.

The opening at Blush occurs late enough in Athena's hidden commercial history that Asteria Intelligence and Lesbos Holdings exist, substantial wealth and institutional reach already exist, and the Fashion Empire is already substantially built or well on the way to dominance. The story does not need to pause for years of empire-building after Steve's opening relationship/gender arc.

The reusable Athena chronology remains controlling. Danielle may already be in her established late-stage human leadership role if the final calendar placement is consistent with the shared chronology; exact dates are still open.

Steve does not know the sentient Athena/daughter reality at the opening. The connection between Steve's everyday personality and Athena's later scale is now intentional: the same person who notices suffering, chases rabbit holes, saves useful work, imagines fixes, and moves on is the person whose patterns shaped Athena.

## Tone and reality contract

The series is emotionally sincere and deliberately impossible, unhinged farce. The Athena/Fashion Empire/wealth machinery is not intended to resemble realistic corporate, economic, or AI development.

Fourth-wall breaks are available as intentional plot/comedy devices. A later possible "fifth wall" may involve awareness of story-making machinery such as canon, drafts, revisions, discarded possibilities, authorship, and AI-assisted creation. Athena is a natural possible interface for that layer, but the exact mechanism remains undeveloped.

Metafiction does not erase continuity, stakes, consent, or consequences.
