## Chapter storage and approval

- Draft chapters live in `chapters/candidate/`.
- Chapters move to `chapters/approved/` only after the author approves that chapter's text. Approval of an outline is not chapter approval.
- Artifact names use `Series Title - Chapter Number - Chapter Title.ext`. Use zero-padded, series-wide numbers (001, 002, ...), retaining the same number across revisions and approval.
- Example: `A Life in Plain Sight - 001 - The Door.md`. This illustrates a filename; it is not an existing or approved chapter.
- Keep the same filename when moving a candidate to approved. Move the file rather than leaving two active copies.
- Record the chapter number, title, approved revision, approval date/source, and link in the approval register. Update the timeline, current state, relevant character/world facts, and outline status in the same revision.
- For a revision to an approved chapter, preserve the existing approved text and prepare the revision in candidate. On explicit approval of the revision, replace the approved version, remove the candidate, and reconcile downstream continuity. Git retains earlier approved versions.
- Book grouping is recorded in the outline; chapter numbers remain unique across the series.
- Replace only filename-unsafe characters (such as a question mark in a title) in filenames; preserve the full display title inside the chapter.
