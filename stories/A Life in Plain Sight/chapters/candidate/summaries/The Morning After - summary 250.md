# The Morning After — Summary (250 words)

Sunday morning, Steve wakes to twenty messages from Blush Witness Protection. Maddie has given Dennis the napkin accountant a tie, Nat deliberately baits Steve into arguing about his Mini, and Chloe’s simple greeting catches his attention. Steve realizes something genuinely new has entered his formerly quiet life: he has awakened inside an ongoing conversation with people who are there simply because they want to be.

Privately, the five women discuss the previous night. Chloe admits she likes Steve, only to learn she is not alone. Sofia has noticed him; Tori finds his genuine listening and argumentative intelligence attractive; Maddie knows she missed him and valued his attention to her art; Nat bluntly admits he is hot, funny, and kind.

They confront their sexuality honestly: all five are lesbians, while Steve identifies as a man. Brittney joins and confirms she has long suspected Steve might be trans, but establishes the essential boundary: Steve says he is a man, so they treat him as a man. Nobody gets to overrule him or hunt for a hypothetical future woman at the expense of seeing Steve now.

Chloe admits Brittney’s suspicion brought guilty relief. The others acknowledge similar complications but refuse to use Steve’s possible future identity to resolve their own discomfort.

Separately, Tori raises the relationship boundary: their polycule is closed. They agree nobody will independently escalate romantically while they work through their feelings. Steve remains their friend, not a problem or candidate.

Later, Steve realizes he finds all five women attractive and immediately rationalizes it: they are hot, he has eyes, and expanding the problem set would be irresponsible.
