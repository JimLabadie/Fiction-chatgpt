# Chapter 4 — Containment Failure
## 450-word reader teaser

Steve has made it exactly two blocks from Blush when his phone begins vibrating with the urgency of an international incident.

Dennis has been kidnapped.

Or misplaced.

Or possibly taken into protective custody.

The details are unclear, but the alleged victim is a hand-drawn Pegasus wearing a necktie and carrying a briefcase. Maddie insists that Dennis has professional obligations, Nat thinks he needs legal representation, and Tori would appreciate it if everyone stopped texting while driving.

Steve, who went out that evening intending to have one quiet beer, finds himself turning his pink Mini around to return a fictional tax accountant to five women he barely knows.

It should be a five-minute errand.

Instead, his second arrival at Blush becomes far more complicated than the first.

On his way back to the table, Steve notices a woman crying alone at the bar. Her girlfriend has just ended their three-year relationship, and Rachel is struggling to make sense of what happened. Steve stops to offer company, expecting nothing more than to help someone through a terrible evening.

But kindness has a way of revealing things that introductions cannot.

Across the room, Chloe, Nat, Tori, Sofia, and Maddie watch the encounter unfold. They see a side of Steve that his awkward explanations and earlier misunderstandings never quite captured. A few overheard words raise new questions, particularly for Chloe, whose attraction to him is becoming increasingly difficult to dismiss.

When Steve eventually returns, the table welcomes him with its usual mixture of affection and complete nonsense.

There are stories of spectacularly unsuccessful relationships, a debate about emotional-support possums, and a disagreement over the final mozzarella stick that threatens to require legal intervention. Somewhere in the middle of it all, Steve finds himself laughing more freely than he has in a long time.

Then Maddie begins showing him her photography.

Behind her exuberant jokes and theatrical declarations, Steve discovers a thoughtful young woman who sees beauty in ordinary moments. And Maddie discovers something equally unexpected: someone who takes the time to look closely at the things that matter to her.

Their conversation leads to a small confession that changes the meaning of Steve's return.

For Steve, the evening has already become something he never anticipated. He came to Blush as an outsider, uncertain whether he belonged among these women and their easy intimacy. Now, as the night draws to a close, he faces a different uncertainty: what to do when people genuinely want him to stay.

**Chapter 4 is a story about unexpected friendship, gentle attraction, emotional generosity, and the peculiar courage of allowing yourself to belong.**

And, naturally, about a Pegasus whose accounting career may have saved the evening.
