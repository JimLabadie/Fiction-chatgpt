# Chapter 4 — Containment Failure
## 250-word reader teaser

Steve has barely escaped Blush when an emergency summons arrives in the group chat.

Dennis is missing.

Dennis, unfortunately, is a Pegasus. A hand-drawn, necktie-wearing, tax-accounting Pegasus whose disappearance has apparently become a matter of grave importance to five women Steve met only hours earlier.

Against his better judgment—and despite a Sunday full of responsibilities waiting at home—Steve turns his pink Mini around.

Returning should be simple: deliver the drawing, endure Maddie's theatrics, and leave again. But Blush has other plans.

A chance encounter with a heartbroken stranger draws Steve into an unexpectedly intimate moment, one that reveals more about his instinctive kindness than he realizes. From across the room, his new friends watch with growing curiosity, and Chloe finds herself reconsidering feelings she thought she understood.

When Steve finally rejoins the table, the evening becomes a delicious collision of ridiculous arguments, questionable relationship histories, unexpected tenderness, and one fiercely contested mozzarella stick.

Yet beneath the laughter, something important is changing. Steve is discovering what it feels like to be welcomed without having to earn his place. The women are discovering that their newest friend may be far more interesting than their first impressions suggested.

And Maddie, for all her theatrical nonsense, may have a reason for wanting him back that has nothing whatsoever to do with Dennis.

Sometimes the smallest excuse to return leads to the beginning of something much bigger.
