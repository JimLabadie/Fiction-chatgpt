# Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues — Publication Chapter Guide

Status: ACTIVE
Purpose: Publication-facing summaries/teasers for approved chapters. These should entice a reader without functioning as continuity substitutes or revealing more than necessary.

## Chapter 1 — Friday at Eight
**Status:** APPROVED

**Publication teaser:**
Jim Caldwell walks into Blush prepared for the usual awkwardness and finds something far more dangerous: Kate Rivera, a polished regular with impossible hair, excellent heels, and absolutely no interest in letting him hide behind his practiced defenses. What begins with a glance at a stranger's shoes turns into sharp banter, unexpected attraction, and the unsettling possibility that being truly noticed might actually feel good.

**Internal one-line summary:**
Jim meets Kate at Blush; their teasing over shoes, presentation, misgendering, and attraction ends with Jim briefly forgetting to suppress the softer part of himself Kate has noticed.

## Chapter 2 — Kindred Spirits
**Status:** APPROVED

**Publication teaser:**
Jim only wanted to know whether Kate's flirting was real. Somehow that turns into four-and-a-half-inch heels, an argument about who gets to decide how the world reads you, and a catastrophically bad cigar. As Jim and Kate test each other's boundaries—and their own—they discover that kindred spirits don't have to be walking toward the same destination to recognize the road.

**Internal one-line summary:**
Jim and Kate confirm their attraction, turn presentation and a disastrous cigar into reciprocal gender play, recognize their shared awkwardness with conventional masculinity, and leave Blush together as self-declared kindred spirits.

## Chapter 3 — Worth the Risk
**Status:** APPROVED

**Publication teaser:**
A walk after Blush becomes a challenge, the challenge becomes a thrift-store competition, and the competition becomes something that is absolutely, emphatically, definitely not a date. Between flannel, boots, a flamingo lamp, and one dangerously gorgeous pair of heels, Jim and Kate discover that taking a stereotype seriously may be much less fun than deliberately making a mess of it.

**Internal one-line summary:**
Jim and Kate turn a community thrift fundraiser into a flirt-heavy lesbian-stereotype challenge; Jim's joy and unguarded attraction to a pair of heels deepen Kate's interest before they leave together to find dinner, still denying they are dating.
