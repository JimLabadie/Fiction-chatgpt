# Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues — Character Bible

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

## Jim Caldwell
Story namespace: Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues.
Opening presentation/name: Jim Caldwell.
Developing feminine identity/name: Emily Caldwell.

### Established opening state
- Mid-thirties.
- 5 feet 6 inches tall and 105 pounds.
- Naturally looks notably feminine despite presenting as male.
- Often misgendered.
- Opening presentation is corporate casual and deliberately masculine/ordinary; exact garments are not yet established.
- Jim has learned to brace for other people's gendered readings of him and may anticipate correction or awkwardness before it occurs.
- Occupation: software engineer; exact employer/company name is intentionally not yet established.
- At Blush, he notices presentation details such as women's shoes and clothing with close, thoughtful attention.
- He is capable of quick, dry humor even while nervous.
- Kate's direct attraction and acceptance visibly disarm him.

### Identity boundary
- Jim is the protagonist's opening name/presentation.
- Emily Caldwell is who Jim becomes / the feminine identity the story will develop.
- Jim/Emily's self-understanding must emerge through the story rather than being dictated by Kate or outside interpretation.
- Emotional direction is integration/wholeness, not competing personalities.
- Final labels, pronoun progression, transition choices, and timing are not yet established.

## Kate Rivera
Story namespace: Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues.

### Established state
- Mid-thirties.
- Central American.
- Strong woman and a middle manager at a logistics company; exact employer/company name is intentionally not yet established.
- More masculine-looking in her underlying physical features.
- Presents very femme in a polished corporate way and is deliberately emphatic about that presentation.
- Corporate casual at the opening; exact garments are not yet established.
- Has extraordinarily long, thick, Rapunzel-like hair, conspicuous enough to function as a signature feature.
- Regular at Blush but not an employee.
- Does not live in St. Claire.
- Lonely and somewhat tired of the usual dating/social routine.
- Finds Jim genuinely different and interesting; attraction is not based on treating him as a project.
- Direct, observant, playful, and capable of letting sincerity land without forcing a label.
- Understands from her own experience what it means for strangers to read physical features before deliberate presentation.

### Relationship with Jim
At the end of approved Chapter One, Kate and Jim have just met at Blush. Kate has openly told Jim she likes him and called him pretty while accepting his immediate statement that he is a man without argument. Their mutual interest is developing; no commitment, relationship status, or predetermined outcome is established.
