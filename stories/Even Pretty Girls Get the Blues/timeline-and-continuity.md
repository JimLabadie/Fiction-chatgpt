### Approved Chapter 1 sequence
1. Jim Caldwell pauses outside Blush before entering.
2. Clara Bennett checks Jim's ID without making the gendered correction Jim has come to expect.
3. Jim enters and takes a place at the main bar, two stools from Kate Rivera.
4. Jules Taka serves Jim.
5. Kate observes Jim's attention to another woman's shoes and initiates conversation by identifying the heel height as four inches.
6. Jim jokes about Kate's extraordinary hair, comparing it to federal highway infrastructure; Kate genuinely laughs.
7. They exchange names and continue flirting/teasing.
8. Jim tells Kate he is often misgendered.
9. Kate notes Clara was not confused and does not interrogate Jim's identity.
10. Kate notices Jim repeatedly softening or expressing himself naturally and then correcting/suppressing it.
11. Kate tells Jim he is pretty when annoyed.
12. Jim urgently says, “I'm a man.” Kate answers, “I heard you,” and does not withdraw the compliment.
13. Jim's attention returns to Kate's shoes. She invites the question; he asks how high they are.
14. Chapter ends with Jim smiling and forgetting to put the smile away.
