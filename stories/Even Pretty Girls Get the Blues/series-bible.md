# Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
Working title: **Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues**
Mode: private fiction
Main character: **Jim Caldwell / Emily Caldwell**
Primary setting: **St. Claire**

## Premise

Jim Caldwell enters Blush as the ordinary male-presenting version of himself. Kate sees Jim, but also begins to perceive Emily Caldwell: not as a diagnosis she announces or an identity she owns for him, but as a feminine self hiding in plain sight.

The story is intended to be light, warm, funny, flirtatious, sapphic, and emotionally restorative. Kate's attention gives Jim/Emily room to feel seen, desired, playful, and increasingly whole without turning the interaction into a gender seminar or forced revelation.

## Established identity boundary

- Jim Caldwell is the protagonist's opening name/presentation.
- Emily Caldwell is who Jim becomes / the feminine identity the story will develop.
- Kate may notice, invite, flirt, tease, and create room for Emily; she does not get to dictate Jim/Emily's identity.
- The emotional goal is integration and wholeness rather than treating Jim and Emily as competing people.
- This story is separate from **The Neon Sign**, **A Life in Plain Sight**, and the separate Jim/Claire story.

## Opening scene seed

The opening scene begins at **Blush**. Jim arrives as plain old Jim. Kate sees him and, through behavior and interaction rather than supernatural certainty, notices the woman Emily Caldwell hiding there. The desired register is fun and flirty with genuine tenderness underneath.

## Open development

Kate's canonical full name, role at Blush, physical presentation, history, and relationship trajectory with Jim/Emily are not yet established and must not be invented as canon without development.
