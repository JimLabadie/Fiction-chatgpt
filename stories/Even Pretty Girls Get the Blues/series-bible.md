# Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
Working title: **Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues**
Mode: private fiction
Main character: **Jim Caldwell / Emily Caldwell**
Primary setting: **St. Claire**

## Premise

Jim Caldwell enters Blush as the ordinary male-presenting version of himself. Kate sees Jim, but also begins to perceive Emily Caldwell: not as a diagnosis she announces or an identity she owns for him, but as a feminine self hiding in plain sight.

The story is intended to be light, warm, funny, flirtatious, sapphic, and emotionally restorative. Kate's attention gives Jim/Emily room to feel seen, desired, playful, and increasingly whole without turning the interaction into a gender seminar or forced revelation.

## Established identity boundary

- Jim Caldwell is the protagonist's opening name/presentation.
- Emily Caldwell is who Jim becomes / the feminine identity the story will develop.
- Kate may notice, invite, flirt, tease, and create room for Emily; she does not get to dictate Jim/Emily's identity.
- The emotional goal is integration and wholeness rather than treating Jim and Emily as competing people.
- This story is separate from **The Neon Sign**, **A Life in Plain Sight**, and the separate Jim/Claire story.

## Kate — established development

Kate is a new character created for this story.

- Her full name is **Kate Rivera**.
- She is Central American.
- She is a strong woman and a manager.
- Her presentation is femme in a polished, corporate way.
- She has extraordinarily long, thick, Rapunzel-like hair: a conspicuous feminine signature rather than a subtle background feature.
- She is a regular at Blush, not an employee there.
- She is not a resident of St. Claire; she comes into the district and Blush from outside.
- Beneath the capable professional surface, she is lonely and a little tired of the usual dating/social routine.
- Her attraction to Jim/Emily should grow from finding something genuinely different and interesting rather than from treating Jim as a project.

Still open: Kate's age, exact management field/employer, home neighborhood/city context, further appearance details, and prior relationship history.

## Opening scene seed

The opening scene begins at **Blush at 8:00 PM on a Friday night**. Jim arrives as plain old Jim. Kate is there as a regular. Kate sees him and, through behavior and interaction rather than supernatural certainty, notices the woman Emily Caldwell hiding there. The desired register is fun and flirty with genuine tenderness underneath.

Kate is not working the bar and must not be drafted as Blush staff. Canonical Blush staffing and establishment detail must be resolved separately from St. Claire authority before prose.

## Open development

Kate Rivera's remaining open character details should be established as they become materially necessary under project governance. Her relationship trajectory with Jim/Emily remains developmental rather than predetermined beyond the opening attraction/recognition premise.
