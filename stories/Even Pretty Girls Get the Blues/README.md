# Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
Title status: Working title established.

## Overview
A warm, organic sapphic romantic story about Jim Caldwell and Kate Rivera meeting at Blush in St. Claire. Jim presents as male while naturally looking notably feminine and is often misgendered; Kate has more masculine-looking underlying features while presenting in an emphatically polished femme way. Their first encounter develops through attraction, humor, recognition, and the shared experience of being read by other people in ways they have learned to negotiate.

The story is intentionally allowed to develop organically rather than being forced through a predetermined transformation or relationship outline. Persistent records preserve what is established without predetermining what happens next.

## Chapter workflow
Candidate prose belongs in `chapters/candidate/`. Jim's exact approval is required before prose is copied verbatim into `chapters/approved/`.

Every approved chapter should also receive:
- a publication-oriented chapter summary/teaser that entices without replacing the chapter;
- continuity/detail extraction sufficient to preserve new established facts and scene state;
- an update to the approval register and current story state.
