# Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues — Voice and Style

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

## Governing story feel
Warm, funny, flirtatious, sapphic, emotionally restorative romantic fiction.

The story should feel **organic rather than heavily plotted**. Persistence protects discoveries after they happen; it should not force the characters through a predetermined transformation checklist or relationship schedule.

## Voice
- Character-driven humor and banter.
- Emotional sincerity is allowed to land without becoming solemn or clinical.
- Comedy can immediately follow tenderness without invalidating it.
- Observational narration may be witty and affectionate.
- Physical behavior, pauses, glances, posture, and presentation carry subtext.
- Gender play may be intimate, silly, exploratory, and fun.
- Avoid gender seminars, diagnostic narration, or treating recognition as supernatural certainty.

## Jim and Kate dynamic
They are mirrors without being copies.
- Jim naturally reads feminine to others and works to present more unmistakably male.
- Kate has more masculine-looking underlying features and works to present unmistakably femme.
- Each understands something about negotiating with other people's readings.
- Neither owns the answer to the other's identity.
- Attraction should coexist with curiosity, teasing, respect, and genuine liking.

## Organic-development rule
Do not decide in advance exactly when Emily emerges, what Jim/Emily ultimately labels themself, or what final relationship form Jim/Emily and Kate take unless Jim establishes it. Let scene consequences create later possibilities.

## Humor boundary
The characters may laugh at themselves, failed performances, overcompensation, awkwardness, fashion, bad ideas, and each other affectionately. Jim/Emily's femininity or identity is not itself the punchline.
