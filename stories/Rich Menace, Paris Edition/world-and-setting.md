# Rich Menace, Paris Edition — World and Setting

Reusable setting authority: `system bible/regency-hill-world-bible.md`

## Established
- A wedding weekend in Paris is central to the premise.
- The story begins in **Regency Hill**, a neighborhood in the same city as St. Claire and directly bordering St. Claire.
- Tim Johnson is in Regency Hill, waiting for a bus outside a dress shop, when Allison Kensington approaches him.
- Regency Hill is deliberately outside St. Claire. Tim is not currently established as a St. Claire resident or member, and the story does not bind him to St. Claire at inception.
- Tim is established as a man and the intended story direction is **not a transgender arc**. He can become a very comfortable cross-dresser, genuinely enjoy feminine presentation, and may later consider body choices such as implants while remaining a guy. His proximity to St. Claire does not create or imply a gender-identity realization.

## Not yet established
- Tim's exact residence within or outside Regency Hill.
- The wealthy woman's home/base, wealth source, and life infrastructure.
- Wedding venue and wedding-party/family structure.
- The opening boutique is **Maison Belle**, an upscale independent women's clothing boutique in Regency Hill, with a public bus stop immediately outside or directly adjacent. It is owned by **Camille Moreau**; **Sophie Bennett** works its register/sales floor. Chapter One establishes a spacious upscale interior with deliberately arranged racks/displays and fitting rooms. Exact brands, broader inventory, hours, and other unnecessary operating detail remain unresolved.
- Allison is established in Chapter One as having a **Bentley** available to take Tim home. Exact model/specification remains unresolved.
- Whether later story events create any actual St. Claire binding; proximity alone does not.
