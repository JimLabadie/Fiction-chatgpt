# Rich Menace, Paris Edition — Series Bible

## Status
In development.

## Premise
Tim Johnson, an ordinary man waiting for a bus outside a dress shop, is approached by a wealthy woman who offers him a weekend in Paris if he will pretend to be her girlfriend at a wedding.

Tim is male and understands himself straightforwardly as a guy. He is physically small/proportioned such that women's ready-to-wear sizing can plausibly work for him. Feminine clothing, grooming, and presentation do not threaten his identity and do not themselves imply a hidden gender identity. His baseline attitude toward "girl stuff" is largely pragmatic and unbothered rather than enthusiastic: if an attractive woman likes him while he is presenting as a woman, that is good enough for him.

The story may explore what Tim individually enjoys or dislikes about the experience without predetermining an identity destination.
