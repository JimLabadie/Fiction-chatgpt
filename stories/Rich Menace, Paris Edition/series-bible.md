# Rich Menace, Paris Edition — Series Bible

## Status
In development. Chapter One — **Software Engineer Barbie** — is FINAL / APPROVED.

## Premise
Tim Johnson, an ordinary man waiting for a bus outside a dress shop, is approached by a wealthy woman who offers him a weekend in Paris if he will pretend to be her girlfriend at a wedding.

Tim is male and understands himself straightforwardly as a guy. He is physically small/proportioned such that women's ready-to-wear sizing can plausibly work for him. Feminine clothing, grooming, and presentation do not threaten his identity and do not themselves imply a hidden gender identity. His baseline attitude toward "girl stuff" is largely pragmatic and unbothered rather than enthusiastic: if an attractive woman likes him while he is presenting as a woman, that is good enough for him.

Tim's established direction is that he remains a man. He can become a very comfortable cross-dresser, genuinely enjoy feminine clothing, grooming, beauty, and presentation, and may later consider body choices such as implants without those choices redefining his gender identity or constituting a transgender arc.

## Mode / audience
Queer romantic comedy with a playful, warm, sexy tone that does not take itself too seriously. Emotional sincerity can coexist with absurdity and character-driven comedy.

## Main character
Tim Johnson.

## POV / tense
Close third-person centered on Tim Johnson; past tense.

## Setting / shared-world binding
The story opens in Regency Hill, a deliberately lightweight affluent neighborhood in the same city as and directly bordering St. Claire but outside it. Maison Belle is the established Regency Hill boutique used in Chapter One. Paris wedding weekend remains central. Tim lives in a clean, organized apartment in Regency Hill a couple of miles from Maison Belle; the home is deliberately not gender-coded and reflects ordinary adult competence. Allison is a society woman and CEO of **Kensington Logistics**, the logistics company her family built. At the story's current level of detail, Kensington Logistics ships stuff, competent people work there, and things move; do not overbuild its operations unless the story needs them. Allison lives in a warm, feminine Regency Hill house large enough to raise a family in, at the opposite end of the neighborhood from Tim/Maison Belle and along the edge touching St. Claire; it remains outside St. Claire. Allison's ex is **Clara Smyth**, another heiress. The bride is **Julie Smith**, one of Allison's former sorority sisters. Julie is the bride. **Allison and Clara are bridesmaids** in Julie's wedding. Allison and Clara's breakup was **messy**, and they are now **civil to each other**. No deeper breakup explanation is required. Detailed family/company structure beyond what is established, wedding venue, and any later shared-world binding remain unresolved until consequential.

## Foundational rules
- Tim remains the owner of his identity and choices.
- Feminine presentation is not degradation.
- Enjoying, tolerating, or being attractive in feminine presentation does not by itself establish a different gender identity.
- No transformation mechanic has been established.
