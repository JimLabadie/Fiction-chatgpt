# Rich Menace, Paris Edition — Timeline and Continuity

## Accepted events
- Tim Johnson waits for a bus outside Maison Belle after his car dies and is being repaired.
- Allison Kensington approaches him and offers a weekend in Paris if he will pretend to be her girlfriend at a wedding so she can make her ex jealous and win the breakup.
- Tim accepts on the condition that Allison pays for the requirements of "Software Engineer Barbie" and that he still gets to code.
- Tim and Allison enter Maison Belle, where owner Camille Moreau and sales associate Sophie Bennett begin a diagnostic styling experiment.
- Tim establishes himself as warm, curious, and a quick study; he asks increasingly insightful questions, treats Camille, Sophie, and Allison's judgments as meaningful, and learns presentation principles rather than receiving magical competence.
- Tim's first dress reveals that Allison's instinct was spectacularly correct: Tim is extraordinarily attractive in feminine presentation, and Allison visibly loses composure.
- Tim notices Allison's reaction, recruits Sophie and Camille into helping him understand the mechanics of feminine presentation, and rapidly applies what he learns.
- Tim deliberately tests posture, movement, expression, and awareness against Allison's reaction, discovers that feminine presentation can short-circuit her composure, and recognizes the competitive possibilities.
- Allison realizes she has not merely recruited an accessory for her breakup scheme; she has recruited competition. Tim accepts the challenge, ending Chapter One with the conclusion that the weekend is going to be fun.
- Allison's ex is established in the approved manuscript as a woman; her identity, wedding role, and breakup history remain unresolved.
- Allison is established in the approved manuscript as owning/using a Bentley for the opening encounter; exact model/specification remains unresolved.
