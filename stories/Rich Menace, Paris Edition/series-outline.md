- The campaign does **not** jump directly from Maison Belle to the Paris weekend. The **weeks before Paris** are part of the romantic/comic engine.

The public-couple appearances he can justify as cover maintenance become real dates in every practical sense: time together, shared routines, attraction, learning each other, and Tim deliberately courting/seducing Allison while continuing to let the original scheme provide plausible deniability.
