# Chloe Johnson

Status: ACTIVE CHARACTER RECORD
Story: Rich Menace, Paris Edition

## Current continuity
- The approved first two chapters use Tim Johnson because the story has not yet reached the Chapter Three name resolution.
- Near the opening of Chapter Three, the protagonist chooses **Chloe Johnson** and uses **she/her** from that point forward.
- Tim remains a real and respected part of Chloe's history.
- Chloe loves femininity and is happy living as Chloe.
- Chloe is a transgender woman. This is an ordinary fact about her, not the plot.
- Immediately after the name discussion, Chloe sends HR a practical name/pronoun update.
- If her earlier history becomes relevant publicly, Chloe and Allison keep the explanation truthful and private rather than inventing a false biography.

## Core character
- 35; software engineer; Regency Hill resident.
- Long hair; extraordinary androgynous-model beauty.
- Quick study, pragmatic, unpanicked, radically self-possessed, competitive, kind, warm, loving.
- Learns principles and adapts; no unexplained instant expertise.
- Observes Allison closely and treats Allison's competence as attractive.
- Not threatened by Allison's wealth, decisiveness, femininity, or ability to lead.
- Chloe deliberately explores courting Allison without treating Allison's affection as owed.

## Ordinary life
- Clean, organized Regency Hill apartment a couple of miles from Maison Belle.
- Likes baseball and softball; plays cards.
- Has five close friends; individual details remain deferred.
- Parents: Steve Johnson and Dorothy “Dot” Johnson.
- Sister: Allison Johnson.
- Steve and Dot knew Gary and Jenny Kensington in school; they liked each other but were in different circles.

## Domesticity, sewing, and care
- Chloe loves domesticity because caring for someone is one of her love languages.
- This does not make Allison incompetent and is not a gender rule.
- Chloe loves sewing and makes prom dresses for girls who cannot afford them.
- Sewing gives Chloe technical knowledge of fabric, construction, fit, and alteration without making her automatically expert in every presentation domain.
- Fashion becomes common ground with Allison: Chloe brings maker/construction knowledge; Allison brings aesthetic and wearer experience.

## Relationship with Allison
- Chloe is strongly attracted to Allison and increasingly serious about building a real relationship beneath the original fake-girlfriend premise.
- Chloe's practical acts of service complement Allison's steady, grounding style of love.
- Chloe is comfortable with Allison having a past. Allison's old photographs of Clara Smyth are history, not something Chloe needs erased.
- Chloe makes room for Allison to return to photography, and they discover a new common interest through it.
