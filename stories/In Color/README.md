# In Color

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
Title status: Working title approved by Jim on 2026-09-20.

## Overview
A warm, playful sapphic romantic story about Jim's sudden recognition of a feminine self during a dress-shopping trip with his girlfriend Claire. During that first day, Jim chooses the name Emily and begins experiencing femininity not as costume or obligation but as joyful self-recognition — described as the world changing from black and white to color.

This series is newly initialized from sustained chat development. Pre-initialization prose remains candidate material until Jim approves exact manuscript text.
