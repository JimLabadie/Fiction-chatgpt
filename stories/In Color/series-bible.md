# In Color — Series Bible

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
Mode: Private persona fiction.
Working title: **In Color**, chosen by Jim on 2026-09-20.

## Durable premise
Jim begins the day as Claire's boyfriend and accompanies her on what begins as a dress-shopping experience. Claire has perceived femininity in Jim that Jim has not recognized. The experience opens a joyful, startling self-recognition: Jim discovers that being pretty can be something felt from the inside, not merely observed from outside.

During the day Jim chooses the name **Emily** and recognizes that she wants to present as herself rather than return to the prior masculine presentation simply because it is easier. The central metaphor Jim/Emily supplies is movement from **black and white to color**: life before was not necessarily miserable or empty, but this newly discovered way of being makes available a kind of joy she had not known existed.

## Core relationship
Emily and Claire are already in love before the discovery. Claire's role is loving recognition, encouragement, teasing, practical guidance, desire, and partnership — not manufacturing Emily's identity. Emily's recognition and choices belong to Emily.

Femininity, gender discovery, and sapphic identity are never humiliation or degradation. Comedy may target Emily's obliviousness, sudden enthusiasm, escalating tastes, shopping chaos, and Claire's delighted inability to resist her.

## Identity boundary
Emily has chosen the name Emily and positively responds to being Claire's girl and girlfriend. She wants to go to work the following day as herself rather than send the old masculine presentation in her place.

A final identity label, transition plan, anatomy/body-change plan, and longer-term social/medical/legal choices are **not yet established**.

## Relationship boundary
Claire loves Emily and finds her intensely desirable. Emily explicitly wants to be pretty both for herself and for Claire. Neither woman's love or attraction is contingent on Emily passing a beauty test.

## Established thematic language
- **Black and white to color:** Emily's own metaphor for discovering an internal experience of beauty and femininity she did not previously know was available to her.
- **Pretty from the inside:** beauty as lived self-recognition rather than merely external appearance.
- **Mine:** Emily's desire for feminine things that belong to her rather than borrowed items or temporary costume.
- **My girl / girlfriend:** emotionally significant relationship language accepted joyfully by Emily.
- **Princess:** playful nickname Emily explicitly accepts.
