## Emily Alvarez
Story namespace: In Color.
Earlier name/presentation: Jim.
Relationship: Claire's romantic partner; by the current story state Emily joyfully identifies herself as Claire's girlfriend.

### Established character state
- Begins the day without recognizing the extent of her own desire for femininity.
- Trusts Claire deeply.
- Discovery is enthusiastic rather than reluctant once Emily understands what she is feeling.
- Wants to try things, fail, succeed, learn, and actively experience femininity rather than be protected from it.
- Wants to be pretty for herself **and for Claire**.
- Chooses the name **Emily** during the shopping day and says, “I think that's me.”
- Experiences the discovery as moving from black and white to color.
- Does not want to hide the discovery by reverting to the old masculine presentation at work the next day.
- Quickly reveals strong high-femme tastes and opinions once she realizes she is allowed to have them.
- Can be delightfully high-maintenance; the comedy is affectionate and chosen, never degradation.

### Current presentation

## Claire Dupont
Story namespace: In Color.
Relationship: Emily's girlfriend.

### Established character state
- Already loves Emily before the shopping-day discovery.
- Saw femininity/beauty in Emily before Emily recognized it herself.
- Protective and loving, but early in the day becomes too cautious about treating exploration like a minefield; Emily explicitly asks her to stop tiptoeing and actively help.
- Once corrected, Claire becomes delighted, teasing, practical, and enthusiastically participatory.
- Respects genuine refusal; femininity is never imposed as coercion.
- Finds Emily's increasingly high-femme presentation intensely attractive.
- Uses affectionate teasing including “princess,” “my girl,” and jokes about Emily becoming high-maintenance.
- Makes clear Emily does not need to become beautiful enough to deserve her; Claire already chose and loved her.
