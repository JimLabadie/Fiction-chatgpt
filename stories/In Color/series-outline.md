# In Color — Series Outline

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

## Established opening arc
1. Claire takes Jim dress shopping after perceiving a feminine possibility Jim has not recognized.
2. Jim tries a soft blue dress, then independently notices and asks for a dark green dress.
3. In the fitting room Jim swishes the skirt, experiences herself as pretty, and begins asking about hair, makeup, jewelry, and shoes.
4. Jim looks at the socks/feet and says, “Ick, I think I need shoes, too.”
5. Exploration expands from a dress into a whole feminine presentation.
6. Jim tells Claire the discovery feels true and exciting and asks Claire to stop treating it like a minefield.
7. Jim explicitly wants Claire's advice and wants to try, fail, succeed, and do things.
8. “Princess” becomes an accepted nickname.
9. Jim/Emily credits Claire for seeing her while Claire protects Emily's agency; **Visa** becomes part of that emotional negotiation.
10. Emily explains the experience as going from black and white to color and decides she will not put the color back in the box.
11. Claire calls her “my girl”; the phrase lands positively.
12. Emily decides to go to work the next day as herself rather than return to the prior masculine presentation.
13. Shopping escalates: clothes, shoes, shape/silhouette preferences, nails, brows, ears/earrings, perfume, hair, makeup, and related high-femme discovery.
14. Jim rejects “Jim” as a lesbian name and chooses **Emily**. The name lands as self-recognition.
15. Emily discovers thirty-inch extensions and wants “princess hair.”
16. At the salon Emily discovers acrylic nails and negotiates medium-long almond nails; the salon menu creates further comic escalation.
17. At Sephora Emily begins building **her** own makeup collection and fragrance rather than merely borrowing Claire's.
18. “Girlfriend” lands as another powerful recognition.
19. Emily becomes afraid to look in the mirror because she feels beautiful inside and fears the reflection may fall short — especially of what she thinks Claire deserves.
20. Claire makes clear this is not an audition and Emily does not have to become beautiful enough to deserve her.
21. Emily looks and recognizes herself: “That's me.”
22. They return home. Claire's attraction to Emily's high-femme presentation is openly, comically intense while the emotional core remains that Claire loved Emily before and loves her now.
