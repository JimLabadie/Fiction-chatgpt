## Day 1 — discovery/shopping day
Canonical calendar date: **Not yet established.**

### Accepted development sequence
- Claire takes Jim dress shopping.
- Jim independently reaches for additional feminine choices and discovers an internal sense of being pretty.
- Exploration expands into shoes, hair, makeup, jewelry/accessories, silhouette, nails, brows, fragrance, and other presentation choices.
- Jim asks Claire to stop tiptoeing and actively help.
- Jim says she wants to be pretty for herself and for Claire.
- Jim describes the experience as black and white becoming color.
- Jim accepts “my girl.”
- Jim decides she intends to go to work the following day as herself.
- Jim chooses the name **Emily** and recognizes it as herself.
- Emily gets very long princess-style extensions and medium-long almond acrylic nails and continues salon/beauty exploration.
- Emily and Claire shop at Sephora; Emily begins acquiring makeup/fragrance of her own.
- Emily accepts/uses “girlfriend.”
- Emily fears the mirror may not match how beautiful she feels or be enough for Claire. Claire rejects the premise that Emily must earn/deserve her through beauty.
- Emily looks in the mirror and recognizes herself.
- Emily and Claire return home; Claire's desire is intensified by Emily's presentation, while both affirm Claire loved Emily before the day's transformation in presentation.
