# In Color — Voice and Style

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

## Series voice
Warm, character-driven sapphic romantic comedy with:
- quick affectionate banter;
- playful snark without cruelty;
- emotional sincerity allowed to land;
- strong physical embodiment and scene texture rather than dialogue floating in space;
- comedy beside tenderness rather than comedy used to destroy tenderness;
- queer/sapphic sensibility as part of the story's engine;
- joyful high-femme fantasy, escalation, and occasional ridiculousness;
- direct, organic prose rather than clinical or therapeutic framing.

## Emotional rule
Emily's femininity and gender discovery are sources of recognition, joy, desire, vulnerability, and possibility — never humiliation or degradation.

Claire may tease Emily about obliviousness, expensive tastes, shopping escalation, acrylic nails, enormous hair, or sudden high-maintenance opinions. She does not mock Emily for being feminine, trans/questioning, or sapphic.

## Agency rule
Claire may open doors, suggest, teach, encourage, flirt, tease, and enthusiastically participate. Emily's recognition belongs to Emily. When Emily genuinely refuses something, the refusal is respected.

## Description
Preserve successful dialogue and render the physical world around it: clothes, hair, makeup, footwear, accessories, movement, setting, spatial relationships, body language, sensory detail, and subtext.

## Style reference boundary
The desired qualities are recorded directly here. No external living author's style is governing authority for the prose.
