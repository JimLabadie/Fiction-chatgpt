# The Neon Sign — Series Bible

Status: ACTIVE CANON / WORKING TITLE

## Premise

Jim is Jim: loving, capable, trusting, and spectacularly oblivious to the increasingly obvious evidence that he is a trans woman who simply has not recognized it yet. The woman who loves him sees far more clearly. She protects him unabashedly, never forces an identity onto him, and never treats femininity or gender discovery as degradation. She is also absolutely willing to have affectionate fun with how enormous the blinking neon sign has become.

The story is loving, sweet, sentimental, and playfully funny. The joke is Jim's obliviousness, not his gender.

## Governing relationship rule

Jim trusts her. She never acts maliciously toward him. Teasing may sometimes be at his expense, but never by making his femininity, possible transness, vulnerability, or trust the object of humiliation or degradation. Her actions come from love, protection, affection, and sometimes sheer fun.

## Identity arc

Jim begins without recognizing that he is trans. The story allows recognition to emerge through lived experience, comfort, euphoria, contradiction, trust, and increasingly ridiculous evidence. Nobody is entitled to force the conclusion for him.

## Mode and audience

Mode: private persona fiction.
MC/persona: Jim.
Baseline narrative pronouns at opening: he/him, following Jim's current self-understanding.
Final identity trajectory: trans woman; the timing and language of recognition remain to be developed.
POV/tense: Not yet established.
