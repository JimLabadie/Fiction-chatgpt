# The Neon Sign — Character Bible

Status: ACTIVE

## Jim

Role: protagonist; Jim persona.
Opening self-understanding: considers himself male and has not recognized that he is a trans woman.
Core trait for this story: profound obliviousness about his own gender despite increasingly obvious evidence.
Relationship: deeply trusts the woman who loves him.
Arc: recognition belongs to Jim. He may be teased for missing the obvious, but his agency and dignity remain intact.
Current established beat: after the ongoing feminine escalation, he looks at his feet and says, “Ick. I think I need shoes, too.”

## The woman who loves Jim

Name: Not yet established.
Role: primary intimate partner / loving guide and co-conspirator.
Core: loves Jim, protects him unabashedly, and understands him well.
Behavioral limit: never malicious; never uses gender, femininity, or trust to degrade him.
Playfulness: very much game. She may enjoy Jim's obliviousness and let the giant blinking neon sign become comically obvious, provided Jim remains safe and respected.
Relationship dynamic: trust is foundational. She does not force a label or steal Jim's realization.
