# Chapter Three — Terms and Conditions Apply

Steve learned something important approximately twelve seconds after entering the house.

Never allow seven lesbians to see you wearing heels for the first time while standing near a staircase.

“STOP.”

Steve froze with one foot inside the door.

“What?”

“Don't move.”

Maddie came running from the kitchen.

Steve looked down at herself.

Green dress.

Green purse.

Heels.

Nothing appeared to be on fire.

“What the hell is wrong?”

Maddie stopped three feet away.

Her hands flew to her mouth.

“Oh my God.”

Steve looked at Britt.

“You brought me here under false pretenses.”

“I said dinner.”

“You failed to mention whatever this is.”

Maddie pointed.

“The shoes.”

“Yes. They're shoes.”

“They're *your* shoes.”

“They are technically Britt's shoes.”

“Nope,” Britt said.

Steve turned.

“What?”

“They're yours.”

“You said they were returnable.”

“They were.”

“Were?”

“You wore them outside.”

Steve stared at her.

“That isn't how returns work.”

“It is in this house.”

“You cannot simply transfer ownership of objects through pavement.”

Chloe appeared in the hallway holding a glass of wine.

“She has a point.”

“Thank you.”

“The purse is also yours.”

Steve's mouth fell open.

“That wasn't the point.”

“You carried it outside too.”

“THAT IS NOT A LEGAL PRINCIPLE.”

Chloe took a sip.

“Possession is nine-tenths of lesbian law.”

“There is no lesbian law.”

Chloe smiled.

“Spoken like someone unfamiliar with the appellate process.”

Steve pointed at her.

“You're going to be a problem tonight.”

“I've been looking forward to it all week.”

Steve's stomach fluttered.

Goddammit.

Then Maddie hugged her.

Steve laughed and hugged her back.

“Hi.”

“Hi, pretty girl.”

That hit differently when spoken aloud.

Steve felt herself blush.

Maddie noticed.

Steve immediately pointed at her.

“No.”

“I didn't say anything!”

“You were about to.”

“I was going to say you look pretty.”

“You already said that.”

“I was going to say it again.”

Steve sighed.

“Fine.”

“You look pretty.”

“Thank you.”

Maddie beamed as though she'd personally accomplished something.

Then Liz came around the corner.

Steve forgot how language worked.

Liz was wearing a simple black dress.

Nothing dramatic.

Nothing elaborate.

She was just—

Liz.

And Steve was apparently an idiot.

Liz stopped when she saw her.

Her smile came slowly.

“Hi.”

Steve swallowed.

“Hi.”

Britt leaned toward Chloe.

“We've lost her.”

“Completely.”

Steve didn't look away from Liz.

“Fuck off.”

Liz laughed.

Then she crossed the room.

She didn't immediately hug Steve.

She stopped in front of her.

“Still okay?”

Steve knew exactly what she meant.

The dress.

The heels.

The purse.

The pronoun.

Everything.

Steve smiled.

“Yeah.”

Liz opened her arms.

Steve went into them.

“Then welcome home.”

There was that word again.

Steve closed her eyes.

Just for a second.

Home.

---

Dinner began normally.

That lasted four minutes.

Steve should have known something was wrong when nobody commented on her shoes during appetizers.

She became suspicious during the salad.

By the time Maddie brought out dinner, Steve had identified the problem.

They were behaving.

All of them.

It was horrifying.

Steve put down her fork.

“Okay.”

Seven women looked at her.

“What?”

Nobody answered.

Steve narrowed her eyes.

“Why are you all being normal?”

Nat frowned.

“We're normal.”

“No, you're not.”

“Rude.”

“I've been here twenty minutes and nobody has called me a girl, criticized my clothes, proposed an unauthorized salon appointment, or attempted to establish new principles of lesbian jurisprudence.”

Chloe lifted one eyebrow.

“Are you disappointed?”

“No.”

Too fast.

Seven faces changed.

Steve pointed around the table.

“Don't.”

Britt smiled.

“You brought it up.”

“I am identifying anomalous behavior.”

“She's disappointed,” Nat said.

“I am not disappointed.”

Maddie raised her hand.

Steve stared at her.

“Why are you raising your hand?”

“I have a question.”

“This isn't school.”

“Can I ask it?”

Steve sighed.

“Yes.”

“Can we dress you?”

Steve blinked.

There it was.

She looked around the table.

Nobody seemed surprised.

“You've discussed this.”

“No,” Liz said.

“Yes,” Chloe said simultaneously.

Liz glared at her.

Steve leaned back.

“Aha.”

“It wasn't a formal discussion,” Liz said.

“There were minutes,” Chloe said.

“CHLOE.”

“What? Nat took notes.”

Nat suddenly became fascinated by her potatoes.

Steve looked at her.

“Natalie.”

“They're not *minutes.*”

“What are they?”

“Action items.”

Steve closed her eyes.

“Jesus Christ.”

Maddie was still patiently holding her hand up.

Steve pointed at her.

“You may lower your hand.”

She did.

“Define ‘dress me.’”

Seven women exchanged looks.

Steve immediately raised a finger.

“No. Absolutely not. There will be no silent committee consultation. One spokesperson.”

Everyone looked at Liz.

Liz looked offended.

“Why me?”

“Because we trust you,” Emily said.

“That seems reckless,” Chloe added.

Steve nodded.

“I agree with Chloe.”

“See?” Chloe said. “We're bonding.”

Liz sighed.

“Okay. Clothes.”

“Obviously.”

“Shoes.”

Steve looked down at the heels.

“Apparently.”

“Accessories.”

Steve picked up the green purse.

“I've already been victimized by accessories.”

“That purse saved your evening,” Britt said.

“It carried three objects.”

“Exactly.”

Liz continued.

“Hair.”

“No.”

“Okay.”

Immediate.

No argument.

Steve noticed.

Again.

“Makeup?” Liz asked.

Steve hesitated.

Seven women remained perfectly still.

“Define makeup.”

Chloe leaned toward Nat.

“We've entered treaty negotiations.”

Steve pointed at her.

“Quiet in the gallery.”

Chloe mimed zipping her lips.

Liz was fighting a smile.

“Whatever you're comfortable with.”

“That isn't a definition.”

“Foundation, eyes, lips, whatever you want.”

Steve considered it.

“Temporary?”

Maddie whispered, “All makeup is temporary.”

Steve turned.

“Maddie.”

“Sorry.”

“I'm establishing terms.”

“Sorry.”

Steve looked back at Liz.

“I reserve veto authority.”

“Obviously.”

“On everything.”

“Yes.”

“And vetoes require no explanation.”

Liz's expression softened.

“Absolutely.”

Steve nodded.

“Good.”

She picked up her fork.

“Then yes.”

Silence.

Steve looked up.

“What?”

Maddie's eyes were enormous.

“YES?”

“To makeup.”

Maddie made a noise like a teakettle.

Steve pointed at her.

“Control yourself.”

“I'M TRYING.”

Nat leaned forward.

“Can we discuss nails?”

“No.”

“Why?”

Steve looked at her hands.

“Because I have perfectly functional nails.”

“So did the Titanic have perfectly functional deck chairs,” Chloe said.

Steve stared.

“That analogy doesn't make any fucking sense.”

“I know.”

“Then why did you say it?”

“It felt right.”

Steve shook her head.

“No acrylics.”

“Okay,” Nat said.

Steve blinked.

That had been easy.

Suspiciously easy.

“What?”

“Nothing.”

“No, you did something.”

“I accepted your answer.”

“You accepted it weirdly.”

“How does someone accept something weirdly?”

“Like that.”

Nat looked around the table.

“Can someone translate?”

Chloe nodded.

“She wants us to negotiate.”

Steve's jaw dropped.

“I do NOT.”

Chloe smiled.

“Then nails are settled.”

Steve looked at her own nails.

Then at Nat's.

Nat's were gorgeous.

Long.

Glossy.

Elegant.

Steve looked away.

“Maybe polish.”

Seven women froze.

Steve sighed.

“Oh, for fuck's sake.”

Maddie's hand shot into the air.

“No.”

It lowered.

“Temporary polish,” Steve said.

Nat nodded solemnly.

“Temporary polish.”

“And not pink.”

Britt frowned.

“Why not pink?”

“Because I said so.”

“Valid.”

Steve hadn't expected that.

Britt shrugged.

“Your nails.”

Steve smiled slightly.

“Thank you.”

Chloe leaned forward.

“May counsel propose red?”

Steve looked at her.

“Counsel may propose whatever counsel likes.”

“Red.”

Steve imagined it.

“Maybe.”

Chloe sat back.

“Excellent.”

Nat whispered, “She's weakening.”

Steve pointed at her without looking.

“I heard that.”

---

The negotiations deteriorated rapidly.

“Earrings,” Emily said.

“My ears aren't pierced.”

Seven women looked at Steve.

Steve looked around.

“No.”

Nobody spoke.

“No.”

Still nothing.

“You're all thinking.”

“We're allowed to think,” Tori said.

“Not collectively.”

“How would we stop?”

“I don't know. Think individually.”

Chloe nodded gravely.

“A foundational principle of democracy.”

Steve ignored her.

“No piercing.”

“Clip-ons?” Emily asked.

Steve considered.

“Permitted.”

Nat picked up her phone.

Steve pointed.

“Do not purchase anything at the table.”

Nat slowly put it down.

“Authoritarian.”

“My ears, my dictatorship.”

Chloe raised her glass.

“To bodily autonomy.”

Steve clinked hers against it.

“Finally, sensible jurisprudence.”

“Stockings?” Britt asked.

Steve paused.

“Why?”

Britt gestured toward Steve's legs.

“To make those even more unfair.”

Steve blushed.

“That's not an argument.”

“It's my entire argument.”

“Rejected.”

“Tights?”

“Maybe.”

“Thigh-highs?”

Steve nearly inhaled her wine.

“BRITT.”

“What?”

“No.”

“Okay.”

Maddie's hand went up again.

Steve sighed.

“Yes, Maddie.”

“Bras?”

Steve stared at her.

Maddie stared back.

Steve looked down at her flat chest.

Then back at Maddie.

“What exactly are we supporting?”

Chloe murmured, “The arts.”

Steve laughed despite herself.

“No bra.”

Maddie nodded.

Then Steve hesitated.

“But…”

Every woman became still.

Steve hated them.

“Some dresses need…”

She gestured.

“Shape.”

Nobody helped.

“You know what I mean.”

Liz rescued her.

“Structure?”

“Yes.”

“Padding?”

Steve's face warmed.

“Hypothetically.”

Britt made a strangled sound.

Steve pointed.

“Do not.”

Britt covered her mouth.

Liz remained calm.

“Would you like to try something sometime?”

Steve thought about the mirror.

The green dress.

The empty neckline.

The strange little thought she'd had.

Not tonight.

But—

“Maybe.”

Liz nodded.

“Then maybe.”

Nothing more.

Steve loved her a little for that.

That thought arrived fully formed.

Steve nearly dropped her fork.

*Loved?*

No.

No, absolutely not.

Smitten.

Infatuated.

Ridiculously attracted.

Those were manageable words.

Love was not entering evidence.

Chloe was watching her.

Steve narrowed her eyes.

“What?”

“Nothing.”

“You're doing the face.”

“What face?”

“The one where you've figured something out and are deciding whether annoying me with it is worth dying.”

Chloe smiled.

“I've decided against it.”

“Good.”

“For now.”

“Chloe.”

---

By dessert, there was an actual document.

Steve couldn't believe it.

Nat had titled it:

**THE STEVE ACCORDS**

Steve objected.

Nobody cared.

Current provisions included:

1. Steve retained absolute veto authority.
2. Vetoes required no justification.
3. Clothes were negotiable.
4. Dresses were explicitly negotiable.
5. Shoes were negotiable, although Britt had been prohibited from invoking “pavement transfer doctrine.”
6. Makeup was permitted.
7. Temporary nail polish was permitted.
8. Acrylic nails were currently outside jurisdiction.
9. Hair remained outside jurisdiction.
10. Ear piercing remained outside jurisdiction.
11. Clip-on earrings were permitted.
12. Padding was classified as “hypothetical research.”
13. Nobody was permitted to purchase Steve underwear without explicit authorization.

That final provision had required fifteen minutes.

Steve still wasn't sure how they'd gotten there.

“This is absurd,” she said.

Nat looked up from her phone.

“You requested clear terms.”

“I did not request the Geneva Convention of crossdressing.”

Seven women went quiet.

Steve heard her own word.

Crossdressing.

Something about it felt—

Wrong.

Not offensive.

Just inaccurate.

She looked down at the green dress.

Chloe spoke carefully.

“You okay?”

Steve nodded.

“Yeah.”

“You made a face.”

“I know.”

Nobody pushed.

Steve appreciated that too.

She picked up her wine.

“I reserve the right to amend terminology.”

Nat immediately typed.

“Added.”

Steve smiled.

“Thank you.”

Maddie raised her hand.

Steve laughed.

“Yes?”

“Can we add hugs?”

Steve blinked.

“Hugs?”

“To the agreement.”

“You've been hugging me all night.”

“I know. I want them protected.”

Steve's heart cracked cleanly in half.

“Oh.”

Maddie suddenly looked worried.

“Was that stupid?”

“No.”

Steve stood.

Walked around the table.

Maddie looked up at her.

Steve opened her arms.

Maddie launched herself into them.

Steve hugged her tightly.

“Unlimited hugs,” Steve murmured.

Maddie squeezed harder.

“Really?”

“Really.”

Someone sniffled.

Steve looked over Maddie's shoulder.

“Is somebody fucking crying?”

“No,” Britt said.

“Yes,” Nat said.

“Shut up.”

Steve laughed.

Then Emily joined the hug.

Then Britt.

Nat.

Tori.

Steve found herself surrounded again.

This time she didn't protest.

She looked over the pile toward Chloe.

Chloe remained seated.

Steve raised an eyebrow.

“You too, asshole.”

Chloe looked genuinely surprised.

Then she stood.

Joined.

Steve found Liz still sitting.

Their eyes met.

Steve didn't say anything.

She simply held out one hand.

Liz came.

And suddenly Steve was buried in them.

Warmth.

Perfume.

Laughter.

Someone's hair in her mouth.

“Whose fucking hair is this?”

“Probably mine,” Maddie said.

“Get it out of my mouth.”

Nobody moved.

Steve laughed.

She should feel trapped.

Instead she had never felt less trapped in her life.

Because she knew something now.

If she said stop, they'd stop.

If she said no, they'd hear it.

If she needed the door, they'd open it themselves.

And because Steve knew she could leave—

She could finally understand how badly she wanted to stay.

Eventually the pile separated.

Steve wiped suspicious moisture from one eye.

“All right.”

She returned to her seat.

“Add unlimited hugs.”

Nat typed.

“Done.”

Steve took a drink.

Chloe glanced at the document.

“One unresolved matter.”

Steve groaned.

“What?”

“Tomorrow.”

Steve frowned.

“What about tomorrow?”

Seven women looked at one another.

“Oh, fuck.”

Liz smiled.

“Brunch?”

Steve relaxed.

“That's it?”

“And maybe shopping.”

Steve stopped.

“No.”

“Okay,” Liz said.

Steve narrowed her eyes.

“What kind of shopping?”

Chloe smiled into her wine.

Nobody answered.

Steve looked around the table.

“What kind of shopping?”

Britt's expression became profoundly innocent.

“Hypothetical shopping.”

Steve closed her eyes.

“Goddammit.”

Maddie raised her hand.

Steve didn't open her eyes.

“Yes, Maddie?”

“Can we pick you up at ten?”

Steve remained silent.

She knew exactly what was happening.

She could say no.

Every woman at the table would accept it.

They'd proven that.

Which meant Steve had nobody to blame for what came out of her mouth next.

“Ten-thirty.”

Maddie squealed.

Steve opened her eyes.

“But I have conditions.”

Chloe lifted her glass.

“Of course you do.”

Steve pointed around the table.

“No buying anything without my approval.”

Seven women nodded.

“No salon.”

Nods.

“No hair.”

Nods.

“No piercings.”

Nods.

“No acrylics.”

Nat sighed dramatically.

“Natalie.”

“Fine.”

“And absolutely nobody says the words ‘settled science’ tomorrow.”

Chloe frowned.

“That's viewpoint discrimination.”

“It's my treaty.”

“Unconstitutional.”

“You invented the constitution!”

“Doesn't mean you can violate it.”

Steve stared at her.

Chloe stared back.

Steve's mouth twitched.

“Fine. You may say ‘settled science’ once.”

“Per person?”

“Total.”

“Per hour?”

“TOTAL.”

Chloe considered.

“Counteroffer. Three.”

“Two.”

“Done.”

Steve blinked.

“Wait.”

Chloe extended her hand across the table.

Steve stared at it.

“You just negotiated me into two.”

“You offered two.”

“You started at three!”

“Yes.”

“You would have accepted one!”

Chloe smiled.

Steve slowly reached across the table and shook her hand.

“I hate you.”

“No you don't.”

Steve squeezed her hand.

No.

She really, really didn't.

“Two,” Steve said.

“Two.”

Maddie raised her hand.

Steve sighed happily.

“Yes, Maddie?”

“Does tonight count?”

Steve looked around at the women she had somehow acquired in seven days.

At Liz.

At Chloe.

At all of them.

Her women.

No.

That was premature.

Absolutely premature.

Steve smiled.

“No.”

Maddie grinned.

“Good.”

She stood and lifted her glass.

“To settled science.”

Seven glasses rose.

Steve stared at them.

“You used one already?!”

The table dissolved into laughter.

Steve tried not to join them.

She failed completely.
