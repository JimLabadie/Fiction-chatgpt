# The Neon Sign

Status: ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
Title: **The Neon Sign** — author approved 2026-09-19.

A St. Claire story about Steve, Brittney, and the seven-woman lesbian polycule whose increasingly affectionate involvement in Steve's life collides with changes in presentation, identity, friendship, attraction, and belonging that Steve has not yet fully understood.

This series is distinct from **A Life in Plain Sight** and from the separate Jim/Claire story.
