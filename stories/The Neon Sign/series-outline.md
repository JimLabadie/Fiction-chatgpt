# The Neon Sign — Series Outline

Status: DEVELOPMENT

## Established trajectory

2. Feminine experiences/presentation accumulate in ways Jim can rationalize individually.
3. She protects his agency and never announces an identity for him.
4. She also enjoys his increasingly heroic ability to miss the obvious.
5. The evidence becomes a figurative giant blinking neon sign.
6. Current developed beat: Jim looks down at his feet and says, “Ick. I think I need shoes, too.”
7. Recognition comes later and should feel loving, earned, and emotionally sincere rather than imposed.
