# The Neon Sign — World and Setting

Status: ACTIVE

## Primary world

The story is set in **St. Claire** and uses the reusable St. Claire shared-world module as controlling authority for district lore, mechanics, businesses, places, population, households, staffing, schedules, and geography.

St. Claire is not generic queer scenery. Before a scene uses a coffee shop, salon, pharmacy, diner, boutique, bar, or other establishment, resolve the actual canonical place and the people plausibly present at that date/time from the live St. Claire records.

Do not invent the establishment names if St. Claire already contains them.

## Continuity rule

No teleportation. Track location, travel, wardrobe, possessions, physical positions, and the actual environment. Do not create timeless/featureless spaces or silently change clothing/objects between beats.

## Shared cultural frame

St. Claire's lesbian identity, sapphic culture, presentation spectrum, and relationship norms are active context. The seven women should have distinct femme/sapphic style rather than being rendered as interchangeable “women in feminine clothes.”
