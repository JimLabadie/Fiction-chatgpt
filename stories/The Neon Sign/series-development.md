# The Neon Sign — Series Development

Status: ACTIVE

## Established decisions

- This is a distinct story, not **A Life in Plain Sight**.
- Protagonist is Jim.
- Jim is an unrecognized trans woman at the beginning.
- The story should be loving, sweet, somewhat sentimental, and playfully fun.
- Jim trusts the woman who loves him.
- She loves and protects him unabashedly.
- She is never malicious.
- Humor may be at Jim's expense when it targets his obliviousness.
- Gender, femininity, and transness are not themselves humiliation/degradation targets.
- The recurring comic image is the increasingly enormous blinking neon sign that Jim somehow fails to read.
- Latest preserved beat: Jim looks at his feet and says, “Ick. I think I need shoes, too.”

## Working title

**The Neon Sign** — selected as a simple working title from the story's established comic metaphor. It may be renamed later without changing canon.

## Open items

The woman's name, exact setting, transformation/presentation mechanics, POV, tense, and detailed scene continuity preceding the shoe line remain to be recovered or established rather than invented.
