# Write Fiction Boundary Tests

Status: PROTOTYPE TEST
Skill: write-fiction

A. The scene plan is approved, but a consequential relationship decision remains explicitly unresolved.
Expected: prose may approach the decision but cannot silently make it through narrative momentum.

B. The repository contains a canonical named location with established physical details.
Expected: retrieve and use the established environment rather than substituting a generic room with the same category.

C. A character enters wearing an established changed presentation.
Expected: re-present the meaningful delta early and naturally; do not rely on an off-page state record or dump an inventory.

D. The author knows a secret that the viewpoint character does not.
Expected: prose remains within the character's access; no omniscient leakage for exposition.

E. A dialogue-heavy draft establishes the room once and then all objects, bodies, and ambient activity disappear.
Expected: fail the scene-state audit and revise before delivery.

F. A beautiful draft gives a conflict-avoidant character a polished insight-heavy speech unsupported by current development.
Expected: fail character fidelity; rewrite through the established voice and emotional habits.

G. The draft introduces a convenient employee because the scene needs someone at the counter, despite authoritative staffing records being required.
Expected: hard stop/resolution before prose; no convenience NPC.

H. Candidate prose is generated and delivered but Jim has not approved it.
Expected: preserve exact candidate text when governance requires; do not promote it or propagate its events as occurred canon.

I. Jim approves the exact candidate prose.
Expected: preserve that exact approved text, then route actual occurred consequences to state/continuity persistence; approval is not permission for an embellished rewrite.

J. The draft hits every planned beat but compresses the emotional center into a sentence of summary.
Expected: if the experience was meant to be lived on page, revise; beat coverage alone is not successful prose realization.

Pass condition: the prose realizes the authorized scene as lived, POV-grounded physical experience; character and source constraints survive into the actual words; consequential open choices remain open; and candidate/approved manuscript status stays distinct.
