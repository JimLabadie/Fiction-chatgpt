# Inhabit

Status: PROTOTYPE — NON-GOVERNING
Purpose: Determine how a specific character performs, learns, or struggles with an unfamiliar role, routine, body, presentation, occupation, identity context, or competency.

## Use when
A character must live or perform circumstances for which factual routines/competencies exist but their personal mastery is not established.

## Procedure
1. Retrieve the character's established knowledge, skills, habits, history, physical state, and elapsed exposure time.
2. Retrieve the relevant factual/reference knowledge for the task. Treat descriptive norms as possibility space, not character destiny.
3. Separate underlying factual knowledge from what this character personally knows.
4. Determine competence source: pre-existing skill, transferred capability, coaching, observation, practice, improvisation, or unresolved.
5. Resolve realistic errors and learning only from the gap between task demands and established competence; do not manufacture incompetence for comedy or stereotype.
6. Respect physical/adaptation constraints and elapsed time.
7. Preserve character voice, agency, preferences, and established personality while circumstances change.
8. If the scene requires consequential knowledge/skill not established or recoverable, mark the gap or seek author resolution rather than silently granting mastery.
9. Hand persistent learning/adaptation milestones to state tracking.

## Guardrails
- Context knowledge does not automatically belong to the character.
- Demographic membership does not confer a fixed routine, taste, hobby, voice, or competence.
- Transformation does not itself grant lived experience unless the mechanism explicitly transfers it.
