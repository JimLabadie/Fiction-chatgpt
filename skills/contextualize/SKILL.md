# Contextualize

Status: PROTOTYPE — NON-GOVERNING
Purpose: Apply relevant cultural, generational, regional, occupational, class, community, or social context without replacing individual canon with a stereotype.

## Use when
Context materially affects interpretation, behavior, language, expectations, institutions, presentation, relationships, or scene texture.

## Procedure
1. Retrieve the individual/entity canon first.
2. Identify which contextual axes are actually implicated by the task.
3. Retrieve the maintained knowledge modules for those axes.
4. Distinguish established individual facts from descriptive group tendencies, historical facts, contested claims, and time-sensitive real-world information.
5. Apply only context that materially explains or constrains the situation.
6. Prefer intersection-aware interpretation when multiple axes interact; do not mechanically stack stereotypes.
7. Never override explicit individual canon with a demographic default.
8. When precision depends on current real-world facts, route to verification/research rather than treating dated reference material as timeless.
9. If context offers several plausible behaviors and character canon does not resolve among them, leave the choice open or hand it to character simulation/author decision.

## Guardrails
Context supplies possibility and meaning, not destiny.
