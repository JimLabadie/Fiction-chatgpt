# Techne — Company Profile (Draft)

*Reusable reference, matching the structure of `threadbourne-robotics.md` and `evergreen-biofoundry.md`. The sixth company under the same parent holding structure — but distinct from every company before it in one key way: Techne was born from a **conscious** decision, after the anchor's realization, not an unconscious rant discovered in hindsight.*

## Origin

Techne exists because Metis Werks exists. The moment the anchor committed to building a real manufacturing and job-training facility (see the Metis Werks facility document), she ran into an immediate, obvious gap: wanting better equipment than what the industry currently offers, and having no source for it. Unlike every other company in this portfolio — which trace back to old rants she didn't understand the meaning of at the time — Techne is a **deliberate, conscious business decision**, made in full awareness of who she is and what she's building. It's the first company in her portfolio she built on purpose, as herself, rather than one she discovers she already built by accident.

## Corporate Mandate

Design, prototype, and commercialize next-generation manufacturing equipment across the full range of categories Metis Werks operates in — textile machinery, apparel automation, footwear tooling, beauty/fragrance manufacturing equipment, and salon/spa equipment. Equipment is proven on Metis Werks' own production floors first, then licensed or sold commercially across the wider manufacturing industry.

## Recruitment Pattern

Unlike Metis Werks' broader workforce (openly, publicly hired), Techne's core leadership team follows the same anonymous pattern as Cassandra Labs — identified by Athena based on published engineering work, funded and contacted under the "J." identity, none of them having ever met the anchor or knowing who she actually is. This is a deliberate contrast: even though Techne itself is a conscious, named decision, the anchor's instinct to find brilliant people quietly and protect them from ever feeling like they're being watched or managed personally is the same instinct running underneath everything else in this portfolio.
