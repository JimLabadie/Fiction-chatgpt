*Continuity reference for this story. Update in place as the story progresses — this document should always reflect current canon, not a changelog. ## Premise
A small, shy man named Jim walks into Blush — a lesbian club — alone, for reasons unrelated to his gender or sexuality (a friend, Marcus, was supposed to come with him and bailed). Jim has no conscious awareness that he's trans; he's lived his whole life as an unremarkable, self-effacing guy. Unbeknownst to him, everyone around him has always perceived him as an extraordinarily beautiful woman in her twenties — a perception gap he's never had reason to notice, because he's spent his life avoiding attention rather than seeking it out. Tonight, in a room full of women primed to actually *see* him, that gap becomes impossible to ignore.

Separately and secretly, Jim is worth approximately $450 billion. No one in his life knows this, including — as of the story's current point — the five women he's just met.

**This is fundamentally a change/transition story.** The romance and the polycule are the support structure the story is built on top of, not the throughline itself.

## The Pattern (Open Plot Thread)
Over the course of one night, Jim has involuntarily used at least five pieces of community-specific language/concepts he has no prior exposure to, each one surprising him as much as anyone else:
1. "Do you listen to girl in red?" — used correctly, unprompted, despite Jim having no familiarity with the artist or the phrase's meaning.
2. "Egg" — used before he knew the community definition; the group explains the egg/hatch/chick wordplay.
3. Recognizing "U-Haul" as a concept/joke, unprompted.
4. Knowing what a strap-on is/how it might feel, despite (per his own account) having no reason to know.
5. "Big girl panties" / "having the ovaries to do it" — idiomatic phrases that land with unintended double meanings he notices himself.

This pattern is being treated narratively as evidence that something true about Jim already exists beneath his conscious awareness — the "shell," per the egg metaphor — surfacing in small, involuntary ways before any deliberate self-discovery has occurred. **Mechanically, this has not yet been run through Gate 1 of the change-worldbuilding-toolkit** (no on-page choice has triggered tracked mechanics yet) — it is currently pre-toolkit narrative texture, not a tracked mechanism.
