## A1. The Jamestown District (jamestown-district.md)

Jamestown is the shared commercial/industrial district where the empire's physical technology-subsidiary buildings, its manufacturing-equipment company, and its worker-benefits infrastructure all sit together on one street — roughly two hours from the estate by helicopter. All six company buildings plus two shared district buildings occupy the same cul-de-sac street.

**Why one district:** Every other holding in the portfolio is either fully virtual (equity stakes, licensing deals) or lives at the estate itself. NAS, OAM, Threadbourne Robotics, and Evergreen Biofoundry were never assigned a physical home of their own prior to this. Housing them together, on the same street as Metis Werks and Techne, keeps the empire's "it was always her, building toward something she couldn't yet name" thesis physically coherent.

**Total combined Jamestown workforce (all six companies, once Metis Werks and Techne close): ~24,300.**

## A2. Metis Werks (metis-werks.md)

*The empire's fifth technology/manufacturing subsidiary — born from a conscious decision, after the anchor's realization, launched openly and publicly as her own named act.*

**Origin rant:**
> "I watched three episodes of Project Runway tonight and could not stop thinking about the kid who got eliminated in episode two. Genuinely talented. Nowhere to go with it. ## B1. Metis Werks — Equipment Descriptions (Plain-Language Version)

*A clean rewrite of the canon equipment lists into plain-language functional descriptions — same machines as Part A's floor tables, described rather than just named. Genuinely useful as reference prose, not new information.*

**L2 — Fiber Processing & Yarn Manufacturing:** Bale Opening Lines open compressed fiber bales and loosen fibers for processing. Blending Feeders combine different fiber types or batches for uniform mix. Carding Machines align and clean fibers into a continuous soft strand (sliver). Draw Frames stretch and merge slivers for consistency. Roving Frames convert sliver into lightly twisted roving. Ring Spinning Frames twist roving into strong, fine yarn. Open-End Spinning Units produce yarn via rotor system, faster and suited for coarser yarns. Cone Winding Machines wind finished yarn onto cones. But it also contains a **complete named personnel roster** — real names for every department head, shift supervisor, and specialist (Dr. Rajan Pillai, Ms. Anika Mehrotra, Mr. Marcus T. Ellison as Director of Operations, Ms. Diane R. Vasquez as COO, and hundreds more). This was explicitly superseded by an earlier decision in this project: drop specific named individuals for roles that were only ever document-formatting placeholders, keep role titles only. Canon's `metis-werks.md` reflects that decision (**"Document Owner | Director of Operations (role title only — no named individual assigned)"**). The named roster should be treated as discarded, not a source to draw names from later.

The same document's Section 5 (Cross-Facility Systems & Workflows) and Section 6 (Facility Metrics) contain real operational detail — ERP/MES platforms (SAP S/4HANA, Siemens Opcenter MES, Dassault ENOVIA PLM), the six-tier badge access system, energy/utilities figures (~48 MW peak load, 40% renewable target by 2028) — **this part matches and was already folded into canon's "Internal Systems" section.** No conflict there.

## C2. The "Megafacility Architectural Blueprint — Fashion, Beauty & Textile Complex"

A separate, much larger draft (6,090 lines) describing an entirely different facility concept: a **single-story, horizontal campus** with ten wings (W-01 through W-10), each 1,800–7,800 sqm, totaling roughly 477,000 sq ft — versus canon's **22-floor vertical tower at 4.2 million sq ft.** These are two incompatible buildings, not two views of the same one. This document was not used in building current canon and directly conflicts with it in scale, structure, and layout.

I have this fully extracted and can produce it as its own reference document if you want to see it — but I'd flag it as an alternate/discarded concept rather than merge it with anything above unless you specifically want to reconcile or replace canon with it.
