# The Jamestown District

*Reusable reference, matching the structure of `metis-werks.md` and `techne.md`. Jamestown is the shared commercial/industrial district where the empire's physical technology-subsidiary buildings, its manufacturing-equipment company, and its worker-benefits infrastructure all sit together on one street — roughly two hours from the estate by helicopter, the same distance already established for Metis Werks and Techne. All six company buildings plus the two shared district buildings below occupy the same cul-de-sac street, giving the whole technology and manufacturing side of the empire a single, real, physical home rather than six scattered, disconnected addresses.*

## Why One District

Every other holding in this portfolio is either fully virtual (equity stakes, licensing deals) or lives at the estate itself. NAS, OAM, Threadbourne Robotics, and Evergreen Biofoundry — the four rant-derived technology subsidiaries — were never assigned a physical home of their own prior to this file. Housing them together, on the same street as Metis Werks and Techne, in the same district Metis Werks already brought back from total economic collapse, keeps the empire's "it was always her, building toward something she couldn't yet name" thesis physically coherent: one place, one skyline, one workforce the anchor's memo actually reaches all at once.

**Total combined Jamestown workforce (all six companies, once Metis Werks and Techne close): ~24,300.**

## The Six Company Buildings

### The Meridian Building (NAS)
Neo-Anatomical Systems Corp's headquarters and clinical installation facility. Deliberately the smallest and roomiest-per-capita building on the street — NAS's client base is narrow (private, ultra-high-net-worth individuals plus a limited clinical licensing program), so this building is built for a small number of extremely expensive installations, not volume production.
- **9 floors + 1 sublevel, ~950,000 sq ft, ~650 employees**
- B1: mechanical, secure medication/materials storage
- L1–2: reception, security, administration
- L3–5: clinical wing — Integration Suite installation bays
- L6–7: R&D — presentation modeling, biometric calibration
- L8: Selective Partner Licensing Division
- L9: executive offices

### The Aura Tower (OAM)
Omnitech Aesthetic & Body Modification Group's headquarters and manufacturing complex, producing Aura-Matrix pods and the full OAM product catalog for licensing to salons and spas industry-wide.
- **18 floors + 2 sublevels, ~2,600,000 sq ft, ~2,900 employees**
- Sublevels: raw materials, mechanical core
- L1–3: Aura-Matrix V-10 pod assembly
- L4–6: Synchronized Salon & Laser-Guided Resurfacing Array manufacturing
- L7–8: Multi-Point Piercing Curation System manufacturing
- L9–10: Multi-Needle Micro-Pigmentation Array (tattoo) manufacturing
- L11: Olfactory Suite compounding & bottling
- L12: ChromaBlend Dispensary formulation labs
- L13: Preview Array hardware/software floor
- L14–15: QA, calibration bay
- L16: packaging/logistics
- L17–18: executive, licensing/client relations

### The Threadbourne Works (Threadbourne Robotics)
The largest of the four subsidiary buildings — full industrial-scale manufacturing across five separate product lines, sized closest to Techne's own footprint.
- **20 floors + 2 sublevels, ~3,400,000 sq ft, ~4,100 employees**
- Sublevels: receiving, mechanical/utilities
- L1–2: lobby, admin, AWS mannequin/robot assembly
- L3–5: Automated Apparel Manufacturing Lines
- L6–7: Discreet Asset Tag Platform (electronics, embedding integration)
- L8–9: Adaptive Textile Research Division ("Living Fabric") labs
- L10–11: Self-Service Tailoring Kiosk production
- L12–14: Threadbourne Home (consumer line) assembly
- L15–17: QA, packaging, outbound logistics
- L18–20: executive, R&D, client training

### The Evergreen Foundry (Evergreen Biofoundry)
Biotech and cleanroom facility rather than heavy manufacturing — genuinely different in kind from the other three, and it reads that way physically.
- **11 floors + 1 sublevel, ~1,300,000 sq ft, ~1,500 employees**
- B1: utilities, biohazard-safe waste handling
- L1–2: lobby, admin
- L3–5: Perennial Restoration Program — cell culture labs, biologic production
- L6–8: Genesis Gem Foundry — lab-grown gem/metal growth chambers, cutting/finishing
- L9: Traceroot Materials Initiative — sourcing verification, certification (office/data work)
- L10–11: QA, executive

### Techne
See `techne.md` for full detail — included here only for district completeness. **23 floors + 2 sublevels, ~3,200,000 sq ft, ~3,900 employees.**

### Metis Werks
See `metis-werks.md` for full detail — included here only for district completeness. **~4,200,000 sq ft, ~8,400 employees.** Note: Metis Werks' own L21 "Training Academy & Employee Welfare" floor is a smaller in-house convenience option (grab-and-go, quick-access wellness) for workers who don't want to leave the megafacility during a shift — the Whitfield Commons (below) is the district's real flagship grocery/fitness/benefits facility, serving all six companies rather than Metis Werks alone.

## Parking

Each company building has its own dedicated structure, sized at roughly 0.6 spaces per employee (accounting for shift work, carpooling, and logistics staff who don't personally drive in). All structures include EV charging on their top two levels and a small reserved visitor/executive tier near ground level. Structures sit outside each building's own interior sq ft accounting, the same convention used at the estate.

| Company | Workforce | Structure | Levels | Capacity | Footprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NAS | ~650 | Meridian Garage | 3 | ~400 spaces | ~140,000 sq ft |
| OAM | ~2,900 | Aura Garage | 6 | ~1,750 spaces | ~610,000 sq ft |
| Threadbourne Robotics | ~4,100 | Threadbourne Garage | 7 | ~2,500 spaces | ~875,000 sq ft |
| Evergreen Biofoundry | ~1,500 | Evergreen Garage | 4 | ~900 spaces | ~315,000 sq ft |
| Techne | ~3,900 | Techne Garage | 7 | ~2,350 spaces | ~825,000 sq ft |

Pedestrian/shuttle connections link adjacent garages along the street (Threadbourne–Techne, OAM–Evergreen) so employees and inter-company logistics don't require re-parking building to building.

## The Whitfield Commons

*A former abandoned mixed-use commercial building at the end of the cul-de-sac — the same district-wide economic collapse that shut down Metis Werks took this building with it. Purchased in the same debt-financed deal as Metis Werks and Techne; not a separate acquisition Athena has to justify on its own. Employee-facing, full stop — no family/guest function here.* Serves all ~24,300 Jamestown employees once Metis Werks and Techne close, fulfilling the "genuine employee grocery store and fitness center" promise made in `metis-werks.md`'s origin note, at full district scale rather than confined to one company.

**~850,000 sq ft, 9 floors + 1 sublevel.**

| Level | Function | Approx. Sq Ft |
|---|---|---|
| L8–9 | Reserve — unconverted, held for future benefits as the program develops | ~190,000 |
| L7 | Financial Wellness Office (credit union branch, benefits counseling) | ~50,000 |
| L6 | Childcare & Family Support Center | ~70,000 |
| L5 | On-site Clinic & Pharmacy | ~60,000 |
| L3–4 | Fitness Center (gym floor, pool, group fitness studios, locker rooms) | ~180,000 |
| L2 | Food Hall (multiple vendors) | ~90,000 |
| L1 | Employee Grocery Store (full-size, real prices subsidized per the company-wide benefits memo) | ~120,000 |
| B1 | Loading dock, cold storage, mechanical | ~90,000 |

The reserve floors are left deliberately open, the same way the estate holds its own reserve square footage — the empire's broader benefits program is still developing, and this building has room to grow into it rather than being filled in for the sake of a tidy total.

## The Whitfield Commerce Center

*A separate, purpose-built, relatively small building — new construction, not converted from anything, the same way the estate treats its own Staff Residence and Cassandra Labs building. Family and guest use only; this is the household's own ground-operations hub in Jamestown, not an employee facility.*

**~70,000 sq ft, 3 floors + 1 sublevel + roof heliport.**

| Level | Function | Approx. Sq Ft |
|---|---|---|
| Roof | **Heliport — 14 pads** (anchor + full partner-suite capacity, matching the estate's own 13-partner Partner Dressing Room count, built with the same room-to-grow logic) | — |
| L3 | Private conference room, security office | ~15,000 |
| L2 | The Whitfield Lounge — arrival/waiting lounge for the anchor and partners between helicopter and ground transport | ~20,000 |
| L1 | Reception, direct elevator access to roof | ~10,000 |
| B1 | Motor Pool Garage — SUV fleet + security vehicles | ~25,000 |

This building exists purely to get the household from a helicopter to a car to whichever company building they're visiting, in minutes, privately — genuinely small compared to everything else in the district on purpose.

## Employee Housing Lot

A separate, currently empty parcel adjacent to the cul-de-sac, explicitly reserved and undeveloped — land banked for a future employee housing project rather than committed to one yet, the same way the estate holds its own reserve interior square footage. Acreage and any development plan remain an open story question.

## Where This Fits

- **Physical location:** One street, ending in a cul-de-sac, in the same district as Metis Werks — roughly two hours from the estate by helicopter. The six company buildings line the street; the Whitfield Commons sits at the end of the cul-de-sac (the former abandoned mixed-use building); the Whitfield Commerce Center is a separate small new-construction building on the same street, family/guest use only.
- **Acquisition timeline:** NAS, OAM, Threadbourne Robotics, and Evergreen Biofoundry's buildings represent physical infrastructure for already-owned companies (the companies themselves launched commercially in "Project Artemis," per that story's chronology — a story should treat these buildings as already-existing infrastructure those companies operate from, not a new acquisition beat). The Whitfield Commons is acquired in the same debt-financed transaction as Metis Werks and Techne, and is not final until that acquisition closes. The Whitfield Commerce Center is new construction, commissioned whenever a story is ready to place it (it does not depend on any acquisition closing).
- **Holdings map:** No new companies are added here — this file is physical infrastructure only, for companies and holdings already established in `holdings-map.md`, `presentation-technology.md`, `threadbourne-robotics.md`, `evergreen-biofoundry.md`, `metis-werks.md`, and `techne.md`.
