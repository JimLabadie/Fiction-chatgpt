# Techne — Floor 23 Facility Profile (Draft)

**Wing Designation:** Floor 23 — Techne Equipment Innovation Division
**Mission Statement:** To design, prototype, and validate next-generation manufacturing equipment across every production category housed in the floors below — textile machinery, apparel automation, footwear tooling, beauty/fragrance manufacturing equipment, and salon/spa equipment — feeding proven prototypes directly into Metis Werks' own production lines, with commercial licensing potential across the wider manufacturing industry.

**Approx. Sq Ft:** ~180,000 (the new top floor, sized comparably to the facility's larger specialty floors)

**Access Level:** RESTRICTED — Techne Personnel Only, plus Executive Access

## Zones Housed

### 1. Design & Engineering Studio
- CAD/CAM workstations (×24): full mechanical design and simulation suite
- PLM integration terminal bank (×8): syncing prototype specs directly into the facility-wide PLM system already governing production floors below
- Rapid-review presentation room, for cross-team design reviews

### 2. Heavy Prototyping Workshop
- CNC machining centers (×6): 5-axis mills for precision metal prototype components
- CNC lathes (×4)
- Industrial welding bays (×8): MIG/TIG stations for structural prototype fabrication
- Sheet metal fabrication line (×1): press brake, shear, and forming equipment
- Overhead gantry crane (×2, 5-ton capacity): for moving heavy prototype equipment sections

### 3. Textile Machinery Test Bay
- Prototype loom test rigs (×3): full-scale weaving mechanism testbeds
- Prototype knitting machine test rigs (×3)
- Dyeing/finishing process simulation rig (×1): small-batch scale, for testing new finishing equipment concepts before full-floor deployment
- Direct data link to Level 4 (Dyeing, Printing & Finishing) for real-world validation runs

### 4. Automation & Robotics Lab
- Robotic arm test cells (×8): articulated arms for prototyping cutting, sewing, and material-handling automation
- Machine vision development stations (×6): optical mapping and quality-inspection prototype systems
- Conveyor/AGV integration testbed: for prototyping new internal material-flow equipment compatible with the facility's existing conveyor and AGV infrastructure

### 5. Beauty & Fragrance Equipment Lab
- Small-batch mixing/blending equipment prototyping stations (×4)
- Filling-line prototype rig (×2): for testing new fragrance/beauty filling and capping equipment concepts
- Cleanroom-standard prototyping bay (×1): matching the GMP gowning protocol already used on Level 15, for equipment intended to eventually operate in that environment

### 6. Salon/Spa Equipment Lab
- Furniture and equipment fabrication bench: prototyping styling chairs, treatment beds, and related equipment
- Electronics integration bench: for equipment with embedded tech components (matching this world's broader pattern of automated salon technology)

### 7. Materials Science Bench
- Fiber and fabric stress-testing equipment
- Materials compatibility testing for new equipment components (heat tolerance, chemical exposure, wear testing)

## Key Systems Integration

- **Freight & Conveyor Access:** Floor 23 connects directly into the existing eight heavy-capacity freight elevator banks, allowing a validated prototype to move straight down to any of the 22 production floors for real-world testing without leaving the building's internal logistics network.
- **Heliport:** Positioned on the new roof level above Floor 23, replacing what was previously the building's top level — supports helicopter access for executive visits, consistent with the facility's distance from the estate.
- **PLM/ERP Integration:** Techne's design output feeds directly into the same ENOVIA-based PLM and SAP S/4HANA ERP systems already governing the rest of the facility, so a finished equipment design can be commissioned into production-floor deployment without a separate handoff process.
