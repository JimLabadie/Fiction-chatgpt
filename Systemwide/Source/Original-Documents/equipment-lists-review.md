## Part 2: Techne — Automated Material Handling Product Line (New, Detailed)

*Since you specifically want automated materials handling built out — this is a genuine gap in fashion/textile manufacturing (most of the equipment above still relies on manual trolleys, overhead conveyor pushes, and human pallet-building), and a real opportunity for Techne to build something with broad cross-industry licensing value, not just internal use.*

**Techne Material Flow Systems** — proposed as a real product line/division, alongside the five already established (Loom & Knit, CutSew Automation, Footwear Tooling, Formulation & Fill, Studio Systems):

- **Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs)** for internal fabric roll, cut-bundle, and finished-carton transport between floors and work zones — replacing the manual trolley/overhead-trolley movement currently used at Metis Werks (e.g., the Eton overhead trolley lines on L10/L11).
- **Automated Storage & Retrieval System (AS/RS)** for the Deep Archive/warehouse zones — high-density robotic shelving that retrieves a specific pallet or tote on demand, rather than manual forklift retrieval.
- **Robotic Palletizing Cells** — end-of-line robotic arms that build and wrap outbound pallets automatically at Logistics (L19), replacing manual pallet-building.
- **Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) for Heavy Freight** — floor-tracked or lidar-guided vehicles for moving raw material (fiber bales, leather rolls, steel stock at Techne's own building) between receiving and production floors.
- **Robotic Pick-and-Place Sortation** — for the kind of high-SKU-count kitting and multi-SKU pack-out work currently done by hand at Packaging (L17).
- **Conveyor Sortation Intelligence** — smart routing/diverting conveyor systems that read a package's destination and route it automatically, rather than fixed conveyor paths requiring manual intervention for exceptions.
