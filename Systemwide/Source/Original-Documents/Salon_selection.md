# OAM Selection Mechanism — Draft for Review

## The Core Fix, Applied to All Three Modules (Salon, Piercing, Tattoo)

Right now the spec reads as "the pod executes the complete catalog" — as if every placement, every treatment, happens to everyone, every time. The actual mechanism should be a **consultation and selection step**, preceding automated execution, using OAM's own Preview Array technology (the same tech behind the vanity mirrors) to show a wearer what a given combination will actually look like before committing. Two paths through that consultation, consistently across all three modules:

1. **Curated Packages** — pre-designed, named combinations at different intensity tiers, built by OAM's styling algorithms (or a human stylist like Amélie) to look cohesive together. Fast, low-decision-fatigue, good default.
2. **Full Custom Build** — a wearer selects individual items from the complete catalog one at a time, previewed live as they go, for anyone who wants full control.

Below is the piercing module worked out in full as the model; salon and tattoo would get the equivalent treatment once this shape is approved.

---

## Piercing Module — Curated Packages (Example Tier Structure)

**Ear placements only, for this draft** (facial/oral/torso packages would follow the same pattern once this is approved):

### "First Constellation" — Minimalist (3 placements)
Single lobe, helix, tragus. The entry point — visible, deliberate, not overwhelming. A good default for a first-ever piercing session.

### "Curated Ear Package" — Moderate (6 placements)
Double lobe, helix, forward helix, tragus, conch (outer), rook. Balanced across the ear rather than clustered — the kind of look that reads as intentionally styled rather than accumulated over time. This is the tier I'd suggest as OAM's actual signature/default recommendation, the one most wearers are steered toward first.

### "Full Constellation" — Maximalist (10 placements)
Triple lobe, helix, forward helix, double helix, tragus, anti-tragus, conch (inner and outer), rook, daith. Everything the ear can visibly hold without looking cluttered — the top of the standard curated range.

### Full Custom Build
Any combination from the complete 16-placement catalog (lobe tiers, helix variants, tragus/anti-tragus, conch inner/outer, rook, snug, daith, industrial/scaffold, orbital, auricle), previewed live via the Preview Array before the pod executes anything, one placement or all sixteen, wearer's full discretion.

**Jewelry selection** happens at the same consultation step, per placement — metal (14k/18k gold), stone (Akoya pearl, VS-diamond, other gemstone), and style, rather than being pre-assigned.
