# Metis Werks

*Reusable reference. The empire's fifth technology/manufacturing subsidiary — distinct from every company before it: born from a conscious decision, after the anchor's realization, and launched openly and publicly as her own named act, not discovered as an old unconscious pattern.*

## Origin

> "I watched three episodes of Project Runway tonight and could not stop thinking about the kid who got eliminated in episode two. Genuinely talented. Nowhere to go with it. There's no path from 'talented and broke' to 'actually employed doing this' unless you already know someone, already have money, already had a head start nobody tells you that you needed. Meanwhile every kid I talk to says the same thing — there are no good jobs, nothing that pays enough to build an actual life, nothing that teaches you a real skill and then actually uses it. Somewhere between all these unemployed talented kids and all these companies that can't find skilled workers, there's a gap nobody's bothered to close, and I don't understand why."

**Acquisition, not new construction:** Unlike a from-scratch build, Metis Werks began as an already-existing manufacturing megafacility, **abandoned and shut down for good** in a district that used to be the backbone of its whole local economy — not merely struggling, but already closed, its jobs already lost once. Athena identified it as a match for the anchor's rant and proposed acquiring and **reopening** it, framing the acquisition explicitly as bringing the district's jobs back rather than simply creating new ones elsewhere — hiring back the people this facility already let go, building the training academy and incubator mission on top of what already existed, and adding a genuine employee grocery store and fitness center as standing amenities, matching the same benefits standard already established at the estate. **Financing structure:** acquired via debt (leveraged against the anchor's existing net worth/empire), not a cash purchase and not outside equity investors — full 100% ownership retained, no diluted control, consistent with every other holding in this portfolio. **Benefits extend automatically:** every benefit from the anchor's company-wide memo (hardship fund, cost-of-living adjustment, etc.) applies to the facility's full workforce the moment the acquisition closes — no separate rollout, no waiting period.

## Corporate Mandate

A combined manufacturing operation, service bureau, training academy, and startup incubator, built to close the gap between talented, skilled people and the jobs that don't currently exist for them. Named for Metis — the Greek goddess of craft, skill, and deep practical wisdom, and, fittingly, Athena's own mother in myth.

## Hiring Pattern

The workforce skews femme lesbian or trans woman wherever the role practically allows it, and butch lesbian where the specific job's physical/safety demands make that the more practical presentation — heavy machinery floors, loading docks, and similarly demanding roles trending butch; design, QA, administrative, and lighter-manufacturing roles trending femme. Not a formal written policy — the same invisible pattern already running through every hiring decision in this empire, visible here at real industrial scale for the first time.

## Facility Identification

**Projected Daily Revenue (Post-Launch): ~$35 Million/day.** Metis Werks is the largest single operation in the entire portfolio by workforce and physical output — 22 floors of genuine industrial manufacturing across apparel, textiles, beauty, fragrance, and footwear, dwarfing any of the tech subsidiaries in raw production volume even before accounting for the training academy and incubator's longer-term value.

**Acquisition Cost: ~$76–77 Billion** (based on this world's established ~6x annual-revenue valuation pattern, consistent with every other company on file). Financed entirely through debt leveraged against the anchor's existing net worth/empire — not a cash purchase, which would have consumed nearly all of her liquid reserves (~15–20% of her ~$647.3B net worth, or roughly $97–130B), and not outside equity, which would have diluted her ownership. Full 100% ownership retained.

## Internal Systems (Retained from Original Specification)

- **Material Flow:** Eight heavy-capacity freight elevator banks (10,000 kg capacity), floor-level conveyor backbones, RFID-tagged pallet routing, pneumatic sample tube system (Level 18 QA Lab, under 4 minutes transit from any floor), AGV tracks on Levels 10, 11, 12, and 17.
- **ERP/MES:** Enterprise-wide ERP integrated with a manufacturing execution system, translating orders into floor-level work instructions and machine allocation across all manufacturing wings.
- **Quality Management:** ISO 9001:2015-based system, real-time defect tracking, automated CAPA workflows, digital Certificate of Conformity generation.
- **EHS:** Floor-specific chemical hazard zoning (PPE Zone 2 on L4, GMP gowning on L15, full ATEX Zone 1 on L16), floor-specific evacuation plans, dedicated Floor Emergency Wardens.
- **Energy:** ~48 MW peak load, dual-feed 11kV supply with emergency generator backup (72-hour runtime), rooftop solar (2.4 MW), battery storage (6 MW), 40% renewable target by 2028.
- **Security:** Six-tier badge access system, biometric dual-factor authentication on all restricted zones, 96-camera CCTV network.
