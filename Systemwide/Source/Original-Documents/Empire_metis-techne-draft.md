## Metis Werks — Company Profile

**Full name:** Metis Werks (named for the Greek goddess of craft, skill, and deep practical wisdom — fittingly, Athena's own mother in myth, a resonance worth using given Athena already exists as a character).

**Mission:** A combined manufacturing operation, service bureau, training academy, and startup incubator, built specifically to close the gap between talented, skilled people and the jobs that don't currently exist for them — launched as a direct, named act of economic repair, not another quiet unconscious pattern.

**Location:** A helicopter flight from the estate, sited deliberately in a struggling former industrial district — real jobs placed where they're actually needed, not where it would be most convenient for the empire.

**Footprint:** 450,000–650,000 sq ft ground floor (~10–15 acres), stacking vertically into a multi-million-sq-ft facility rather than expanding outward.

**8 Floors Total:**
1. Heavy Industrial (Textile) — tallest ceilings, 700–1,000 employees
2. Apparel Manufacturing — 500–800 employees
3. Footwear + Accessories — 350–550 employees
4. Beauty + Fragrance Manufacturing — 300–450 employees
5. Light Manufacturing (Home Textiles, Soap, Plush, Salon/Medical Spa/Barber Furniture) — 570–880 employees combined
6. Sustainability Annex + R&D Labs — 80–120 employees
7. Logistics Spine + Loading + Vertical Conveyors — 200–300 employees
8. **The Techne Incubator** (new — see below), plus Training Academy and general incubator space — 270–450 employees

Additional cross-cutting roles: Trims/Notions, Thread/Yarn, Technical Textiles, Training Academy, and Administrative Bureau bring the facility's total to **2,800–4,200 employees**, on top of Techne's own smaller specialist team (below).

**What actually gets made here:** the facility's first seven floors handle real manufacturing across textiles, apparel, footwear, beauty/fragrance, and salon/spa equipment — plausibly including scaled-up production runs for Threadbourne's Automated Apparel Manufacturing lines and OAM-adjacent beauty/fragrance goods, though Metis Werks operates as its own company, not a subsidiary of either.
