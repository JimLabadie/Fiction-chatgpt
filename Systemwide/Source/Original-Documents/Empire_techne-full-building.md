# Techne — Full Building Structure (Draft)

*A separate building from Metis Werks entirely — Techne's own dedicated manufacturing complex for producing the equipment it designs, at real industrial scale, for the fashion, footwear, textile, beauty, and salon/spa industries broadly (not just supplying Metis Werks' own needs). Floor 23 is the top floor — the R&D/design floor already drafted — with a heliport on the roof directly above it.*

**Proposed Total Footprint:** ~3.2 million gross sq ft across 23 floors + 2 sublevels
**Proposed Total Workforce:** ~4,200 personnel
