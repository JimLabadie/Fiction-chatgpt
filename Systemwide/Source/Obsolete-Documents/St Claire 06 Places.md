# St. Claire — Places

The record of physical locations — buildings, public spaces, and sites — independent of whatever organization or household may occupy them. A Place can exist without any Organization (a park, the amphitheater, the cemetery) or without being residential.

## Place Record Template

- **Name** (or address-equivalent, if it doesn't have a proper name)
- **Zone** — one of the six zones in St_Claire_00_Concept.md, and why it fits there
- **Type** — commercial storefront, residential building, public space, industrial site, etc.
- **Physical description** — what it actually looks and feels like
- **History** — how long it's existed, what (if anything) occupied it before
- **Units** — a list, always present. A single-occupant Place has exactly one unit (no number needed). A multi-unit Place has one entry per unit/suite/apartment number. Each unit entry directly contains its own occupant(s):
  - An Organization (cross-referenced to St_Claire_05, tagged: primary tenant / servicing-contracted / secondary tenant), or
  - A named resident/Household (cross-referenced to St_Claire_03/St_Claire_04), or
  - "Vacant," or
  - For the Place as a whole rather than a unit — "public, unoccupied" (parks, civic space)

**Keystone Lofts and Market Hall**
- Zone: Working Riverfront (Old Town) — a converted former textile mill, consistent with the zone's uneven-revitalization industrial character
