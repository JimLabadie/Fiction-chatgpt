# St. Claire — Households

The record of shared living arrangements. Household-level facts (economic status, shared residence, relationship structure, shared assets) live here once, rather than being duplicated across each member's individual Character record in St_Claire_03_Population.md.

Consistent with Household Economics in St_Claire_00_Concept.md: affluence is a household-level outcome, not strictly an individual one. A member of modest personal means can genuinely belong to an affluent household if the combined household supports it.

## Household Record Template

- **Household label** (document heading only — for searchability, not necessarily an in-world name). Per the naming convention in St_Claire_00_Concept.md (Rites of Passage): individuals keep their own surnames regardless of household size. A two-person hyphenation here is a labeling convenience, not a claim that the characters share a legal or social surname. Larger households should use an informal house name instead of stringing multiple surnames together — ask what the household actually calls itself, don't default to hyphenating everyone.
- **Members** — one or more Characters (cross-referenced to St_Claire_03_Population.md)
- **Relationship structure** — single (not seeking), single (dating/open to it), partnered, polyamorous, chosen family, platonic roommates, etc.
- **Residence** — the specific Unit/Place occupied (cross-referenced to St_Claire_06_Places.md)
- **Household economic status** — where this household actually sits, and why (which members' occupations/organizations drive it)
- **Assets** — a list; each entry has specific descriptive detail (make, model, distinguishing features), not generic, and a primary user tagged to a specific Character if relevant
- **Animals/Pets** — a separate list, same logic: specific, named, with a primary caretaker if relevant
