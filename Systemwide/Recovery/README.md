# Recovery Records

Status: EVIDENCE AND RECONSTRUCTION WORKSPACE
Authority: NOT CANON

This tree contains source inventories, version comparisons, conflict analysis, provenance, and reconstruction work. It may point into `Systemwide/Source` because its purpose is to establish where claims came from and whether they were propagated correctly.

Recovery records do not control story or shared-world truth. Canon-facing records live under `system bible` and must state accepted material directly rather than requiring a reader to follow links into raw or superseded sources.

Allowed direction:

- recovery record → raw source or archived conversation;
- recovery record → controlling canon destination;
- controlling canon → other controlling canon.

Canon-facing records must not use raw sources or non-controlling recovery drafts as substitutes for substantive destinations.
