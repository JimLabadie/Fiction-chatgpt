### Reconfigurable partner suites

The residential program provides thirteen partner-suite positions, but they are constructed as a reconfigurable residential suite system rather than thirteen permanently fixed apartment footprints.

The system extends real-world operable-wall and divisible-suite architecture into an exceptionally high-budget residential installation. Structural columns, exterior walls, primary utility trunks, and other necessary building infrastructure remain fixed. Selected bedroom, sitting-room, dressing-room, closet, bathroom, and circulation boundaries are designed around predetermined reconfiguration zones.

Reconfiguration changes how the existing residential area is allocated; it does not create additional floor area. Individual suites can remain independent, expand into adjacent residential bays, or combine and redistribute adjoining bays into substantially larger private suites when household needs change.

Dressing and closet space is part of the reconfigurable program. Closet modules, dressing areas, cabinetry, and staging points can be reassigned or combined so that a larger suite can receive a correspondingly larger private dressing environment.

Ensuite space is also part of the reconfigurable program. Plumbing-intensive components remain connected to predetermined wet-service zones, but the Estate was constructed with sufficient plumbing, drainage, ventilation, electrical, and mechanical capacity to allow adjacent bathroom zones to be combined or reconfigured. A larger suite can therefore have one substantially larger ensuite rather than inheriting several smaller bathrooms merely because several original suite bays were combined.

Reconfigured spaces remain finished permanent-quality residential rooms rather than visibly partitioned conference spaces. Acoustic isolation, flooring transitions, ceilings, lighting, HVAC zoning, millwork, waterproofing, and concealed wall storage are designed around the permitted configurations.

Reconfiguration is deliberate rather than instantaneous. The Estate can change an approved configuration without conventional demolition and reconstruction, but rooms do not magically reshape themselves around their occupants.

The thirteen-position design capacity and partner relationship are established Estate specifications, not open story-state fields. A story may select a particular configuration without changing the reusable baseline.

## Dressing rooms and retrieval

### MC dressing room

The MC's dressing room contains twenty walk-in closets, each with three slide-out storage units and categories that can evolve with the collection. A Threadbourne Robotics Ariadne Adaptive Dressing Form provides a visible AWS staging point. A secure biometric vault joins the fine-jewelry and Footwear Vault collections.

The Fashion Empire's product-copy rule operates here: every applicable product released by a holding produces a copy in the MC's current size. The copy may enter active wardrobe, fitting, alteration, conservation, underground storage, or display according to its state. It does not require the MC to shop or place an order.

### Partner dressing room

The connected partner dressing room is a shared household wardrobe environment for the thirteen partners, with thirteen walk-in closet stations and thirteen permanently installed Threadbourne Robotics Ariadne Adaptive Dressing Forms. Reconfiguring residential partner-suite bays does not remove or cannibalize these thirteen shared wardrobe stations. Personal suites may also contain private dressing and closet space; the shared room exists for the deep wardrobe and retrieval ecosystem.

Six mobile retrieval robots dock in wall alcoves. Each has a warehouse-style drive base, telescoping vertical reach, and articulated arms and hands appropriate to garment handling. They travel between the dressing rooms and the B1/B2 wardrobe areas through the private lift network. The robots are docked out of sight when idle; the Ariadne forms remain ordinary visible fixtures.

The Estate maintains six additional unassigned Ariadne units in reserve for replacement, expansion, or approved residential reconfiguration. Together with the fourteen baseline installed forms — one in the MC dressing room and thirteen in the partner dressing room — the reusable Estate inventory therefore contains twenty Ariadne units. Reserve inventory does not establish additional household members or assignments.

### Private delivery connection

A discreet lift connects the MC dressing room, partner dressing room, Grand Wardrobe, B1 active inventory, and B2 long-term storage. Valuable or archived items can reach a fitting room without traveling through public or guest circulation.

## CLIO wardrobe and collection behavior

CLIO is the unified object, wardrobe, display, conservation, and logistics system that replaces the older WIS name. It does not create separate collections merely because objects occupy different rooms.

CLIO records at least:

* identity and description;
* owner;
* size and fitting state;
* current location;
* active wardrobe, storage, conservation, loan, display, staging, or transit state;
* condition and care requirements;
* access permissions;
* retrieval and delivery status;
* provenance and historical significance where applicable.

The MC and committed partners have reciprocal household access through tablets and appropriate interfaces. Ownership is recorded but is not automatically the access boundary. Staff receive only the access required for their jobs. A garment moving between a closet, B1, B2, a fitting room, and Mnemosyne remains the same object.

CLIO is logistics and recordkeeping, not an independent intelligence and not Athena.
