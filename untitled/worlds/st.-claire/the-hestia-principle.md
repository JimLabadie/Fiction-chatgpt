# The Hestia Principle

**Status:** ACCEPTED CULTURAL AND HISTORICAL BASELINE — ORIGIN DETAILS OPEN\
**Authority:** CONTROLLING ST. CLAIRE LORE COMPONENT

## Core truth

St. Claire's Hestia Principle expresses a simple civic and relational belief:

> Loving people protect the people they love.

Protection means building conditions in which a person remains safe enough to choose, refuse, leave, return, disagree, or change. Love does not turn housing, money, healthcare, employment, family recognition, or community belonging into weapons.

## Place in St. Claire

The principle belongs to St. Claire's history and lore. It may inform the district's family structures, mutual aid, housing protections, parallel legal instruments, chosen-family practices, and response to unequal power without reducing all of those institutions to one rule.

St. Claire's version is its own cultural development. It is not derived from a later story character's Court-TV rabbit hole or Athena's legal implementation, even though those may express the same underlying principle.

The following remain open and must not be silently invented:

* its exact relationship to St. Claire family court and parallel legal instruments;
* named historical figures, cases, organizations, or ceremonies associated with it;
* differences between the cultural ideal and imperfect practice.

The absence of those details does not make the Hestia Principle optional or merely a candidate. Its presence and core meaning are accepted; its history awaits collaborative recovery or development.
