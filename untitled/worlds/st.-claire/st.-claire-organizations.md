# St. Claire — Organizations

The record of every named business, nonprofit, and social/civic organization. "Organization" covers all three under one template, since the fields (leadership, staffing, category, community role) genuinely apply to all of them.

## Organization Record Template

* **Name**
* **Type** — business / nonprofit / social-civic
* **Category** — from the Master Category List in St\_Claire\_00B\_Rules\_and\_Mechanics.md
* **Locations** — one or more Places (cross-referenced to St\_Claire\_06\_Places.md), each tagged by role (headquarters, branch, warehouse, clinic, etc.); for social/civic organizations, this can be empty or "rotating" instead of a fixed location
* **Capacity** — the real size measure appropriate to the category, so staffing can actually be checked against something: seating capacity for dining; approximate floor size or a size descriptor (small/single-room, mid-size, large) for retail; patient/client panel size or visit volume for health & wellness; caseload or membership count for civic/nonprofit; bay count or crew size for trades. Every Organization gets one of these, not just food & hospitality.
* **Owner/leadership** — named, cross-referenced to St\_Claire\_03\_Population.md
* **Staffing structure** — roles and rough quantities needed (e.g. "2 servers, 1 cook"); can be broken out per location if large enough to need it. Named only as a story actually needs them — unnamed slots aren't gaps, they're just not yet needed. Once a role is named, it includes a typical schedule/shift (e.g. "Bartender — Ruth Halloway's cousin, works Tues–Sat evenings"), so a scene set at a specific day/time can identify who's actually on duty rather than picking arbitrarily or having every named staff member present at once.
* **Price tier / accessibility** (for-profit) or **funding source** (nonprofit — ties to the gala/mutual-aid funding pattern in St\_Claire\_00)
* **Community role** — any connection to the safety net, mentorship, fundraising events, or "pay it forward" culture, if applicable
* **Operating hours** — for businesses with public hours (especially food/hospitality)
* **Meal service** — which meals are served (breakfast/lunch/dinner/late-night), where applicable
* **Seating capacity** — for dine-in establishments, a real number consistent with the business's described scale
* **History**
* **One real, specific detail** — something that makes it feel like an actual place, not a category filled in
* **Title exceptions** — if this organization uses a non-standard title for a role (rather than the standard owner/president/director/coordinator convention), flag it here
