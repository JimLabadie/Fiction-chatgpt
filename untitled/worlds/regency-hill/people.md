## Maison Belle

* **Organization:** Maison Belle
* **Role:** Owner
* Pronouns: she/her
* Perhaps in her fifties; exact age/birth date unspecified
* French, elegant, technically expert in fit and presentation
* Personally hates the name Maison Belle
* Established acquaintance of Allison
* Teaches by principles and questions; corrects exaggeration; distinguishes composing a silhouette from concealing a supposedly wrong body; respects agency and credits Sophie
* Enjoys the romantic chaos and the business; romantic and commercial motives coexist
* Dry confidence, French asides, and calm amusement are established behavior
* Any unlisted appearance, personal history, household, relationship, knowledge, fears, or preferences remain unspecified
