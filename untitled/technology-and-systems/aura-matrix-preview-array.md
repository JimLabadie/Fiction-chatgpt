# Aura-Matrix Preview Array

**Status:** Active reusable technology\
**Type:** Presentation and visualization product\
**Manufacturer:** Threadbourne Robotics

## Purpose

The Aura-Matrix Preview Array is an advanced smart-mirror system that renders proposed appearance and presentation changes on the user in real time without physically altering the user.

The technology originated from an existing commercial smart-mirror concept and was substantially extended within the fiction technology ecosystem.

Its fictional development began with a simple visualization problem:

_"I have stared at fourteen swatches for three weeks and I still don't actually know what any of them will look like on that wall. I don't want to guess and regret it later. I want to just see it, before I have to commit to it."_

The underlying rendering concept was later generalized from interior visualization into personal presentation preview.

## Presentation Preview

The Preview Array can render supported appearance changes including:

* hairstyle;
* hair length and arrangement;
* hair color;
* makeup looks and individual cosmetic choices; and
* other supported visual presentation changes.

The displayed result remains a visualization. Previewing a change does not perform the change, authorize a treatment, or imply consent to later execution.

## System Boundaries

The Aura-Matrix Preview Array is a standalone Threadbourne Robotics product.

It is not an OAM treatment device, an OAM treatment surface, CLIO, or Athena.

OAM systems may use Preview Array technology during consultation and treatment planning.

CLIO may exchange relevant wardrobe, sizing, styling, and presentation information with a Preview Array when useful, but neither system controls or subsumes the other.

## Estate Installation

Lesbos Estate's master ensuite contains fourteen Aura-Matrix Preview Arrays, one at each of its fourteen salon-quality vanity stations.

The Estate installation does not define the product itself; it is one deployment of reusable technology.
