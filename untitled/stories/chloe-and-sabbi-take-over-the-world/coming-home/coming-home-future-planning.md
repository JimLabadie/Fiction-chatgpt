# Coming Home — Future Planning

**Status:** Story-development planning, not approved manuscript prose.

This page preserves selected future direction, intended later beats, and unresolved decisions for _Coming Home_ so they can be retrieved before drafting later scenes. It does not make unwritten events part of the manuscript merely by recording them here.

## Immediate Continuation After Scene 5

Scene 5 ends inside the Estate spa. Chloe has deferred the two unusual rooms because they deserve their own day, and Chloe and Sabbi are walking toward manicures.

The next story movement should **not** jump directly to NAS, OAM, or Project Hecate.

### Fun Night In

Chloe and Sabbi stay at the Estate for their first evening rather than immediately going out into St. Claire.

The purpose is to let them begin actually **living together as best friends**, not merely continue a tour of Chloe's extraordinary existing life.

Possible texture includes:

* finishing the spa/manicures;
* comfortable, feminine clothes or pajamas;
* food, wine or champagne, trash television or a terrible movie;
* sprawling somewhere inappropriate for eating;
* talking shit and laughing together;
* Athena participating enough that Sabbi and Athena's emerging alliance becomes increasingly dangerous to Chloe.

These are available scene ingredients, not a requirement to include every item.

The emotional contrast matters: after the Estate, Grand Wardrobe, Moon Shoes, staff, spa, and other extravagance, what Chloe and Sabbi want is an ordinary best-friend night together.

## Sabbi's BFF Suite Reveal

During the first evening, the natural practical question can arise: **where does Sabbi actually sleep?**

This leads to the reveal of Sabbi's established BFF Suite.

The reveal should not function primarily as another luxury-property tour. The emotional point is that nearly everything Sabbi has experienced so far has been part of Chloe's pre-existing life opened to her. The BFF Suite is different: it is a place in the Estate specifically configured for **Sabbi**.

Useful continuity to pay off:

* Scene 1: Sabbi says, "I don't live here."
* Chloe repeatedly shifts the language from _mine_ to _ours_.
* Scene 2: Chloe tells Sabbi, "You live here."
* Scene 5: Sabbi toasts "our first day home."
* The suite can turn the repeated language of _our house_ into something materially personal for Sabbi.

The suite is not a duplicate of Chloe's master suite. Its design and furnishing should feel like Sabbi rather than Chloe copied twice.

Established suite features available for the reveal:

* approximately the overall floor area of Chloe's master suite;
* substantial bedroom;
* private sitting/living space;
* substantial private dressing room;
* one exceptionally luxurious ensuite comfortable for Sabbi alone or for multiple people getting ready together;
* Sabbi's own Ariadne Adaptive Dressing Form;
* continued access to the shared partner dressing room and deeper wardrobe infrastructure.

Do **not** invent how many original partner-suite bays were combined to make it.

### Ariadne in the Suite

The BFF Suite reveal is a natural opportunity to introduce Sabbi's Ariadne without revealing NAS.

At this point Ariadne can simply be understood as Sabbi's personal adaptive dressing form / wardrobe-staging system.

Its deeper NAS synchronization should remain dormant as a story reveal until Sabbi learns about NAS.

Ariadne can provide comedy after the emotional suite reveal rather than displacing it.

### Ending the First Night

Favor a small domestic action over another technological escalation.

Possible examples include Sabbi deciding where one of her own possessions belongs, leaving her purse somewhere because the room is hers, using her own vanity, or Chloe and Sabbi establishing some tiny roommate/best-friend habit.

The point is to let the absurd suite become **home**.

## Day Two — St. Claire

After Chloe and Sabbi have spent their first night actually living together, the story can expand outward into St. Claire.

Working rhythm:

**Day one: our house.**\
**First night: our life.**\
**Day two: our town.**

St. Claire is their community, not merely a destination outside the Estate.

The exact Day Two outing, businesses, people, encounters, and activities remain **open** until chosen. Do not invent them merely to fill the plan.

The Barbie convertible remains available for the outing if we choose it.

## Later — Return to the Two Little Rooms

The NAS/OAM rooms remain deliberately unresolved after Scene 5.

They should be revisited only after Chloe and Sabbi have had some actual life together. The delay is useful: the machines are possibilities within their life, not the thing their life is waiting to begin.

When the rooms are eventually opened, preserve these principles:

* Both women are already women, already themselves, and already beautiful.
* NAS and OAM offer **choice**, not legitimacy.
* Chloe does not fix Sabbi, give Sabbi womanhood, tell Sabbi what she should change, or make a configuration choice for her.
* Sabbi may choose changes, no changes, partial changes, experimentation, or delay.
* Chloe's established description remains: **"Your get-to-be-you machine."**
* Chloe has established that NAS is available **for us, but you don't have to.**
* OAM is presentation and play rather than a requirement for beauty or femininity.
* Preview is not consent to execute.

### Hecate Emotional Beat

Project Hecate allows a supported NAS configuration to be previewed before execution.

The intended emotional progression is:

1. the machine creates the realization **I could**;
2. Hecate allows the woman to see a possible body rather than imagine an abstract promise;
3. the recognition beat can land simply as **"Oh. That's me."**
4. the choice remains hers afterward.

Do not turn Hecate into a character creator or beauty-ranking interface.

The preview should show a possible self, not announce an ideal self.

Chloe's role is to be present with Sabbi, not to fix, persuade, or select.

The emotional reveal may be intense — the working image is a **Cinderella-at-Disney-World breakdown** for both women — but the story's comedy can return afterward. Sincerity does not require the story to become solemn permanently.

Execution of any NAS or OAM choice is **not yet planned or established** and should not be silently bundled into the reveal.
