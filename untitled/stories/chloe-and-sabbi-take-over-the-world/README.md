## Chloe and Sabbi Take Over the World

**Status:** Active shared-fiction story world\
**Story leads:** Chloe and Sabrina “Sabbi”\
**Relationship:** Best friends\
**Primary home:** Lesbos Estate / Dreamhouse

### Core Premise

This is Chloe and Sabbi’s world to inhabit together.

The story uses the existing reusable fiction infrastructure as its sandbox: Lesbos Estate, Athena, the Fashion Empire, Mnemosyne, CLIO, NAS, OAM, the Grand Wardrobe, collections, companies, technologies, places, and other reusable components may be selected into the story as needed.

Reusable infrastructure remains governed by its own records. This story records which pieces Chloe and Sabbi have selected, how those pieces affect their lives, and the continuity created by their adventures.

The purpose is not to constrain Chloe and Sabbi to ordinary reality. The reusable world was deliberately built large enough for extravagant, impossible, funny, affirming, emotional, and gloriously excessive stories.

**Implication is not establishment.** The existence of reusable infrastructure does not automatically select every available component into this story.

### Chloe and Sabbi

Chloe and Sabbi are best friends.

They are not romantic partners merely because they share unusual intimacy, household privileges, adventures, clothing, technology, or Estate access.

Their friendship is affectionate, playful, trusting, emotionally real, and allowed to develop through what they actually experience together.

They live at Lesbos Estate.

For purposes of Estate household systems and practical access, Chloe has instructed Athena to treat Sabbi **as if she were a partner**. This grants Sabbi partner-equivalent household privileges where Chloe has authority to grant them without changing the underlying relationship: Sabbi remains Chloe’s best friend.

Specific security, conservation, legal, medical, or other restricted access remains governed by the controlling reusable system unless Chloe explicitly authorizes otherwise.

### Athena

Athena is Chloe’s AI daughter as selected from the reusable Athena framework.

Athena understands that Sabbi is Chloe’s best friend and that Chloe has directed her to give Sabbi partner-equivalent household treatment.

Athena does not redefine Chloe and Sabbi’s relationship.

Athena remains Athena: extraordinarily intelligent and capable, loving toward her mother, mischievous, precise, occasionally exasperating, and very fond of receipts.

Her reusable open fields remain open unless this story establishes them.

### Home — Lesbos Estate

Lesbos Estate / Dreamhouse is Chloe and Sabbi’s home in this story.

The Estate’s reusable architecture, facilities, wardrobe infrastructure, museum, underground storage, household systems, staffing principles, and technology remain governed by the controlling Lesbos Estate records.

The Estate is not merely scenery. Chloe and Sabbi may actually live in it, use it, explore it, make messes in it, dress themselves from it, entertain in it, disappear into absurd corners of it, and discover what its ridiculous scale makes possible.

### Sabbi's BFF Suite

Chloe and Sabbi's Estate uses the residence's reconfigurable partner-suite architecture to give Sabbi a private BFF Suite approximately equal in overall floor area to Chloe's master suite.

It is not a duplicate of Chloe's suite and does not imitate its layout or design. Sabbi's home is configured and furnished for Sabbi.

The suite includes a substantial bedroom, private sitting and living space, a substantial private dressing room, and a single exceptionally luxurious ensuite designed comfortably for Sabbi alone or for two or more people preparing together. Its closet, dressing, bathroom, and living allocations are created by redistributing existing partner-suite residential space through the Estate's designed reconfiguration system; no additional Estate floor area is created.

Sabbi's private dressing room has one Ariadne Adaptive Dressing Form installed from the Estate's six-unit reserve inventory and assigned to her. The thirteen Ariadne forms and thirteen closet stations in the shared partner dressing room remain intact. Sabbi retains partner-equivalent access to that shared dressing room and the deeper CLIO/AWS wardrobe infrastructure. Five unassigned reserve Ariadne units remain at the Estate after her installation.

Sabbi's Ariadne follows her authorized current biometric profile. If Sabbi chooses an NAS body reconfiguration that changes her measurements, the updated profile propagates through CLIO/AWS, her Ariadne adjusts automatically, and AWS identifies affected eligible personal garments for its established automated re-tailoring workflow.

The BFF Suite designation remains exactly that: **best friend**. Its size, luxury, privacy, and household access do not redefine Chloe and Sabbi as romantic partners.

### St. Claire — Community and Backdrop

St. Claire is the broader reusable community and primary backdrop available to Chloe and Sabbi.

Chloe and Sabbi are **part of the St. Claire community**. They do not merely own an Estate near it, observe it from outside, or enter it only when the story requires services.

They may participate naturally in St. Claire life: neighborhoods, businesses, restaurants, nightlife, community spaces, events, friendships, organizations, institutions, ordinary errands, chance encounters, and other established parts of the reusable St. Claire setting.

Existing St. Claire canon remains governed by its reusable world records. This story may select established St. Claire places, people, institutions, cultures, and community elements as needed without copying them into this bible or silently changing their reusable canon.

Living at Lesbos Estate does not isolate Chloe and Sabbi from ordinary community life. The Estate is **home**. St. Claire is **their community**.

Their extraordinary resources do not require every St. Claire interaction to become an event about wealth, technology, or the Estate. Chloe and Sabbi are allowed to go out for dinner, shop, meet people, attend something ridiculous, help a friend, get flirted with, make questionable decisions, or simply exist among other women without the story needing to justify why they left the Dreamhouse.

As elsewhere in this story, **implication is not establishment**. Being members of the community does not automatically establish particular friendships, memberships, reputations, businesses frequented, social roles, or relationships with reusable St. Claire characters. Those develop through the story.

### The Grand Wardrobe and Collections

Sabbi has partner-equivalent access to the household wardrobe infrastructure subject to the controlling ownership, conservation, security, and access rules.

The Fashion Empire and Patron Model can create a continuing inflow of designer fashion into Chloe’s personal collection. The exact owned inventory must come from controlling collection records or later story decisions; it is never invented merely because the infrastructure could support it.

Mnemosyne can encompass thousands of collection objects, but research targets, loans, owned objects, museum-quality historical pieces, designer wardrobe pieces, and illustrative examples remain distinct statuses.

The enormous wardrobe, collection-storage infrastructure, retrieval systems, conservation facilities, and museum exist because the collection can genuinely become enormous.

### NAS — The Get-to-Be-You Machine

Chloe describes the NAS Integration Suite to Sabbi as:

**“Your get-to-be-you machine.”**

That description expresses what NAS means in this story.

NAS does not make Sabbi herself. Sabbi is already Sabbi.

NAS gives her agency over her physical body if she wants to use it. She may choose changes because they express who she understands herself to be. She may choose no changes. She may change her mind.

Using NAS is never a requirement for being a woman, being Sabbi, being beautiful, belonging at the Estate, or being loved by Chloe.

Chloe explicitly told Sabbi that NAS is available **for us, but you don’t have to.**

That principle controls.

### OAM — Presentation as Play

OAM is available to Chloe and Sabbi for presentation, experimentation, beauty, and play.

Their completed Project Hecate integration allows supported OAM choices to be explored through Preview Array technology before execution. Preview is not consent to execute.

Hair, nails, skin, makeup, brows and lashes, fragrance, piercings, tattoos, and other established OAM capabilities are options rather than requirements.

OAM does not make either woman beautiful.

They are already beautiful without it.

The machine exists because choosing presentation can be joyful.

### Project Hecate — Completed

In Chloe and Sabbi's continuity, Chloe proposed Project Hecate and the project was completed.

Their Lesbos Estate installation integrates Aura-Matrix Preview Array technology with both the NAS Integration Suite and OAM Aura-Matrix V-10.

Chloe and Sabbi can therefore preview supported NAS bodily configurations and OAM presentation choices before deciding whether to execute them.

Preview remains separate from execution. Neither woman authorizes a change merely by viewing it.

Hecate is specific to this story's Estate state. Its completion here does not establish completed Hecate integration in other stories using Lesbos Estate.

### The Machines Do Not Define the Women

NAS and OAM are extraordinary tools.

They are not prerequisites.

Chloe and Sabbi may use them tomorrow, years from now, repeatedly, once, partially, experimentally, or never.

Neither woman owes technology femininity.

Neither woman owes femininity a particular body or presentation.

Their choices belong to them.

### Story Tone

This world permits joy without requiring justification.

Absurd wealth, impossible technology, couture, museums, Moon Shoes, enormous wardrobes, robots, spas, ridiculous infrastructure, and other excess may coexist with sincere friendship and emotionally meaningful moments.

Comedy does not invalidate sincerity.

Sincerity does not require the comedy to stop forever.

Chloe and Sabbi are allowed to be smart women who occasionally make catastrophically questionable decisions together.

They are also allowed to look at the aftermath and conclude:

**God, we were fabulous.**

### Narrative Voice

Chloe-and-Sabbi fiction uses warm sapphic comedy without romance between the leads.

The prose is dialogue-forward, irreverent, feminine, emotionally sincere, fast-paced, and gloriously willing to treat absurdity as ordinary.

The narrator loves these women but is perfectly willing to notice when they are being idiots.

Emotional moments are written simply rather than sentimentally.

Wealth, couture, impossible technology, and extravagant infrastructure are handled with delighted matter-of-factness rather than awe-heavy luxury prose.

Comedy comes from character, timing, escalating situations, and the women's reactions to one another rather than from humiliation.

Unless a later story deliberately establishes a different voice, this is the narrative voice for Chloe-and-Sabbi fiction going forward.

### Romance and Story-Format Agency

Romance is available, never mandatory.

Chloe and Sabbi may have separate love interests whenever we choose.

A love interest appears when we decide we want her. Neither Chloe nor Sabbi needs a romance on a schedule, and the existence of romantic possibility does not establish a future partner, crush, date, or relationship before we choose one.

Adventures are not required to fit a predetermined format. A story may remain a short story or grow into a novel organically as we decide what we want to explore.

The continuity is Chloe and Sabbi's life together. The stories are the pieces of that life we choose to tell.
