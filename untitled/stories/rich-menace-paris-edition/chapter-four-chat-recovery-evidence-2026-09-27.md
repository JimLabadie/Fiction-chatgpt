## Domestic Saturday recovered from chat

The developed material treats the shopping as practical infrastructure while their behavior undermines the fake-girlfriend label. The point is not a shopping spectacle. It is the first ordinary public texture of two women building a household while insisting the relationship is temporary/fake.
