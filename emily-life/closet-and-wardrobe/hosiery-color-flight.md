#### Role of the flight

This flight tests what happens when the legs themselves provide a major part of the outfit's color. It intentionally favors expressive colors over safer navy, forest, chocolate, gray, military, or black alternatives already represented elsewhere in the hosiery vocabulary.

#### Approval boundary and verification

Do not silently substitute another Oroblù line, adjacent shade, or generic opaque tight.

Current product research identified these as real All Colors 50 colorways and indicated relevant L/XL availability during the shopping pass. Exact current SKU, size availability at integration time, fiber content, and manufacturer care must be captured or reconfirmed during item-level operational integration rather than invented.

This flight extends the governing hosiery principle: color is a first-class expressive dimension alongside opacity, finish, texture, construction, and detail.
