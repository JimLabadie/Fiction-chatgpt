### Emotional / styling role

This pairing is for private glamour with no audience or occasion required: silk, drape, color, and theatrical ease for the simple pleasure of being beautifully dressed at home.

The point is not sleep utility or ordinary loungewear efficiency. The point is **I get to be pretty when nobody is looking**—with enough drama that the outfit feels deliberately excessive rather than merely nice.

Heels, jewelry, hosiery, fragrance, and hair may be added when the mood supports them, but they are optional styling layers rather than required parts of the owned outfit.
