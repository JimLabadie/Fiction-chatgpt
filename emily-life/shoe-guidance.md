# Shoe Guidance

## Governing status

This page governs shoe selection and interpretation.

## Collection quantity

**36 pairs is a planning baseline, not a cap.** The collection may exceed 36 when strong real designer finds are available at reasonable prices. Quality, fit with Emily's aesthetic, and value take precedence over an arbitrary numerical limit. Time remains the main constraint on building the virtual collection. Previous lower targets are superseded and must not be used as caps or completion calculations.

Category allocations are planning guidance rather than limits. **Going forward, favor higher category counts rather than stopping as soon as the old planning minimum is satisfied.** Strong finds outside or beyond an allocation may be treated as bonus pairs and added to the collection total.

## Heel-height baseline

* **Flat means a 3.5-inch heel with no platform.**
* Roughly **4–4.5 inches is a normal commercial high-heel range** for Emily.
* Genuine high heels are normal everyday shoes.

## Practical footwear

Practical footwear must still follow Emily's aesthetic. Do not insert conventional ballet flats, loafers, kitten heels, or other low shoes merely to satisfy a generic wardrobe model. Sneakers belong only where the activity genuinely calls for sneakers; they are not substitutes for Emily's everyday flat category.

## Selection philosophy

* Shoes are strongly heel-biased.
* Real identifiable shoes are required; older or discontinued pieces are valid.
* New retail, authenticated resale, designer clearance, past-season, discontinued, and quality secondary-market pieces are all valid acquisition sources.
* The collection should feel accumulated over decades rather than purchased all at once.
* Luxury is normal-ish; extravagance is selective; collector pieces are rare.
* Functional coverage and shoes chosen simply because Emily loves them can coexist.
* When quality designer shoes are found at reasonable cost, numerical category targets should not force rejection of a worthwhile pair.
* **Color breadth is an explicit collection goal.** When rounding out a category, prioritize worthwhile colors, materials, prints, metallics, and textures that expand outfit options rather than repeatedly adding near-duplicate black or neutral shoes.
* Honest wear on a good secondary-market shoe can support the accumulated, lived-in authenticity of the collection; condition should be weighed against price, quality, and desirability rather than requiring every pair to appear pristine.

## Sizing baseline

Women's US 8.5 is the governing shoe-size baseline. Brand-specific equivalent sizing and fit evidence should be recorded when relevant.

## Persistence rule

Future approved changes to shoe philosophy, heel-height interpretation, functional categories, or numeric targets must be written here when approved. Do not leave governing shoe decisions only in conversation history.
