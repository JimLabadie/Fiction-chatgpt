**Operational container ledgers**

Contents ledgers belong to established **generic container classes/roles**, not to individual physical products. The generic classes include ordinary roles such as **Purse**, **Makeup Bag**, **Tech / Dense Utility Pouch**, **Personal Care / Emergency Pouch**, **Wristlet**, and **Lanyard**, and may also be purpose-specific generic roles such as **Admirals Fan Bag** and **Brewers Fan Bag**.

A physical object is an inventory item that can fill one of those roles. Multiple physical objects may exist for a role; for example, Emily may own multiple makeup bags while maintaining one Makeup Bag ledger.

When a subcontainer is currently carried inside a parent container, the **subcontainer itself appears once as an item in the parent container's ledger**. Its internal contents remain only in the subcontainer's own ledger. The parent ledger therefore records the physical containment relationship without copying, flattening, or absorbing the subcontainer's contents.

Changing which physical purse, makeup bag, wristlet, or fan bag fills a role does not create a new ledger. Moving a subcontainer between parents changes the single subcontainer item recorded in the relevant parent ledger; it does not move the subcontainer's individual contents into the parent ledger.

**Collection counts**

The authoritative bag inventory is the set of current item rows.

Tier membership and item identity may be recorded on those rows. Current tier counts and total handbag counts are derived from the live item rows when needed; they are not separately maintained as authoritative facts.

Do not use a historical acquisition-round count, prose total, remembered number, or previous summary as evidence of current inventory completeness.
