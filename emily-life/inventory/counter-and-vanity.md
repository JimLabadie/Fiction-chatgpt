**Hair-tool collection direction**

Emily's hair-tool inventory is intentionally not minimalist. She likes to experiment with hairstyles and may own overlapping real tools when they provide meaningfully different styling results. Current owned tools are the starting core, not a completeness ceiling.

Future real-product selection should evaluate capability coverage including multiple curl sizes, waving, crimping/texture, hot rollers/volume, precision styling, sectioning/support tools, and other genuinely distinct techniques. Do not mark generic tools as owned before a real product is selected.

**Expanded experimental styling tools**

Different barrel sizes and specialty tools are intentional capability overlap. Do not treat them as redundant merely because multiple tools can curl or wave hair.

**Completed vanity hardware initialization**

A pencil sharpener remains unnecessary unless a future owned cosmetic requires one. Additional brushes and specialty applicators are optional collection growth rather than foundation requirements.
