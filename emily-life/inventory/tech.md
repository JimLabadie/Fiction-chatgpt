# Tech

Actual personal technology inventory. Record real owned or assigned devices; do not substitute recommended products for actual hardware.

## Boundary

Tech inventory records actual devices separately from purse/backpack utility standards. A recommended charger, power bank, cable, mouse, flash drive, or other accessory is not owned merely because it appears in the Product Ledger as a restock or infrastructure standard. Employer-owned/work equipment should be recorded distinctly from personal ownership when its exact device is recovered.
