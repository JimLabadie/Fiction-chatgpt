**Excluded from automatic utility selection**

Medication and personally sensitive health items require existing personal requirements. Emily does not wear contact lenses, so contact-lens products are not part of the purse/backpack system. Cosmetics and fragrance are selected in their dedicated later beauty/fragrance work and then referenced here for purse carry/restocking rather than selected independently. Bags, wallet/cardholder, backpack, padfolio, umbrella, water bottle, cosmetic pouches, reusable shopping bag, and other visible style-bearing pieces remain part of the personal-style pass.

**Initial-stock reconciliation — 2026-09-28**

**Location-accounting rule**

The Product Ledger records ownership/system-wide stock, but operational availability is location-specific. Purse inventory, bathroom/home inventory, closet inventory, travel stock, and other assigned locations must not be treated as interchangeable merely because the item is owned.

A transfer changes location allocation without changing system-wide quantity. Exact-count stock should reconcile location counts to the total. A home product does not satisfy purse readiness unless a unit is actually carried, and purse stock does not satisfy bathroom/home stocking. If a location shortage can be solved by moving existing surplus, prefer reallocation over declaring a purchase need.

**Dedicated purse duplicates**

Ordinary recurring purse essentials may intentionally duplicate home products. When both locations require routine readiness, record separate physical units rather than treating one home unit as if it exists in both places. Each copy has its own location and depletion state. Exact-count bulk supplies can be reallocated into the purse, reducing home quantity while preserving system-wide total.

**Scarf collection**

Approved 2026-09-29. Scarves are treated as punctuation for the wardrobe, analogous to the personality role of brooches: they may polish, add color, soften, create movement, introduce mischief, or take control of an outfit. They are not required to literally match shoes, handbags, or clothing.

**Collection principle:** accessories can comment rather than merely coordinate. polish, play, color intervention, storytelling, weird art, and one spectacular statement piece. Price and brand status do not determine importance.

**Belt collection — statement foundation**

Approved 2026-09-29.

**Governing styling principle:** solid footing → intentional tension → visual reconciliation. A belt may provoke a first reaction of “I would not have put those together” when color, hardware, proportion, texture, silhouette, or another element then reconciles the apparent mismatch. The goal is adjacent to clash, not random clash.

**Traditional-anchor principle:** traditional belts are not generic basics. Their job is elegance and precision: restrained enough to alter an outfit quietly, with proportion, finish, and hardware creating the effect. They provide a deliberate contrast to expressive pieces.
