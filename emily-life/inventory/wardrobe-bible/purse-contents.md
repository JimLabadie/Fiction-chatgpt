# Purse Contents

## Structure

Contents scale with bag size. Larger tiers inherit smaller-tier functions without requiring duplicate objects. Necessary medication, identification, payment, keys, and occasion needs override tier limits. Exact non-expressive restock products are maintained in the Product Ledger rather than duplicated here.

## Tier 1 — Clutch / Micro Bag

### Core

* Smartphone
* Driver's license or ID
* Primary credit card
* Backup payment card
* Small amount of cash
* Required house or car keys
* Emergency-contact card

### Beauty

* Lipstick or lip tint
* Lip balm or gloss
* Tiny compact mirror
* Mini perfume vial or atomizer
* Oil-blotting sheet
* 1–2 hair pins or ties

### Personal

* 1–2 feminine hygiene products
* Necessary medication
* Dental floss pick
* Breath mint or gum
* Tissue

### Emergency

* One adhesive bandage
* One blister bandage
* Safety pin
* Stain-removal wipe

## Tier 2 — Small Purse / Crossbody

Tier 2 inherits Tier 1 functions and expands them for several hours away from home.

### Wallet & organization

* Compact wallet or cardholder
* Coins
* Insurance cards
* Gift cards when relevant
* Receipt sleeve

### Beauty & grooming

* Pressed powder compact
* Hand cream
* Small sunscreen stick
* Additional bobby pins or hair ties
* Tiny folding comb or brush
* Perfume atomizer

### Hygiene

* Hand sanitizer
* Wet wipes
* Additional tissues
* Additional feminine hygiene products
* Breath mints or gum
* Floss picks

### Small health kit

* Usual over-the-counter personal-care items as needed
* Adhesive and blister bandages
* Cough drops
* Eye drops if relevant

### Tiny emergency kit

* Stain-removal pen
* Several safety pins
* Earplugs
* Very small purse hook

## Inheritance rule

Tier 2 does not require a second copy of every Tier 1 object. It replaces or expands the smaller version where appropriate. The same principle governs later tiers.

## Tier 3 — Everyday Handbag

Tier 3 is the normal daily purse. It inherits Tier 1 and Tier 2 functions and carries enough support for a full ordinary day without becoming a tote or backpack.

### Health & first aid

* Adhesive bandages
* Blister bandages
* Antibiotic ointment packet
* Cough drops or throat lozenges

### Personal care & hygiene

* Travel tissues
* Wet wipes or body wipes
* Feminine hygiene products
* Dental floss picks

### Food & comfort

* Tea bag or sweetener packet when personally useful

### Tier 3 boundary

Bulky or heavy support items normally move to Tier 4 or Tier 5 rather than turning the everyday handbag into a backpack. Examples include a wall charger, tablet or e-reader, full water bottle, umbrella, larger first-aid supplies, travel towel, larger emergency kit, and other equipment better suited to a tote or backpack.

### Tier 4 boundary

Tier 4 is still a handbag. A laptop, full-size tablet, bulky charger collection, large water bottle, larger first-aid kit, and other heavy or bulky support equipment belong in Tier 5 / Backpack.

Items found during general research are not automatically canonical; situational extras require specific approval.

## Tier 5 — Backpack

Tier 5 is the full-day, work, and travel-support tier. It inherits the functions of Tiers 1–4 without requiring literal duplication of every object. The backpack is modular: work, travel, seasonal, health, and personal-care modules are added when relevant rather than carried at maximum load every day.

### Work & tech

* Laptop
* Full-size tablet when useful
* Laptop charger
* Phone charger
* Wall adapter

### Tier 5 modularity rule
