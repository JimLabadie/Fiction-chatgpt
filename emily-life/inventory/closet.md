**Shared Fan Gear Closet**

Emily Life maintains a **Shared Fan Gear Closet** for wearable sports-fan clothing and accessories that are available for use by Emily, Kate, and other established girls when fit, occasion, and continuity permit.

The Shared Fan Gear Closet is distinct from Emily’s and Kate’s dedicated Brewers and Admirals fan bags. Wearable fan gear such as T-shirts, sweaters, jackets, jerseys, hats, and similar clothing belongs in the Shared Fan Gear Closet rather than being treated as bag contents. Personal fan bags remain the authoritative homes for the portable fan objects assigned to them.

The Shared Fan Gear Closet contains the girls' owned fan inventory assigned to that location. It does not represent Emily's separately owned copy of a mirrored item. Real-world sports items mirrored into Emily Life create separate Emily and girls copies, and each copy is recorded separately in its actual established container or surface.

Real fan gear established from Jim’s existing possessions may be retrospectively reconciled into the Shared Fan Gear Closet. New real products that Emily explicitly chooses or approves are acquired into Emily Life under the normal simulated-acquisition rules and become owned inventory.

**Shared fan-gear sizing:** Use Emily’s established sizing as the working size for Shared Fan Gear Closet acquisitions unless the specific product requires a brand-specific adjustment. Shared fan apparel is considered practically available to the established girls without maintaining separate individual sizing records; handle meaningful fit exceptions only when they actually arise.

**Hosiery**

Hosiery is a substantial wardrobe category because dresses and skirts are the norm. The collection should include full tights/pantyhose, stay-ups/thigh-highs, and traditional stockings with a garter belt, with variety in color and texture rather than only nude and black basics.
