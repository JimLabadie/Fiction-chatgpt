## Great Gathering Hall

The former central concourse, three anchor courts, and glass atrium provide approximately 46,000 square feet of inherited space. Approximately 40,000 square feet is finished as the Great Gathering Hall; approximately 6,000 square feet remains vestibules, common restrooms, protected circulation, and support.

Custom acoustical treatment, massive stone fireplaces, and continuous conversational seating allow the hall to feel habitable in ordinary use while scaling for family, staff, invited guests, celebrations, and major gatherings. It remains the principal above-grade circulation spine and is not counted inside the 26,000-square-foot private residence.

## Epicurean kitchen and dining

The culinary program controls approximately 66,000 square feet of inherited junior-anchor, food-court, restaurant, and food-hall space. Approximately 45,000 square feet is finished program; approximately 21,000 square feet supports catering, storage, circulation, cold rooms, event preparation, and hospitality reserve.

### Master kitchen

The master kitchen is the converted gourmet food hall. It is monumental but meant for real cooking rather than visual display.

- A 30-foot imported Italian-marble island provides multiple simultaneous workstations.
- Four commercial induction ranges integrate into residential-quality millwork.
- Multiple Sub-Zero column refrigerators, drawer freezers, wine storage, and produce refrigeration support daily and event use.
- Two industrial dishwashing systems are screened within the cabinetry.
- A dedicated pastry station has marble rolling slabs, flour handling, and specialized cooling.
- Espresso machines, high-speed blenders, dehydrators, fermentation equipment, precision tools, and an automated pasta maker remain accessible rather than staged as decoration.
- Zoning allows dinner preparation, baking, pastry, and plating to occur at the same time without collisions.

### Dining spaces

- an intimate family dining room for ordinary meals;
- a formal banquet hall for up to approximately 500;
- an outdoor kitchen and dining terrace overlooking the pool;
- food-court and restaurant-unit support for casual meals, staff dining, pastry, coffee, and catered events.

The culinary department may support Anchor B events but Mnemosyne does not maintain a redundant standing catering department.

## Internal receiving and distribution

The main building contains approximately 6,000 square feet of climate-controlled intake, sorting, and staging inside the former loading dock. It accepts only people and goods cleared through the external operations district.

After handoff, the internal network uses:

- approximately 18,000 square feet of above-grade service corridors and loading support;
- B1 receiving, processing, and freight lobbies;
- B2 service drives and protected freight circulation;
- quiet low-speed carts;
- private and service elevators;
- dedicated lifts to Anchor A, wardrobe, laundry, kitchens, guest areas, fitness, and Anchor B.

Commercial trucks do not enter residential or ceremonial circulation. Household deliveries do not require visible movement through public rooms.

## B1 laundry and garment services

The 45,000-square-foot plant handles household clothing, linens, guest services, fitness laundry, and the extreme throughput produced by the wardrobe ecosystem.

It contains separate soiled and clean routes; sorting; washing; wet cleaning; specialty dry cleaning; drying; pressing; steaming; tailoring; stain treatment; condition inspection; quality control; folding; packaging; linen processing; chemical control; equipment maintenance; staff support; and rolling-rack staging.

Freight lifts connect it to Anchor A, guest housing, kitchens, fitness, staff operations, and active apparel overflow.

## Ordinary life rule

The Estate's scale does not eliminate ordinary domestic experience. People still cook, lose things, need coffee, change shoes, host relatives, read, watch films, play games, do laundry, and move from one room to another. Infrastructure makes those experiences possible; it does not replace them with summary.
