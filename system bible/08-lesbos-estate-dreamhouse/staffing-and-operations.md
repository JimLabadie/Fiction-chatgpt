Authority: CONTROLLING ROLE AND OPERATING-COVERAGE RECORD

## Workforce principle

Estate and Anchor B roles are filled by women who are lesbian or transgender; a woman need not be both. This is a deliberate dream-world demographic premise, not a claim about real workforce demographics or universal personality.

Role tendencies may supply cultural texture, but no job is biologically or morally assigned by presentation. Grounds, security, care, culinary, museum, technical, beauty, and leadership work belong to the people trained and hired to perform it.

## Availability is not continuous labor

The Estate operates at all hours without requiring any employee to work all hours.

- Security and other continuous functions use staffed shifts.
- On-call roles receive standby compensation and defined response windows.
- Retained professionals receive retainers plus service fees where appropriate.
- Event staff activate for actual events.
- Headcount scales when residents, children, collections, facilities, or programs activate.
- Housing is a benefit and option where appropriate, not a mechanism of control.

The salary figures below are a dated planning model. Counts and roles are the reusable baseline; compensation may be updated without rewriting the institution.

## Headcount and cost baseline

The older complete operating model produces approximately 60–70 full-time core Estate roles plus retained and on-call professionals. Its dated salary model estimates approximately $5.2–$6 million in base salaries and roughly $6.5–$7.5 million annually after ordinary benefits and employment cost.

The earlier fashion-museum-only model estimated another 16–19 positions and approximately $1.1–$1.6 million in salaries. Mnemosyne is broader than that museum, so its role table above replaces the old label; exact current payroll depends on activation, programming, and overlap with Estate support.

These figures are operating-scale checks, not immutable economic canon. They may be updated for a story date without removing the roles or pretending the work happens by itself.

## Myrina boundary

[Myrina and Lesbian NORAD](family-concierge-lesbian-norad.md) coordinates family requests, travel, appointments, gifts, events, and cross-property logistics. It does not absorb the Estate Manager, curators, chefs, security officers, housekeepers, engineers, pilots, artists, or other specialists into an anonymous command system.

Myrina may coordinate; the responsible department executes.
