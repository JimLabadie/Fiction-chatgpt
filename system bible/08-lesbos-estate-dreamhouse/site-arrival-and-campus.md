# Lesbos Site Arrival and Campus

Status: DETAILED REUSABLE BASELINE
Authority: CONTROLLING SITE GROUNDS OUTBUILDINGS AND EXTERNAL OPERATIONS RECORD

## Site and perimeter

The 42.5-acre former Galleria property is enclosed as a high-security private campus. A continuous perimeter wall works with wooded greenbelts, landscaped berms, controlled service gates, mature oaks, and dense screening rather than reading as an exposed institutional fence.

The primary entrance uses a limestone-clad security pavilion with protected glazing, biometric vehicle recognition, visitor screening, and controlled entry. The public-facing pavilion is distinct from the rear security operations building.

Beyond the gate, a half-mile maple-lined drive curves through restored parkland and withholds the main residence until the final approach.

## Dreamhouse arrival court

The former galleria's principal entrance is rebuilt as a full-scale architectural interpretation of the 2021 Barbie Dreamhouse. Magenta, hot pink, teal, crisp white, and rose-gold accents sit against durable commercial construction.

Water, lawn, and sculptural elements mount to a heavy track-and-lock system so the plaza can be rearranged. The arrival sequence may change without rebuilding the court. A transparent wheelchair-accessible elevator tower serves as the central landmark and gives direct access to the residential levels. High-gloss weather-resistant finishes and screened remnants of the mall prevent the entrance from reading as an abandoned retail conversion.

Fortress-grade security remains present but visually subordinate to the joyful arrival.

## Landscape and outdoor recreation

Approximately 18 acres are developed as landscaped recreation and garden zones. Approximately 22 acres of former parking are selectively reclaimed; these figures overlap in site-planning function and should not be added as separate land parcels until the acreage diagram is finalized.

The site contains:

- a regulation-size soccer and multisport turf field;
- a competition-grade heated infinity pool with cabanas;
- an organic greenhouse;
- a farm-to-table production garden;
- ornamental gardens, walking paths, and naturalized woodland buffers;
- private terraces and family garden areas;
- protected outdoor play yards and sensory gardens for the education center.

Useful drainage, utility corridors, service pavement, and structurally sound paved areas remain. Removed paving is recycled as aggregate. The visible character becomes layered parkland rather than a sea of parking.

### Fitness Aquatics and Wellness Center

The main fitness floor uses generous equipment spacing, premium strength circuits, Olympic lifting platforms, connected cardio equipment, sound-isolated cycling, hot yoga, Pilates reformers, and a 60-foot indoor sprint/performance track for sleds, ropes, and functional training.

The aquatics wing contains a three- to four-lane 25-meter indoor saltwater lap pool, acoustic ceiling treatment, a large hot whirlpool, a zero-edge ambient pool, and a coeducational hydrotherapy circuit.

Executive dressing rooms provide digital lockers, private rainfall showers, illuminated vanities, skincare amenities, and workout-gear laundry. Recovery spaces include massage, facial/aesthetic, and physical-therapy rooms; multi-temperature cold plunges; infrared and Finnish saunas; quiet lounges; and professionally supervised hyperbaric oxygen rooms.

The hospitality hub includes a hotel-style lobby and concierge, valet interface, towels, biometric access, an organic fuel bar with juices and tailored meals, a Wi-Fi coworking lounge, and supervised childcare. Equipment planning uses Technogym Artis, Eleiko Prestera, and Woodway 4Sight as the performance level and functional reference rather than requiring those brands forever.

## Campus boundaries

- The education center is permanent Estate infrastructure; its current enrollment is story state.
- Staff housing provides independence and privacy; it does not imply that every employee lives on-site.
- The wealth headquarters controls physical office and vault facilities, not financial canon.
- The motor court establishes capacity and services, not a complete fleet inventory.
- Cassandra Labs has a detailed Estate R&D-building program. Its precise size and whether the Cassandra component is active in a given reusable configuration remain open; selecting it does not authorize invention of additional laboratories beyond the recorded program.
- Aviation and watercraft operations may use Estate support, but the complete fleet and remote facilities require their own controlling ledgers.
