## Identity

The family has a dedicated concierge and logistical command department called **Myrina** or **Lesbian NORAD**.

Its established interchangeable names are **Myrina** and **Lesbian NORAD**. Lesbian NORAD is not merely an out-of-world joke or development shorthand. Myrina is not a formal name used to conceal an embarrassing nickname. Both are actual names for the department and may be used naturally by family, staff, and narration.

**Myrina names the department as a group and institution**, analogous to Cassandra Labs. It is not the name of a woman, commander, AI, goddess-avatar, or personified system. Individual women may lead or work within Myrina, but they require their own identities and must not be silently collapsed into the organization's mythological name.

The Greek name follows the wider function-derived naming method:

- Mnemosyne relates to memory.
- CLIO/Clio relates to history and record.
- Myrina expresses Amazon command, military coordination, capable women in important commands, and the department's relationship to Lesbos.

Myrina was surfaced from Jim's own `Greek Women in Mythology Table`, where she appears as an Amazon queen associated with the conquest of Libya. Follow-up review of the Diodorus tradition supplied the decisive functional fit: Myrina assembles and commands a large coordinated force, negotiates alliances, selects sites and founds cities, names cities after women holding important commands, takes Lesbos, and founds Mytilene with a name honoring her sister.

This selection demonstrates both source recovery and collaboration: Jim's prior rabbit-hole list successfully surfaced the woman, while discussion and focused research established why she belongs.

## Function

The department coordinates the family's complex personal and household logistics. Its governing comic premise is that it treats family whims with military precision **as if lives depend on successful execution**.

Potential operational subjects may include travel, aircraft, vehicles, reservations, appointments, wardrobes, gifts, events, property coordination, deliveries, scheduling, and urgent family requests. This list defines the domain broadly; it is not a finalized catalog or authority to invent detailed systems without discussion.

## Culture and agency

The women of Lesbian NORAD are not anonymous servants, interchangeable operators, or invisible extensions of Jim or Athena.

They know their ruthless dedication and efficiency are respected. They take professional pride in converting absurd or difficult requests into flawless operations. Their judgment, coordination, anticipation, and execution belong to them.

Apply **Visa**: the responsible women receive credit for their intelligence, decisions, and work.

Apply **Pencil** inside the organization: competent specialists perform routine duties without requiring every intermediate action to be separately designed or narrated.

Apply **Rabbit Hole** to the organization itself: the command-center excess exists because it is delightful.

The humor comes from institutional seriousness applied to disproportionate family needs:

- a minor personal preference may be handled with the gravity of a continental defense alert;
- an aircraft repositioning, salon appointment, dinner reservation, or forgotten gift may enter the same terrifyingly competent operational picture;
- the department's seriousness is not incompetence, parody, or humiliation—it is the source of its excellence.

The staff are in on the identity. The joke does not diminish them.
