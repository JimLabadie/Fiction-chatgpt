## Dressing rooms and retrieval

### MC dressing room

The MC's dressing room contains twenty walk-in closets, each with three slide-out storage units and categories that can evolve with the collection. A standing dressmaker's-form mannequin provides a visible staging point. A secure biometric vault joins the fine-jewelry and Footwear Vault collections.

The Fashion Empire's product-copy rule operates here: every applicable product released by a holding produces a copy in the MC's current size. The copy may enter active wardrobe, fitting, alteration, conservation, underground storage, or display according to its state. It does not require the MC to shop or place an order.

### Partner dressing room

The connected partner dressing room is a shared household wardrobe environment for the thirteen partners, with thirteen walk-in closet stations and thirteen standing dressmaker's-form mannequins. Personal suites still have ordinary closets; the shared room exists for the deep wardrobe and retrieval ecosystem.

Six mobile retrieval robots dock in wall alcoves. Each has a warehouse-style drive base, telescoping vertical reach, and articulated arms and hands appropriate to garment handling. They travel between the dressing rooms and the B1/B2 wardrobe areas through the private lift network. The robots are docked out of sight when idle; the mannequins remain ordinary visible fixtures.

### Private delivery connection

A discreet lift connects the MC dressing room, partner dressing room, Grand Wardrobe, B1 active inventory, and B2 long-term storage. Valuable or archived items can reach a fitting room without traveling through public or guest circulation.

## CLIO wardrobe and collection behavior

CLIO is the unified object, wardrobe, display, conservation, and logistics system that replaces the older WIS name. It does not create separate collections merely because objects occupy different rooms.

CLIO records at least:

- identity and description;
- owner;
- size and fitting state;
- current location;
- active wardrobe, storage, conservation, loan, display, staging, or transit state;
- condition and care requirements;
- access permissions;
- retrieval and delivery status;
- provenance and historical significance where applicable.

The MC and committed partners have reciprocal household access through tablets and appropriate interfaces. Ownership is recorded but is not automatically the access boundary. Staff receive only the access required for their jobs. A garment moving between a closet, B1, B2, a fitting room, and Mnemosyne remains the same object.

CLIO is logistics and recordkeeping, not an independent intelligence and not Athena.

It supports routine household medicine, recovery, stabilization, and privacy. It does not replace a hospital for care requiring hospital resources.

## Footwear and jewelry vault

The secure vault is physically inside the MC dressing room and shares security with fine jewelry. Its twenty-piece museum-grade footwear core and approximately $82-million working total are controlled by [Footwear Accessories Jewelry and Beauty](../10-footwear-accessories-jewelry-and-beauty.md).

Objects may be displayed at Mnemosyne and later return to the dressing room. Conservation staff may advise against use; they do not become the owner. If the MC chooses to wear an owned piece, ownership and agency control.

## Privacy and access boundaries

- Residential technology may support comfort, safety, retrieval, and preparation without turning the household into a public demonstration environment.
- Biometric security protects high-value and private areas; it does not grant Athena ownership or human authority.
- Personal suites remain private even within a highly connected household.
- A partner's access to shared wardrobe infrastructure does not erase ownership records, bodily autonomy, or personal boundaries.
