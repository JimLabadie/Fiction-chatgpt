# Regency Hill — Reusable World Bible

Status: ESTABLISHED REUSABLE SETTING — DELIBERATELY LIGHTWEIGHT

## Purpose and scope
Regency Hill is a reusable neighborhood setting in the same city as St. Claire. It exists to provide a stable nearby upper-class environment when a story needs one without requiring the neighborhood to be built into a dense standalone world.

**Pencil governs this setting.** Regency Hill should remain lightly specified unless a later story actually needs another durable detail. Do not pre-populate streets, businesses, institutions, residents, history, civic machinery, or neighborhood lore merely because a world bible exists.

## Established identity
- **Name:** Regency Hill.
- **Type:** Urban neighborhood.
- **Class/economic register:** Lower end of upper class — affluent, polished, expensive, and visibly comfortable without requiring every resident, business, or visitor to be ultra-wealthy.
- **Geographic relationship:** In the same city as St. Claire and directly borders St. Claire.
- **Jurisdictional/cultural distinction:** Regency Hill is outside St. Claire. Proximity does not confer St. Claire residency, membership, business status, community rules, or other St. Claire bindings.

## General texture
Regency Hill can plausibly support attractive walkable commercial frontage and expensive independent retail alongside its affluent residential character. This is a broad environmental register, not permission to invent a complete commercial district or fixed architectural history.

## Established business

### Maison Belle
- **Type:** Upscale independent women's clothing boutique.
- **Name intent:** French-adjacent, elegant, legible, and deliberately uncomplicated.
- **Location:** Regency Hill, on the commercial block used by the opening of *Rich Menace, Paris Edition*.
- **Exterior relationship:** A public bus stop is immediately outside or directly adjacent to the boutique, allowing someone waiting for the bus to stand in front of the shop.
- **Market register:** Expensive/upscale independent retail appropriate to Regency Hill. Exact brands, inventory, ownership, interior, hours, and staffing are not yet established.
- **Reuse rule:** Maison Belle may recur if useful, but its existence does not imply a larger boutique network or fashion district.

## Current story binding — Rich Menace, Paris Edition
The opening encounter occurs on a weekday in late afternoon/early evening outside Maison Belle. Tim Johnson is waiting for the bus because his car died and is being repaired. He has his laptop backpack and is dressed in deliberately ordinary everyday clothes: jeans, a plain T-shirt, a light casual jacket, and ordinary sneakers. Allison Kensington approaches in an exceptionally polished, expensive fitted skirt-suit look with a blouse, restrained expensive jewelry, immaculate hair and makeup, and spectacular high stilettos worth thousands of dollars. Exact brands and colors are not established.

This story binding records the opening-scene use of Regency Hill; it does not make Tim or Allison a Regency Hill resident.

## Intentionally unresolved
Unless a future story makes them consequential, leave unresolved:
- exact street names;

- detailed architecture;
- neighborhood history;
- government/civic structure;
- named residents;
- other businesses and institutions;
- Maison Belle ownership, staff, interior, brands, inventory, and operating schedule;
- Tim's or Allison's residence in relation to Regency Hill.

Do not build these out merely for completeness.
