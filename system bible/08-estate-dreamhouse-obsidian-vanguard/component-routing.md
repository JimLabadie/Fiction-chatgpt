# Estate Cross-Component Routing

Status: IN PROGRESS
Authority: RECOVERY ROUTING RECORD; NOT INDEPENDENT CANON

This file assigns mixed Estate material to an owning component. A route says where a fact must be reconciled; it does not automatically promote the fact or declare the destination complete.

## Routing principle

The Estate is a reusable physical component, not a monolithic universe.

A fact belongs in the Estate module when it defines the site's architecture, installed physical capacity, circulation, or a stable interface offered to another component. The Estate may point to a related component without importing that component's complete people, history, inventory, technology, ownership, or story state.

Relationship types:

- **Required:** the component cannot function without the dependency.
- **Optional integration:** available when both components are selected.
- **Physical host:** the Estate supplies space; the hosted component owns its program.
- **Reusable trigger:** the component defines repeatable trigger conditions and effects; each story records whether and when it fires.
- **Story-state activation:** records a particular story's occurrence or current phase without owning the reusable trigger definition.
- **Reference only:** useful provenance with no automatic activation.
- **Unresolved:** the evidence does not yet establish the relationship.

### Estate boundary

The older Whitfield Museum, fine-art gallery, Combined Showroom and Innovation Showroom are developmental ancestors or sources. They must not survive as automatically separate institutions merely because each once had its own file.

### Unresolved activation

The evidence establishes that Mnemosyne was developed for Anchor B. It does not yet settle whether:

1. every story selecting the Estate receives a fully operating Mnemosyne;
2. Anchor B is fixed but Mnemosyne is an optional compatible component;
3. Mnemosyne exists physically but its opening/operation is story-state dependent.

No default will be invented until Jim decides or the recovered record settles it.

## CLIO

- CLIO succeeds the WIS name.
- It has a tablet interface for the MC and partners.

## Athena

Athena is not merely a smart-building feature. She is the specific sentient AI/algorithm Jim created, with established history, personality, judgment, agency, family relationship, corporate activity, and development. Those facts require a reusable AI character/agent home if Athena is intended for reuse.

The Estate may define:

- compatible sensors, controls and system interfaces;
- which facilities she can operate when selected;
- an ordinary non-sentient fallback when she is not selected;
- physical locations relevant to her reveal or interaction, if a story chooses them.

The Athena/event-sequence component, rather than the Estate's architecture file, owns reusable reveal and authorization semantics. Individual stories own whether the MC has reached them and the characters' current emotional state.

Confirmed reusable timing:

- Athena's reveal is a reusable framework event whose occurrence is recorded in the story timeline.
- Project Artemis is triggered **one day after Athena's reveal**, after the MC has embraced the reality of the world.
- Athena presents the pending commercial launch and asks the MC to authorize it instead of acting unilaterally.
- The exact dialogue and emotional reactions remain story prose/state; the sequence, delay, and authorization requirement are framework canon.

## Objects and collections

Module 10 currently owns physical objects, storage, collection state, grooming services and beauty workflows. Estate owns placement. Module 16 owns engineering. Module 12 owns commercial obligations.

Required separation:

- **Object exists** is different from **object was researched**.
- **Owned** is different from **desired**, **targeted**, **loaned** or **proposed**.
- **Canonical home** is different from **current location**.
- **Displayed** is a state, not a surrender of ownership.
- **Museum-grade** does not automatically mean unworn.
- A named real-world object in a research list is not automatically present in the reusable framework or every story.
- Story acquisition and use events update that story's state; they do not silently rewrite the reusable baseline.

- current partner wardrobes and who brought what from staff housing;

This exclusion is routing, not deletion. Story material belongs in its own Bible, timeline and current-state records.
