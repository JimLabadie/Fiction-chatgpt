# Estate Cross-Component Routing

Status: IN PROGRESS
Authority: RECOVERY ROUTING RECORD; NOT INDEPENDENT CANON

This file assigns mixed Estate material to an owning component. A route says where a fact must be reconciled; it does not automatically promote the fact or declare the destination complete.

## Routing principle

The Estate is a reusable physical component, not a monolithic universe.

A fact belongs in the Estate module when it defines the site's architecture, installed physical capacity, circulation, or a stable interface offered to another component. The Estate may point to a related component without importing that component's complete people, history, inventory, technology, ownership, or story state.

Relationship types:

- **Required:** the component cannot function without the dependency.
- **Optional integration:** available when both components are selected.
- **Physical host:** the Estate supplies space; the hosted component owns its program.
- **Story-state activation:** exists or changes only after an event in one story.
- **Reference only:** useful provenance with no automatic activation.
- **Unresolved:** the evidence does not yet establish the relationship.

## Mnemosyne

### Owning scope

A Mnemosyne component should own:

- institutional mission and identity;
- chronological Core Gallery;
- changing and experimental exhibitions;
- Future/emerging-maker program;
- working making and demonstration environments;
- visible conservation and study functions;
- partner/subsidiary recognition displays;
- established and emerging designer programs;
- Soapbox/debate installations and their online relationship;
- Aglaea and footwear-display program;
- curatorial substitution rules;
- public, invitation, security, education and research operations;
- relationship with Erato.

### Estate boundary

The Estate owns Anchor B's approximately 125,000-square-foot, two-floor shell, independent public arrival, controlled Estate connection, passenger/freight circulation, receiving, and B1 support connection.

Mnemosyne owns what happens inside that shell.

The older Whitfield Museum, fine-art gallery, Combined Showroom and Innovation Showroom are developmental ancestors or sources. They must not survive as automatically separate institutions merely because each once had its own file.

### Unresolved activation

The evidence establishes that Mnemosyne was developed for Anchor B. It does not yet settle whether:

1. every story selecting the Estate receives a fully operating Mnemosyne;
2. Anchor B is fixed but Mnemosyne is an optional compatible component;
3. Mnemosyne exists physically but its opening/operation is story-state dependent.

No default will be invented until Jim decides or the recovered record settles it.

## CLIO

### Recovered functional baseline

Later direct corrections establish:

- CLIO succeeds the WIS name.
- It has a tablet interface for the MC and partners.
- Wardrobe, vault, storage, display, conservation and other locations form one material ecosystem rather than separate collections.
- Ownership remains attributable, but ownership is not automatically an access wall within the consenting household.
- Access is reciprocal: partners can request items available in their size, and their items can likewise be available to the MC.
- An object's location or current state distinguishes closet, vault, display, storage, conservation, loan, transit or current wear.
- Display does not permanently convert a wearable object into an untouchable museum object.
- If a displayed wearable is selected for use, appropriate staff handling occurs and a comparable piece can assume its curatorial role.
- Athena can know location and state through the system rather than through unexplained omniscience.

### Routing boundary

CLIO should not be reduced to Estate automation or museum inventory. It is a reusable system candidate spanning personal wardrobe, collection management, conservation, display, logistics and authorized household access.

The Estate records only its physical endpoints and service paths. Module 10 owns item-level states unless a standalone CLIO component becomes their controlling home. Module 16 supplies technical guardrails and engineering references without redefining household access or ownership.

Invented database fields from the prior assistant remain proposals unless supported by source or approved by Jim.

## Athena

Athena is not merely a smart-building feature. Recovered material attributes identity, history, personality, judgment, agency, family relationship, corporate activity and development to her. Those facts require a reusable character/agent home if Athena is intended for reuse.

The Estate may define:

- compatible sensors, controls and system interfaces;
- which facilities she can operate when selected;
- an ordinary non-sentient fallback when she is not selected;
- physical locations relevant to her reveal or interaction, if a story chooses them.

The Estate must not define as universal:

- when she reveals herself;
- whether the current MC knows she exists;
- whether she calls the MC “Mom” at a given point;
- her current authorization relationship;
- Project Artemis timing;
- the current emotional state of either character.

Those are story state.

## Objects and collections

Module 10 currently owns physical objects, storage, collection state, grooming services and beauty workflows. Estate owns placement. Module 16 owns engineering. Module 12 owns commercial obligations.

Required separation:

- **Object exists** is different from **object was researched**.
- **Owned** is different from **desired**, **targeted**, **loaned** or **proposed**.
- **Canonical home** is different from **current location**.
- **Displayed** is a state, not a surrender of ownership.
- **Museum-grade** does not automatically mean unworn.
- A named real-world object in a research list is not automatically present in the reusable framework or every story.
- Story acquisition and use events update that story's state; they do not silently rewrite the reusable baseline.

This exclusion is routing, not deletion. Story material belongs in its own Bible, timeline and current-state records.
