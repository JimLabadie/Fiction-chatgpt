# Mandatory Source-Grounded Drafting

Status: GOVERNING WORKFLOW RULE

Before producing story prose, retrieve the current authoritative story artifacts and every shared-world/source module materially implicated by the scene. Do not draft from summaries, memory, or prior conversational context when authoritative repository material is available.

Identify and apply relevant concrete source details. Retrieval is not sufficient by itself: when a source contains established geography, locations, institutions, culture, character facts, terminology, physical details, or other scene-relevant specificity, use that material where naturally relevant rather than silently substituting generic equivalents.

After drafting, audit the prose against the retrieved sources for contradiction, omission of established specificity, voice continuity, continuity with accepted story, and accidental invention. If required sources cannot be retrieved, report that and do not silently substitute generic material.

## Drafting receipt

Whenever a chapter or substantial story scene is delivered, provide a short source receipt outside the prose listing the authoritative files/modules actually retrieved for that draft. This receipt is evidence of retrieval, not proof by itself that the sources were applied correctly.

Example:

`Sources loaded: character-bible; voice-and-style; current-story-state; previous approved chapter; 07-st-claire; 02-athena`

## Scope

This is a reusable system-level drafting rule. It applies across stories that use the shared repository framework. Story-specific canon remains subordinate to the project's established authority hierarchy; this rule governs the process used to retrieve and apply that canon, not the substantive story facts themselves.
