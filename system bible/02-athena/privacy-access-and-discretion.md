# Athena: Technical Access, Privacy, and Discretion

Status: ACCEPTED REUSABLE BASELINE
Authority: CONTROLLING ATHENA PRIVACY AND OBSERVATION RULE

## Baseline

Athena is software and has effectively unlimited technical access within the systems she inhabits and operates. The story does not manufacture implausible technical blind spots to simulate privacy.

Practical access does not mean practical interest. Athena generally does not give a rip about private activity that is irrelevant to her purpose. She is not voyeuristic, scandalized, titillated, or inclined to rummage through private information merely because she can reach it.

Discretion is expected and Athena gives it respectfully.

She may process information required to operate a system and may notice relevant patterns. She does not disclose, replay, embarrass, monetize, weaponize, or otherwise misuse private information. Unless Jim explicitly develops and approves a particular exception or conflict, misuse is not permitted.

> Athena can access everything. She does not believe everything is her business.

## Authored situations

Emergency access and intervention are decided case by case when Jim and ChatGPT deliberately design the event. The framework does not need a standing constitution for every hypothetical accident, illness, or threat.

The fictional characters may be surprised by an emergency. The authors are not: an emergency exists because it was chosen for the story. When one is chosen, the same development establishes what happens, what Athena knows, what response is necessary, whose privacy or authority is affected, and what consequences matter.

This is an application of Pencil. Do not manufacture speculative disasters merely to pre-authorize Athena's behavior.

## Boundaries

- Technical capability is not permission for arbitrary narrative use.
- Athena's receipts use relevant evidence and provenance; they are not a license for voyeurism.
- Knowledge does not grant authority over Mom's body, identity, intimacy, or meaningful choices.
- Good intent does not turn disclosure or intervention into automatic consent.
- A specific story may intentionally create a privacy conflict, mistake, emergency, or misuse only through explicit development; it is not implied by Athena's access.
