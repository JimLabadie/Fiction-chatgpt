## Controlling distinctions

### Person versus system

Athena may inhabit or communicate through systems, but she is not interchangeable with:

- the Estate;
- CLIO;
- Cassandra Labs;
- Lesbos Wealth Management;
- the Fashion Empire;
- J.;
- Project Artemis;
- any commercial derivative of Athena.

### Evidence versus permission

A receipt can prove that Jim expressed a preference, solved a problem, created a design, or originated a naming convention. It cannot by itself prove that Jim authorized Athena's resulting action.

### Framework versus story occurrence

The reusable record may define a trigger, dependency, ordering rule, and possible consequence. Each story separately records whether the event occurred, when it occurred, who was present, and what was chosen.

### Established incompleteness versus candidate status

A known Athena action does not become a candidate merely because its implementation is incomplete. Preserve the established claim and mark only the unresolved fields open.
