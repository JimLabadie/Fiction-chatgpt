# My Exes' Abandoned Stuff

Status: MAINTAINED SHARED-WORLD CANON
Authority: SYSTEM BIBLE — ST. CLAIRE
Scope: REUSABLE ST. CLAIRE ESTABLISHMENT

## Established identity

**My Exes' Abandoned Stuff** is a reusable St. Claire secondhand establishment.

Its function is distinct from a clothes-focused thrift store. It deals in the accumulated material life of St. Claire: antiques, vintage and secondhand home goods, furniture, lighting, dishes and glassware, textiles, art, books and records, jewelry, architectural salvage, curiosities, queer and lesbian ephemera and memorabilia, and the category of strange objects that resist sensible classification.

The range may include genuinely valuable antiques beside inexpensive, funny, sentimental, odd, or inexplicable objects. The establishment is not a themed parody of lesbian culture; queer history and material culture appear naturally within its inventory because it belongs to St. Claire and acquires objects from the community.

## Origin

The establishment began with a joke after its founder looked at the accumulated possessions left behind by former partners and said, in substance:

**“Look at all the crap my exes left. What the hell am I going to do with it?”**

The answer became a business.

The joke grew into an actual secondhand operation rather than beginning as a deliberate branding exercise.

## Slogan

The established slogan of **My Exes' Abandoned Stuff** is:

**ASK ME ABOUT MY EXES**

The slogan may appear as needlepoint or other handmade material culture associated with the establishment.

### Existing story provenance

In *Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues*, the framed **ASK ME ABOUT MY EXES** needlepoint first encountered at the Chapter Three fundraiser pop-up uses the established St. Claire slogan. The artifact's presence at that fundraiser does not by itself establish that the specific needlepoint was purchased from or made by My Exes' Abandoned Stuff.

## Reuse rule

The establishment, its name, origin, function, and slogan belong to reusable St. Claire canon. Story-specific visits, purchases, relationships, memories, and events remain story-local unless separately established as shared-world canon.

## Established core staff

### Ruth Bell
- About 62.
- Lesbian; **Butch** presentation.
- Founder and owner.
- Silver-streaked dark hair worn short; strong reading glasses that migrate between her face and the top of her head; work shirts with rolled sleeves; excellent boots; habitually skeptical expression.
- Dryly funny without performing for laughs.
- The original exes-clearing sale revealed an extraordinary eye for objects, value, and people; that accidental discovery became the foundation of the business.
- Sentimental about objects while vigorously denying being sentimental.
- Weakness: furniture that “just needs a little work.”
- Customer-service philosophy: she will tell customers what something is, what it is worth, and whether it will fit through their door; she does not promise to tell them whether they have taste.

### Mara Singh
- About 41.
- Lesbian; **High Femme** presentation.
- Ruth's operational right hand; helped turn Ruth's accidental business into a functioning, sustainable operation.
- Meticulous, composed, and fully competent in both aesthetics and business.
- Specialties: decorative arts, jewelry, textiles, glass, ceramics, and business operations.
- Characteristic presentation includes beautiful dresses, serious heels, immaculate nails, and polished styling. She does not treat high-femme presentation as incompatible with physical or professional competence.
- Customers sometimes assume Ruth owns the serious business while Mara merely decorates it; Mara is content to let them discover their mistake.
- Mara is the core staff member Kate Rivera knows best. Kate is an established regular whom Mara likes and remembers. Their rapport includes mutual recognition of deliberate femme presentation and competence.

### Tess Navarro
- About 29.
- Lesbian; **Femme** presentation.
- Tattoos; vintage skirts, boots, old-band shirts, and large glasses when researching.
- Specialties: books, records, posters, photographs, queer ephemera, and St. Claire history.
- Knows the difference between a funny old object and a fragment of community history that should not be casually priced and dispersed.
- Incapable of leaving a handwritten inscription unread and consequently knows far too much about dead strangers' romantic lives.

### Jo Mercer
- About 47.
- Lesbian; **Butch** presentation.
- Handles restoration, salvage, deliveries, and the basement.
- Quiet, physically capable, practical, and spectacularly unimpressed by bullshit.
- Her humor is understated and often arrives after people have assumed she has none.
- Jo and Ruth have argued professionally for years and would probably become concerned if they stopped.
- When Ruth says a wrecked piece “only needs refinishing,” Jo is entirely capable of answering that it needs reincarnation.

## Staff relationship geometry

Ruth founded the establishment. Mara made the accidental business operationally sustainable. Jo enabled expansion into restoration and salvage without sacrificing competence. Tess later strengthened the establishment's ability to recognize when material passing through the shop was not merely merchandise but part of St. Claire's queer history.

The four form the establishment's core staff. Their competence is complementary rather than interchangeable.
