# My Exes' Abandoned Stuff

Status: MAINTAINED SHARED-WORLD CANON
Authority: SYSTEM BIBLE — ST. CLAIRE
Scope: REUSABLE ST. CLAIRE ESTABLISHMENT

## Established identity

**My Exes' Abandoned Stuff** is a reusable St. Claire secondhand establishment.

Its function is distinct from a clothes-focused thrift store. It deals in the accumulated material life of St. Claire: antiques, vintage and secondhand home goods, furniture, lighting, dishes and glassware, textiles, art, books and records, jewelry, architectural salvage, curiosities, queer and lesbian ephemera and memorabilia, and the category of strange objects that resist sensible classification.

The range may include genuinely valuable antiques beside inexpensive, funny, sentimental, odd, or inexplicable objects. The establishment is not a themed parody of lesbian culture; queer history and material culture appear naturally within its inventory because it belongs to St. Claire and acquires objects from the community.

## Origin

The establishment began with a joke after its founder looked at the accumulated possessions left behind by former partners and said, in substance:

**“Look at all the crap my exes left. What the hell am I going to do with it?”**

The answer became a business.

The joke grew into an actual secondhand operation rather than beginning as a deliberate branding exercise.

## Slogan

The established slogan of **My Exes' Abandoned Stuff** is:

**ASK ME ABOUT MY EXES**

The slogan may appear as needlepoint or other handmade material culture associated with the establishment.

### Existing story provenance

In *Even Pretty Girls Get the Blues*, the framed **ASK ME ABOUT MY EXES** needlepoint first encountered at the Chapter Three fundraiser pop-up uses the established St. Claire slogan. The artifact's presence at that fundraiser does not by itself establish that the specific needlepoint was purchased from or made by My Exes' Abandoned Stuff.

## Reuse rule

The establishment, its name, origin, function, and slogan belong to reusable St. Claire canon. Story-specific visits, purchases, relationships, memories, and events remain story-local unless separately established as shared-world canon.

Staff identities, staff characterization, exact physical layout, operating history beyond the established origin, and other detailed establishment mechanics remain to be developed and approved. Do not invent them as established facts during story use.
