# Adaptive Textile Research Division ("Living Fabric") — Product Manifest

Status: ACTIVE RECOVERED DESIGN DETAIL

(System Specification: Research Program LF-1, Early-Stage)

## System Environment & Core Parameters

- **System Environment:** A dedicated materials-science research program developing textiles with built-in adaptive properties, distinct from Threadbourne's manufacturing and retrieval product lines — this division produces fabric capabilities, not finished automated systems.
- **Target Scope:** Fabric-level solutions that reduce a garment's dependency on manual or robotic intervention for minor fit and condition changes.
- **Development Status:** Framed deliberately as an ongoing R&D frontier rather than a fully mature commercial line — individual capabilities below may be at different stages of development within any given story, from early prototype to limited deployment.

## Capability Catalog

### Self-Repairing Fiber Matrix
- **Function:** A fiber structure capable of mending minor tears, loose threads, and small snags without external intervention, gradually re-knitting compromised fibers back into a stable weave.
- **Scope of Repair:** Effective on minor everyday damage — a small snag, a loosened seam thread — not a substitute for structural repair of major damage.

### Climate-Responsive Weave
- **Function:** A weave structure that adjusts its own warmth and breathability in response to ambient temperature and the wearer's body heat, without requiring a separate heating or cooling element.
- **Range:** Operates across a moderate comfort range — noticeable adjustment between a cool interior and a warm outdoor environment, rather than extreme-condition performance.

### Stretch-Adaptive Fabric Membrane
- **Function:** A fabric membrane capable of accommodating minor day-to-day body changes — slight swelling, minor measurement fluctuation — by adjusting its own tension and give, without requiring a trip to the robotic tailoring units for small variations.
- **Boundary:** Intended for minor, temporary fluctuation only; a genuine dimensional change (such as one driven by the household's presentation-technology pods) still routes to AWS's robotic tailoring units for a proper, permanent re-fit rather than relying on the membrane's temporary give.
