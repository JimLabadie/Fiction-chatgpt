(System Specification: Model Variant TBH-1, Consumer Line)

## System Environment & Core Parameters

- **System Environment:** A compact, mass-market closet-organization and retrieval unit for ordinary residential use — the direct commercial descendant of the anchor's original private frustration with a missing watch, scaled down from the full Automated Wardrobe System.
- **Target Scope:** Item retrieval, organization, and basic outfit staging for an individual consumer's own wardrobe, installed within a standard residential closet footprint.
- **Installation Footprint:** Designed to retrofit into an existing closet space rather than requiring the dedicated architecture a full AWS installation needs.
- **Recovery/Downtime:** Not applicable; standard consumer-grade maintenance intervals apply.

## Stage One: Compact Retrieval

- **Simplified Retrieval Arm:** A single, smaller-scale articulated arm retrieves requested items from a home unit's own catalogued storage, using the same Discreet Asset Tag technology as the household-scale system, at consumer-appropriate cost.
- **App-Based Requests:** Requests are placed through a companion mobile app rather than a dedicated in-room tablet interface.

## Stage Two: Organization & Cataloguing

- **Automatic Inventory:** Newly added items are catalogued automatically on first placement into the unit, building a personal wardrobe inventory without manual data entry.
- **Wear-Pattern Tracking:** Usage frequency is logged the same way as the full-scale system, allowing the companion app to surface underused items or suggest outfit rotation.

## Stage Three: Basic Outfit Staging

- **Retrieval Without Full Dressing:** Unlike the full AWS, Threadbourne Home retrieves and stages requested items on a simple hanging rail rather than a fully articulated, self-dressing mannequin — keeping the consumer unit's cost and complexity well below the household-scale system.
- **Outfit Suggestions:** The companion app can suggest simple pairings based on cataloged inventory and past wear patterns.

## Explicitly Out of Scope (Consumer Line)

- No robotic tailoring capability — alterations remain the customer's own responsibility or a local tailor's.
- No mannequin-based full dressing or preview function.
- No integration with presentation-technology body-modeling data.
