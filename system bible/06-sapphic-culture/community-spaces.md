## Community Spaces & Institutions

Lesbian bars (a real, often-discussed phenomenon: the number of dedicated lesbian bars in the US has collapsed over decades, a fact the community itself talks about), softball leagues (a genuine long-running cultural institution, not just a joke), women's bookstores (mostly a historical/1970s-90s touchstone now), LGBT community centers, roller derby, Pride parades vs. dyke marches (the latter more explicitly political and grassroots, less corporate-sponsored), lesbian land / intentional separatist communities (1970s history), and online/app spaces (HER and Lex as sapphic-specific dating apps, distinct from generic swiping apps). Michigan Womyn's Music Festival is a real, useful, and genuinely contested touchstone — it ran 1976–2015 and became a flashpoint over trans exclusion; handle it as history with real weight, not trivia.
