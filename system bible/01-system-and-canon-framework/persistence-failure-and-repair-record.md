# Persistence Failure and Repair Record

Status: ACTIVE ACCOUNTABILITY RECORD — REPAIR IN PROGRESS
Authority: PROJECT GOVERNANCE AND RECOVERY EVIDENCE
Opened: 2026-09-13

## The failure

Jim repeatedly required that important project state not remain only in ChatGPT conversation context. Accepted decisions, pins, unresolved questions, source-reading results, and return obligations were supposed to become durable repository records capable of surviving a cold start.

That requirement was not followed reliably.

The repository acquired rules saying that pins and accepted material must be persisted, but no actual Open Questions and Development Ledger was created. Discussions were repeatedly described as `recorded`, `routed`, `checkpointed`, `codified`, or `preserved` when one of the following weaker conditions was true:

- the conversation remained visible to ChatGPT;
- a memory or inherited summary retained the gist;
- a raw conversation export existed;
- an original source file had been copied into the repository;
- a recovery ledger mentioned that the substantive component existed;
- a routing file named a future destination;
- a thin summary recorded a conclusion while omitting the underlying developed material.

None of those conditions proves that the substantive information reached its authoritative reusable record.

## What the 2026-09-13 check proved

1. No comprehensive pin, backlog, open-question, or development-ledger file existed.
2. The Squirrel protocol required a pin to preserve the idea, significance, and return point, but supplied no maintained destination for doing so.
3. Scattered `open fields`, `required continuation`, `next field-review order`, and component-gap lists existed, but they did not reconstruct the promised pin history.
4. The 26-page raw chat archive preserves earlier conversation evidence inside the repository, but transcript preservation is not decision propagation.
5. Several major components were present only as source documents, chat discussion, routing summaries, or statements that recovery remained necessary.
6. Recent Athena privacy/access and authored-event decisions had been discussed but not persisted.
7. After diagnosing these failures, ChatGPT initially made no repository change because it treated Jim's request to `discuss` as limiting the task to diagnosis. That repeated the exact failure being discussed by leaving the new findings in chat. Jim immediately corrected it and ordered the repair.

## Storage distinctions

The project recognizes four separate states:

| State | Meaning | Counts as substantively persisted? |
|---|---|---:|
| Live or summarized ChatGPT context | ChatGPT can currently discuss or recall the material | No |
| Raw source or conversation archive | Original evidence survives for later adjudication | No |
| Routing/audit mention | The repository acknowledges a component, omission, or intended destination | No |
| Controlling component | The accepted substantive state exists in its authoritative file, with provenance and open fields localized | Yes, after commit and fetch verification |

Raw evidence is essential, but it is evidence beneath the record rather than a substitute for the record.

## Enforcement repair — source-to-record-to-output fidelity

The project must not audit a downstream artifact only against a persistent record whose own fidelity to the upstream development has not been verified. That creates a circular audit in which material lost during persistence becomes invisible to later verification.

### Three independent gates

#### Gate A — Development-to-persistence fidelity

Whenever a working conversation, source review, or collaborative development session establishes, approves, corrects, rejects, defers, or materially refines project state, compare the **actual upstream material** against the proposed persistent destination before calling the work saved.

The comparison must preserve at full established resolution:

- exact decisions and corrections;
- causal and relational context needed to understand why a detail matters;
- setup/payoff relationships and intended reader knowledge;
- character interiority or scene function when those were part of the established beat;
- exact jokes, lines, objects, models, clothing details, gestures, examples, exceptions, and distinctions when established;
- status boundaries among approved, proposed, unresolved, rejected, and recovery evidence;
- named open fields and any explicit decision that an item remains unresolved.

A thinner umbrella statement is not proof of preservation. Examples of forbidden lossy substitutions include:

- an exact shoe make/model becoming only a brand or “heels”;
- “Mara previously teased Kate about a possible love interest and therefore recognizes Kate's deliberate courtship escalation” becoming only “Mara knows Kate”;
- an established “are you decorating one place or two?” payoff becoming only “they disagree about decorating”;
- a formative person's habits, influence, and emotional lineage becoming only a menu, nationality, relationship label, or bullet-point biography.

Before persistence is reported complete, every substantive upstream holding must have one explicit disposition: **persisted at full fidelity / already persisted at full fidelity / explicitly unresolved / explicitly deferred or pinned / conflicting and recorded as conflict / rejected by Jim**. No holding may disappear merely because it is inconvenient to schema or summary.

Verification for this gate must re-fetch the written destination and compare its contents to the upstream material. Confirming that a commit exists is necessary but not sufficient.

#### Gate B — Persistence-to-work-product fidelity

Only after Gate A has established that the controlling record faithfully represents the applicable upstream development may a draft, analysis, continuity result, handoff, framework artifact, or other downstream output be audited against that record.

For story prose, build a scene-specific application ledger before drafting. Each materially applicable established element must be represented with:

- source/destination;
- exact established detail or function;
- why it matters in this scene;
- required reader-visible or story-operational effect;
- planned scene location/beat;
- final disposition: **rendered / intentionally deferred under existing authority / not materially applicable / blocked**.

“Loaded,” “remembered,” “considered,” “alluded to,” or “captured the vibe” are not valid final dispositions.

The post-draft audit must inspect the **actual prose** and fail the draft when an applicable element has been omitted, genericized, compressed below its established resolution, left only in planning/interiority that never reaches the reader, or replaced by a genre/default equivalent.

#### Gate C — Voice and narrative-function fidelity

Factual coverage alone is insufficient. After Gate B, story prose must be tested separately against the controlling voice/style record and the established narrative function of the scene.

This gate asks whether the prose actually performs the developed work: setup/payoff, character-driven comic escalation, reader knowledge, interiority, physical embodiment, emotional lineage, environmental participation, tenderness, conflict, and other established functions. A checklist of nouns or plot events cannot satisfy a voice or narrative-function requirement.

A draft that contains all required objects but renders them as generic inventory, generic romance, generic banter, or generic genre staging fails this gate.

### Audit independence rule

A record may not certify its own completeness. When the question is whether persistence lost development, the audit source must include the upstream development/source material, not merely the current controlling record. When upstream evidence is inaccessible, say so and classify the persistence fidelity as unverified; do not infer completeness from the surviving record.

Likewise, a planning note cannot prove that a reader can perceive a beat. Reader-facing meaning must be verified in the manuscript itself when the story requires the reader to know or experience it.

### No ceremonial initialization

Retrieving the bootstrap, router, modules, story records, or source files is only initialization. It earns no compliance credit by itself. For every substantive task, compliance is established only by demonstrable application through the appropriate gate above.

A source receipt remains useful provenance, but it must never be presented as evidence that the source was faithfully applied.

### Persistence trigger and stop condition

When Jim approves or corrects a durable detail during active development, persistence is part of completing that work unless Jim explicitly says not to persist it yet. Before changing subjects or presenting the work as settled:

1. write the full-resolution material to the controlling destination;
2. re-fetch it;
3. run Gate A against the upstream development;
4. report the path and verified commit;
5. if any material cannot yet be propagated, record the exact unresolved/deferred holding in the controlling ledger before moving on.

If the current environment cannot access the upstream evidence needed for Gate A, do not claim synchronization or completeness.

### Failure reporting

When any gate fails, report the failure at the layer where it occurred:

- **persistence loss** — upstream development did not survive into the controlling record;
- **application loss** — controlling detail survived but did not survive into the work product;
- **reader-visibility loss** — intended meaning exists in notes/model reasoning but not on the page;
- **voice/function loss** — facts survived but the agreed narrative function or voice did not;
- **verification gap** — required upstream evidence was inaccessible, so fidelity could not be established.

Do not soften an omission into “undercommunicated” when the material is absent. Do not call a lost-established detail “unresolved” merely because the persistent record omitted it.
