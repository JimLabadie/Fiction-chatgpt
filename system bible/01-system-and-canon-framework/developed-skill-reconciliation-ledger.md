## Reconciliation procedure

For each family:

1. Read every packaged Markdown member completely.
2. Compare every variant at claim level; do not choose by filename.
3. Compare those claims with all current controlling System Bible records and later direct Jim decisions.
4. Classify each claim as accepted, superseded, conflicting, unclear, non-canon idea, or story-instance state.
5. Ask Jim about every unclear or conflicting claim before disposition.
6. Write accepted content into complete substantive System Bible destinations, not this ledger.
7. Record unresolved questions in the Open Questions and Development Ledger.
8. Check cross-component harmony and links.
9. Commit, fetch, and verify the published files before calling the family reconciled.
