# Character Record Template

Status: ACTIVE STRUCTURAL TEMPLATE
Scope: STORY-LOCAL SUBSTANTIAL / RECURRING CHARACTERS

## Purpose

Use this record when a character has enough established characterization that a compact Character Bible entry would lose meaningful detail. The Character Bible remains the story-level index and may contain compact entries for minor characters; substantial character records preserve developed characterization at the resolution at which it was established.

A template field is not permission to invent an answer. Use **Not yet established** when the repository and author have not established the value.

### Identity-resolution rule

The character identity key is the compound of **story namespace + canonical full name**. Resolve the active story namespace before resolving the character. Never merge or import character facts across story namespaces merely because names, nicknames, first names, roles, archetypes, or even full names match.

Within the active story, a unique contextual nickname, shorthand, misspelling, or retrieval alias may resolve to the established character without requiring Jim to reproduce canonical spelling. If more than one materially plausible story-local character remains, treat that as a genuine ambiguity rather than choosing.

## Maintenance rules

1. Preserve established detail; do not replace developed characterization with a shorter label or summary.
2. Organization must not change meaning.
3. Empty fields remain **Not yet established** rather than being filled by inference.
4. Implication is not establishment.
5. Update current state and development ledgers without rewriting the historical baseline.
6. A vibe/reference is not the characterization itself. Preserve the underlying established qualities.
7. Relationship records are directional and person-specific; do not assume reciprocity or copy one relationship onto another.
8. The Character Bible should link/index substantial character records rather than duplicating and later compressing them.
9. Resolve story namespace first. Cross-story name matches are never permission to merge or import facts.
10. Nicknames and retrieval aliases support natural conversation and discovery; only established names belong in canon/prose.
