# Project Purpose and Recovery Mandate

Status: ACTIVE PROJECT GOVERNANCE
Authority: JIM-APPROVED PURPOSE AND CURRENT-PHASE MANDATE
Approved: 2026-09-12

## Why the framework exists

This framework supports both Jim's private, personal fantasy fiction and commercial fiction for other readers. Both use the established house voice. The current story brief selects its audience and main character under [Persona, MC, and Commercial Story Binding](persona-mc-and-commercial-story-binding.md).

For personal reasons, Jim cannot live the lives available to his characters. The stories provide an imaginative way to experience those lives. The world is therefore closer to a persistent dream than a simulation of ordinary reality.

Reality is a constraint and a source of useful texture, not a requirement or creative authority. Impossible scale, wealth, technology, beauty, social structures, and coincidences are welcome when they serve the fantasy. Believability still matters, but it comes primarily from:

- internal consistency;
- emotional truth;
- understandable causes and consequences;
- characters with coherent desires and agency;
- physical and sensory detail;
- systems that behave consistently once established;
- enough real-world grounding to support immersion.

The framework must never sacrifice the desired fantasy merely to make it commercially plausible, ordinary, or realistic.

## Audience and value

For private persona fiction, the primary audience is Jim. For commercial fiction, the intended readership is set by the product brief. Jim remains the author, source of creative intent, and equal participant in development; the protagonist does not inherit his name.

The operational audience is ChatGPT in a cold-start conversation. The framework must let a new chat recover and use the world without depending on temporary conversation memory.

The original private-fiction purpose remains available. Publication, sale, public readership, and economic return are now supported when the author selects a commercial project. Commercial work preserves the voice, emotional specificity, and imaginative richness of the framework; it does not require importing the author's private biography. Progress is not measured by speed, word count, module count, or declarations of completion. It is measured by whether the recovered world is trustworthy, immersive, usable, and something Jim wants to inhabit through fiction.

## Creative partnership

Jim is a creative partner, not an autocrat approving choices generated elsewhere. ChatGPT is a creative partner, not an autonomous canon authority or a passive order-taker.

During later development, the normal collaboration cycle is:

1. Ask Jim about intent before framing an undefined creative question.
2. Listen before proposing a resolution.
3. Explore the subject together.
4. Contribute analysis, connections, consequences, and possibilities.
5. Preserve Jim's reactions, humor, corrections, reasoning, and unexpected connections as part of the development record.
6. Ask whether the discussion is settled enough to codify.
7. Persist the accepted result at the level of detail at which it was established; codification may organize it but must not compress away its reasoning, distinctions, conditions, examples, relationships, mechanisms, intent, or other established resolution.
8. Verify the persisted result against the accepted development before moving on.

Asking is an essential authorship step, not merely a fallback when data is missing. Jim's voice appears through the discussion itself and cannot be reconstructed completely from static rules.

## Current phase: recovery and reconstruction

Recovery remains an active workstream, but it does not prohibit author-requested story writing or commercial development. The recovery phase uses the surviving archive, imported skills, documents, spreadsheets, and recovered conversations to reconstruct durable framework states comparable in depth and function to what Jim reasonably believed had already been saved.

Exact lost wording may be impossible to reproduce. The required recovery target is the substantive state:

- decisions;
- relationships;
- functions;
- boundaries;
- histories;
- triggers;
- detailed content;
- creative reasoning and intent;
- known unresolved fields;
- provenance and version relationships.

### Recovery workflow

For each component:

1. Inventory its complete source family.
2. Identify evidence of exact duplicates, semantic duplicates, variants, evolutionary stages, and story contamination. These are analytical findings only; they do not authorize disposition.
3. Read the relevant archived-chat discussion as a controlling decision source.
4. Separate Jim's direct statements from assistant proposals.
5. Recover assistant contributions that Jim explicitly accepted, including acceptance expressed in an adjacent short reply.
6. Reconstruct the component at the fullest state the evidence supports.
8. Localize uncertainty to the exact disputed field or claim.
9. Ask Jim only when the available record cannot resolve a material contradiction.
10. Produce the actual persistent component artifact.
11. Commit the change and fetch the published result to verify it.
12. Complete the component's recovery before changing focus without a recorded reason.

During this phase, “ask first” prohibits invented resolutions. It does not require Jim to reconfirm facts the archive already establishes.

Recovery analysis may determine what the evidence shows, including relationships among sources. It may not decide what is done with the material. Removal, omission, descoping, downgrading, reclassification, merging, generalization, replacement, deletion, discarding, or declaring material superseded or redundant requires Jim's express disposition decision. When Jim already made that decision, execute it without requiring reconfirmation.

## Resolution before motion

There is no deadline that justifies moving on from an unresolved subject merely to demonstrate progress.

When a discussion begins, the preference is to resolve it, codify it, persist it, and verify it before changing focus. Productive rabbit holes are part of the creative method, but their results must be captured and their relationship to the active subject recorded.

An incomplete component remains usable at its established state. Missing fields do not push the entire component into purgatory.

## Candidate and unresolved material

A candidate is an active discussion obligation, not a rejection, optional-content bin, or place where unfinished work can be forgotten.

Every candidate must record:

- the actual proposed material;
- its source;
- why it matters;
- what is already established;
- the precise open question;
- affected components;
- the next discussion or recovery action.

Candidates remain visible until they are accepted, rejected, superseded, scope-separated, or explicitly deferred by Jim.

If the archive shows that a discussion was actually settled, the material must be restored as accepted content rather than left as a candidate merely because persistence failed.

## Decision authority during recovery

- Jim's direct statements and corrections control.
- Assistant text is a proposal, interpretation, acknowledgement, or recovery aid; it is not independent canon.
- An assistant contribution becomes accepted when Jim explicitly approves it, including through an unambiguous adjacent response.
- Lack of objection is not approval.
- A polished file, confident summary, successful upload, or filename containing `final` does not prove authority.
- Genuine contradictions are presented to Jim precisely; ChatGPT does not silently choose.
- Story-shaped material is not automatically story-local. A reusable trigger may be dramatized as a scene while remaining framework canon.

## Persistence and visible proof

A promise to create a file is not a file. A routing note is not a completed destination. A manifest naming an artifact is not the artifact.

Work counts as persisted only when:

1. the substantive content exists in the repository;
2. the relevant path is identified;
3. a Git commit records the change;
4. the published file is fetched and verified;
5. failures, partial results, and remaining work are reported honestly.

For pins and deliberate deferrals, the substantive content required at that stage is the current understanding, reason for deferral, exact return point, provenance, and affected components in the [Open Questions and Development Ledger](open-questions-and-development-ledger.md). A raw transcript or routing reference does not satisfy that requirement.

Visible commits, verification checks, audit ledgers, and explicit failures are part of the trust model. A failed operation must not be represented as completed work. Recovery status must describe what actually exists, not what ChatGPT intended to produce.

The words `saved`, `recorded`, `codified`, `checkpointed`, `persisted`, and `complete` require a named substantive repository path and a fetched, verified commit. If only a source, transcript, route, inventory entry, or missing-work notice exists, state that narrower fact instead.

## Sequence after recovery

Once the foundation has been reconstructed:

1. Jim and ChatGPT refine it together.
2. Components are tested individually and in selected combinations.
3. Gaps, conflicts, and weak transitions are discussed and repaired.
4. The framework is exercised through exploratory scenarios and play.
5. Story writing begins only when the selected foundation is sufficiently trustworthy and useful.

“Complete” is not the ultimate objective. The objective is a durable world that supports the fantasy, preserves Jim's voice and agency, and remains pleasurable to explore.
