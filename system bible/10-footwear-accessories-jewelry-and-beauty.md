## Footwear Vault

At Obsidian-Vanguard, the Footwear Vault shares a biometric vault with fine jewelry inside the Anchor Dressing Room and is keyed to the anchor. Its museum-grade core contains twenty named collector pieces valued at roughly $82 million. Leading pieces include Antonio Vietri Moon Star Shoes ($19.9M), Jada Dubai Passion Diamond Shoes ($17M), Debbie Wingham High Heels ($15.1M), Jada Dubai diamond stilettos ($12.8M), Harry Winston Ruby Slippers ($3M), and multiple Stuart Weitzman collector designs.

Museum value does not imply routine wear. The registry tracks identity, designer, category, size/fit, material, color, acquisition state, location, value basis, wearability, conservation needs, and story appearances. Duplicate CSV registries require row-level reconciliation before totals become final.

## Accessories jewelry and body art

Track ownership, location, contents, condition, history, and whether an object is everyday, archive, display, or loan. Corporate ownership of a brand does not give every character every product.

For jewelry, piercings, and tattoos record material, stone, placement, gauge, closure, fit, healing, aftercare, permanence, maker, meaning, and who chose it. Constellation designs may use white gold, chains, stars, violets, or Pleiades references when a specific design establishes them. A preview, proposal, or rejected scene does not establish completion.

## Beauty workflow

Track starting state, requested result, procedure, products or system used, time, reversibility, maintenance, physical sensation, and reaction. OAM may preview and perform hair, makeup, fragrance, piercing, tattoo, and presentation services only within file `16`'s technical and consent rules. Permanent regenerative hair restoration belongs to Evergreen, not an ordinary styling session.

## Collection states

Use one supported state per item: proposed, ordered, in transit, received, fitted, available, worn, stored, loaned, displayed, conserved, damaged, retired, or superseded. Do not move state without an event or source.
