## Origin

Techne exists because Metis Werks exists. Once MC commits to reopening that facility, the need for better manufacturing equipment becomes obvious. Athena identifies an existing struggling equipment manufacturer rather than proposing a new company from nothing. The developed financing model uses debt against the established empire and retains full ownership without outside equity. Techne occupies its own facility in the same broader industrial geography as Metis; exact placement is controlled by the later St. Claire integration and remains open. Unlike the earlier companies discovered through old workups, this acquisition is a deliberate, conscious decision made after MC understands what she is building. Established company-wide benefits extend to Techne's workforce when the acquisition closes.

## Corporate Mandate

Design, prototype, manufacture, and commercialize next-generation manufacturing equipment across the full range of categories Metis Werks operates in — textile machinery, apparel automation, footwear tooling, beauty/fragrance manufacturing equipment, and salon/spa equipment. Equipment is designed and prototyped on Techne's own top floor, manufactured at real industrial scale across the floors below, and sold or licensed commercially across the wider manufacturing industry — not limited to supplying Metis Werks' own needs.

## Recruitment Pattern

Techne's core leadership team (six named leads) follows the same anonymous pattern as Cassandra Labs — identified by Athena based on published engineering work, funded and contacted under the "J." identity, none of them having ever met the anchor or knowing who she actually is. This is a deliberate contrast: even though Techne itself is a conscious, named decision, the anchor's instinct to find brilliant people quietly and protect them from ever feeling personally watched or managed is the same instinct running underneath everything else in this portfolio. The building's broader manufacturing workforce (~3,900 across floors 1–22 and the sublevels) is hired openly, following the same demographic pattern established at Metis Werks (see `metis-werks.md`).

- **Salon/Spa Equipment Lab** — furniture/equipment fabrication bench, electronics integration bench.
- **Materials Science Bench** — fiber/fabric stress-testing, materials compatibility testing.

### Roof Level: Heliport
Positioned directly above Floor 23 — supports helicopter access, consistent with Techne's distance from both the estate and Metis Werks.

### Systems Integration
- **Freight & Conveyor Access:** Floor 23 connects directly into the building's own heavy-capacity freight elevator system, allowing a validated prototype to move straight down to any of floors 1–22 for production scale-up without leaving the building.
- **PLM/ERP Integration:** Techne's design output feeds directly into the same PLM and ERP systems used across the building, so a finished equipment design can move from Floor 23 into production-floor manufacturing without a separate handoff process.

- **Distinct from Cassandra Labs:** Cassandra Labs builds the anchor's own personal rant-derived patents. Techne builds industrial manufacturing equipment for Metis Werks and the wider industry. Different mission, same anonymous-recruitment pattern for its core leadership team only.
