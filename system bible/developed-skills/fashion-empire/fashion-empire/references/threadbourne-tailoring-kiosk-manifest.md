## System Environment & Core Parameters

- **System Environment:** A standalone, publicly accessible kiosk housing a scaled-down version of the household's own robotic tailoring technology, deployed in flagship retail locations.
- **Target Scope:** Instant, on-the-spot garment alteration for retail customers, without scheduling a separate tailor appointment or waiting days for completion.
- **Session Time:** Full measurement capture and alteration typically complete within 15–20 minutes per garment, depending on the extent of the requested change.
- **Recovery Period:** Not applicable — customer remains present and receives the altered garment at the end of the same session.

## Stage One: Instant Measurement Capture

- **Optical Body Scan:** A brief, fully clothed optical scan captures the precise measurements needed for the requested alteration, without physical measuring tape or direct contact.
- **Garment-Specific Mapping:** The system cross-references the scan against the specific garment's current construction to identify exactly what needs to change (hem length, waist take-in, sleeve length) rather than requiring the customer to specify technical alteration terms.

## Stage Two: Robotic Alteration Execution

- **In-Kiosk Alteration:** A compact version of Threadbourne's robotic tailoring mechanism executes the identified alteration directly within the kiosk — hemming, seam adjustment, and similar standard alterations — while the customer waits.
- **Alteration Scope:** Limited to standard, well-defined alterations suited to an unattended retail environment; complex structural changes remain outside the kiosk's scope and are referred to in-store human tailoring staff where available.

## Stage Three: Quality Verification

- **Automated Fit Check:** A follow-up optical scan confirms the alteration matches the intended specification before the garment is released to the customer.
- **Customer Confirmation:** The customer may request the garment be tried on and, if needed, submit a further minor adjustment request in the same session.

## Stage Four: Customer Pickup

- **Immediate Release:** The altered garment is returned to the customer at the end of the same visit, tagged and catalogued (see the Discreet Asset Tag Platform manifest) if part of a tracked retail inventory system.
