## System Environment & Core Parameters

- **System Environment:** A biological treatment protocol, not a robotic system — a series of scalp and follicle treatments administered through a dedicated treatment chair, distinct from OAM's automated hair-extension work.
- **Target Scope:** Genuine, permanent restoration of natural hair growth and thickness at the follicle level, as a slower but permanent alternative to OAM's instant, reversible extension-based solutions.
- **Treatment Timeline:** A multi-session protocol over several weeks, with visible natural regrowth beginning within the first two to three sessions — deliberately not instant, in contrast to the rest of this world's usual sci-fi immediacy, since the mechanism here is genuine biological regeneration rather than mechanical installation.
- **Permanence:** Unlike OAM's extension work, results are permanent rather than reversible — the wearer's own hair, restored, not a maintained addition.

## Stage One: Follicle Assessment

- **Scalp Mapping:** Detailed follicle-density and scalp-health scanning identifies areas suited to regenerative treatment versus areas requiring a different approach.
- **Biological Baseline:** A genetic and hormonal baseline assessment informs the specific regenerative formula used for that individual's treatment course.

## Stage Two: Regenerative Treatment

- **Follicle Stimulation:** Targeted regenerative compounds are administered directly to identified follicles across the treatment course, encouraging natural regrowth rather than simulating it.
- **Progressive Sessions:** Each session builds on the last, with density and growth monitored and the treatment formula adjusted as regrowth progresses.

## Stage Three: Integration Period

- **Natural Growth Cycle:** Because this is genuine biological regrowth rather than mechanical installation, the hair grows in following its own natural cycle and texture — indistinguishable from hair that was never treated at all, since it is, in fact, the wearer's own.
- **Maintenance:** Occasional follow-up sessions maintain density over time; no daily upkeep beyond ordinary hair care is required.

## Positioning Relative to OAM's Extension Work

The Perennial Restoration Program is deliberately marketed as a complement to, not a competitor with, OAM's instant extension technology — a woman wanting an immediate, dramatic change on a specific timeline still reaches for OAM; a woman wanting a permanent, natural change with no ongoing extension maintenance chooses this instead. A story can use the choice between the two as a small, meaningful character beat about patience versus immediacy.
