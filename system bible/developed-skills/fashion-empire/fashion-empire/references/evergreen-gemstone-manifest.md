## System Environment & Core Parameters

- **System Environment:** A controlled-growth laboratory facility producing fine gemstones and precious metals chemically and structurally identical to their mined equivalents, entirely outside any extraction supply chain.
- **Target Scope:** Full-spectrum gemstone and precious-metal production — diamonds, colored gemstones, and refined precious metals — suitable for museum-grade fine jewelry as well as the empire's broader commercial lines.
- **Growth Timeline:** Variable by stone size and type; a standard fine gemstone completes its growth cycle in a fraction of the geological timescale required for a mined equivalent, without any of the associated extraction footprint.
- **Authentication:** Every piece produced carries a Discreet Asset Tag (see Threadbourne's `threadbourne-asset-tag-manifest.md`) identifying it as lab-grown at the point of creation — full transparency rather than passing lab-grown stones off as mined.

## Stage One: Controlled Crystal Growth

- **Precision Growth Chambers:** Gemstones are grown under precisely controlled temperature, pressure, and chemical conditions replicating the natural formation process at dramatically accelerated speed.
- **Structural Fidelity:** The resulting stones are chemically and structurally identical to mined equivalents at the molecular level — not simulants or synthetics in the lower-quality sense, but the genuine mineral, grown rather than extracted.

## Stage Two: Precious Metal Refinement

- **Recycled & Refined Sourcing:** Precious metals are sourced from recycled material and refined on-site to the purity standards required for fine jewelry work, reducing dependency on new mining for the metal components alongside the stones themselves.

## Stage Three: Cutting & Finishing

- **Precision Cutting:** Grown stones proceed to precision cutting and polishing using the same master-level standards as mined gemstones intended for fine jewelry.
- **Quality Grading:** Every finished piece is graded against the same clarity, color, and cut standards used industry-wide for mined stones, ensuring genuine parity rather than a "good enough" lab-grown standard.

## Stage Four: Authentication & Distribution

- **Full Transparency Tagging:** Every piece is tagged at creation identifying its lab-grown origin, growth date, and facility — sold on the basis of ethical sourcing as a genuine selling point rather than something to obscure.
- **Household & Commercial Use:** Feeds directly into the household's own jewelry and Footwear Vault pieces (see the Lesbos Estate), alongside broader commercial sales through the empire's retail holdings.
