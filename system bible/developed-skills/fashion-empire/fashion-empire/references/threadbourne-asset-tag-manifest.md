## System Environment & Core Parameters

- **System Environment:** Near-invisible electronic tags embedded directly into a garment or accessory during manufacturing — woven into a seam, embedded beneath a button shank, or laminated within a sole — rather than attached as a visible external marker.
- **Target Scope:** Permanent digital identity, provenance record, ownership chain, and live usage tracking for any tagged item, readable instantly by any licensed scanner without requiring the item to be removed, opened, or physically inspected.
- **Activation Time:** Tag pairing to the Wardrobe Intelligence System (or, for externally sold goods, to a licensed retailer's own inventory platform) completed at the point of manufacture — no separate activation step required at point of sale.
- **Read Range & Speed:** Instant read on approach to any licensed scanner, sub-second response, no line-of-sight requirement.

## Stage One: Manufacturing Integration

- **Embedding Method:** Tags are integrated during garment construction rather than added afterward — sewn into a seam allowance, set beneath a hardware component, or laminated between sole layers, depending on the item category.
- **Durability Standard:** Rated to survive the full expected lifespan of the garment, including regular laundering, dry cleaning, and normal wear, without signal degradation.
- **Zero External Marking:** No visible tag, sticker, or hologram of any kind — authentication and tracking are entirely invisible to anyone handling the physical item.

## Stage Two: Data Architecture

- **Identity Record:** A unique, non-duplicable digital identity assigned to the specific physical item at the moment of manufacture — not the product line, the individual piece.
- **Provenance Chain:** A complete, tamper-evident record of the item's manufacturing origin, any authorized resale or transfer, and current ownership status.
- **Usage Logging:** Within a household or private collection using the full Wardrobe Intelligence System, each time an item is retrieved, worn, and returned is logged automatically — the mechanism behind AWS's usage-based tailoring-priority queue (see the AWS product manifest).

## Stage Three: Authentication Protocol

- **Instant Verification:** Any licensed retailer, resale platform, or authentication partner can confirm an item's authenticity in real time by scanning the embedded tag — no physical inspection, no reliance on a human expert's trained eye.
- **Counterfeit Detection:** An item without a valid, matching tag record is immediately flagged as unverified rather than authenticated by default, inverting the traditional burden of proof in the counterfeit market.
- **Ownership Transfer Logging:** A legitimate resale updates the provenance chain automatically once processed through a licensed partner, preserving a clean, verifiable history for the item's next owner.

## Stage Four: Licensing & Industry Integration

- **External Licensing Model:** Made available to outside luxury houses and resale platforms on a per-unit or platform-licensing basis, positioning the technology as shared industry infrastructure rather than a closed, single-brand feature.
- **Internal Empire Adoption:** Deployed across participating holdings, giving the parent structure visibility into authentication data across its own brand portfolio subject to company and privacy controls.
- **Adoption Variance:** Not universally adopted — some independent or heritage houses may decline integration for reasons of cost, tradition, or founder resistance to tracking technology, leaving a believable, low-key gap in industry-wide coverage.
