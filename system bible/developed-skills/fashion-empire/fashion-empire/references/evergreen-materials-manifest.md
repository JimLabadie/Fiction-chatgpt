(System Specification: Program Variant TMI-2)

## System Environment & Core Parameters

- **System Environment:** A supply-chain verification and certification program, not a physical device — software and auditing infrastructure layered across a garment or material's entire journey from raw source to finished product.
- **Target Scope:** Full-chain material sourcing transparency, verified-ethical labor certification, and environmental impact tracking, licensed across the empire's own holdings and to outside luxury houses.
- **Verification Method:** Combines the Discreet Asset Tag Platform's item-level tracking (see Threadbourne's `threadbourne-asset-tag-manifest.md`) with supplier-level auditing to build a complete, verifiable chain from raw material to finished garment.

## Stage One: Supplier Auditing

- **On-Site Verification:** Independent auditing of raw material sources — textile mills, tanneries, precious metal refiners — against labor and environmental standards, rather than relying on supplier self-reporting.
- **Ongoing Monitoring:** Certified suppliers are subject to periodic re-audit rather than a one-time certification that goes unchecked afterward.

## Stage Two: Chain-of-Custody Tracking

- **Material-Level Tagging:** Raw materials are tagged at the point of certified sourcing, with that tag's data carried forward through every stage of processing and manufacture.
- **Integration with Threadbourne:** Once a material becomes a finished garment fitted with its own Discreet Asset Tag, the item's full sourcing history — not just its post-manufacture identity — is available in the same record.

## Stage Three: Certification & Labeling

- **Verified-Ethical Certification:** A finished piece meeting the full chain-of-custody standard carries verified certification, distinguishing genuinely audited sourcing from unverified marketing claims common elsewhere in the industry.
- **Consumer & Partner Transparency:** Certification data is available to any licensed retailer or, where a brand chooses, directly to the end consumer.

## Positioning Relative to the Empire's Own Practices

The Traceroot Initiative sits in genuine tension with empire-scale ownership and some of Threadbourne's manufacturing ambitions. A story need not resolve that tension neatly.
