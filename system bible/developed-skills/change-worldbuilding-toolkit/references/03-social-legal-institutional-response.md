> **CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR AI:** Check the Disclosure Status Map before writing any scene where a secondary character might react to a change. Do not let someone marked "unaware" act informed, or someone marked "aware" act oblivious, without an on-page reveal or reversal logged in Section 6.
