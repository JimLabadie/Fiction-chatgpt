# Presentation Technology Subsidiaries

*Reusable reference. Two standalone corporate entities under the same parent holding structure as the rest of the empire — a deliberate, over-the-top science-fiction element, on-brand with everything else in this world. The core narrative function: the anchor, without consciously realizing why at the time, built herself a safety net for physical transition long before she understood she needed one — doing the heavy lifting a story doesn't want to spend chapters on, the same dramatic-irony logic as the estate's own origin story in `obsidian-vanguard-estate`. **Both NAS and OAM are genuine commercial enterprises with real initial product offerings, valuations, and investment structure — not devices that exist solely for the anchor's private household use.** A story is free to have the anchor's own units be the flagship/prototype installations, with the broader commercial line rolling out alongside or after her own story beats.

## Neo-Anatomical Systems Corp (NAS)

**Corporate Mandate:** Advanced, deliberately science-fictional physical presentation technology — external, visible presentation only. This entity does **not** cover reproductive, genital, urinary, or any internal intimate anatomy in clinical/explicit terms, and does not target any specific age or use virginity/sexual-history framing of any kind. Any story beat involving a character's intimate/sexual history is handled entirely through ordinary prose, with no mechanical or anatomical specification needed — that's a writing choice, not a technology one, and this file deliberately stays out of it.

**Acquisition, Not Invention:** Unlike every other technology subsidiary in this holdings structure — OAM, Threadbourne, and Evergreen Biofoundry (see below and `threadbourne-robotics.md`, `evergreen-biofoundry.md`) all trace back to the anchor's own recorded frustrations — NAS was **acquired outright**, an already-operational presentation-technology company the anchor purchased rather than personally patented. **The reason is simple, not a locked mystery: she wanted a way to make her body match who she actually was, years before she had the conscious words to say so.** A story doesn't need to treat this as a puzzle requiring its own arc — the anchor (or someone close to her) can state this plainly whenever it comes up, the same unhurried way the rest of this world's "it was always her" pattern gets stated once it's noticed. **Because it was acquired rather than invented, NAS falls entirely outside Cassandra Labs' scope** (see `cassandra-labs.md`) — that team only ever builds the anchor's own rant-derived patents, and NAS was never one of them. The team can, however, be commissioned to build *new*, complementary technology that integrates with NAS — ownership of NAS itself is complete and unrestricted, so nothing stops the anchor from directing her own R&D team to extend it.

**Ecosystem Integration:**
- **WIS/AWS synchronization:** Any change made through the Integration Suite updates the anchor's stored biometric profile in the Wardrobe Intelligence System automatically, so Threadbourne's AWS retrieval-and-dressing stations (see `threadbourne-aws-manifest.md`) fit and stage garments correctly from that point forward without a separate manual re-measurement step.
- **Medical Bay — deliberately no integration:** the Integration Suite's zero-recovery, fully self-contained design is intentional; it does not route through or require monitoring from the estate's Medical Bay & Wellness Center (see `obsidian-vanguard-estate`). The Medical Bay remains available for any of the household's unrelated ordinary health needs, entirely separate from this device.

**Flagship Product — the Integration Suite:**
- **Scope:** A complete external structural and dermal reconfiguration of a male body into a female one — this is the device's actual, direct purpose, not a euphemism for something gentler. Full configurable list:
  - **Skeletal/proportion framework:** bone structure, gait, shoulder width, hip and pelvic girdle proportion, overall bone density and weight (a lighter, less heavily-boned frame, independent of shape).
  - **Soft tissue and fat distribution:** breast development; waist, hip, and buttock reshaping; body-wide fat redistribution to a typically female pattern; independent muscle mass/tone softening (distinct from fat redistribution — male-pattern muscle definition can persist even after fat is redistributed, so this is its own setting).
  - **Facial feminization, broken into independently adjustable components** rather than one blanket toggle: brow ridge, jaw angle, and cheek fullness each configured separately.
  - **Extremity proportion:** hand and foot size, adjustable independently of overall height/frame — a common, specific dysphoria point.
  - **Thyroid cartilage reduction** (reducing a prominent Adam's apple), configured alongside the voice modulation below.
  - **Dermal envelope:** skin, hair growth pattern and distribution.
  - **Voice modulation.**

  Fully reversible/adjustable rather than a one-time irreversible procedure — a wearer can revisit and change any of these settings later.

**Default Configuration & Typical Ranges** (a story can override any of these per-character — this is a baseline reference, not a mandate):

| Metric | Typical Pre-Transition Baseline | Default Post-Integration Target |
|---|---|---|

| Shoulder width | Broader, male-typical | Narrowed to proportionate female-typical range |
| Waist-to-hip ratio | Typically minimal waist/hip differentiation | ~0.7 ratio (the commonly cited typical female range) |

| Bust | N/A | Cup size is a per-character choice, not defaulted — this is one of the more personal configuration decisions and should be decided in-scene, the way Jim's other choices were |
| Hand/foot size | Male-typical, larger | Reduced proportionate to new overall frame |
| Muscle tone | Male-typical definition | Softened, redistributed beneath the new fat pattern |
| Body fat percentage | ~10–15% male-typical | ~20–25%, typical female-range distribution (hip, thigh, gluteal, chest) |

This table is meant to give a scene concrete numbers to reach for by default, so a character's transformation doesn't have to rely on vague language like "curvier" or "softer" unless a story specifically wants a per-character table with exact figures instead (see the comparative-table format used elsewhere in this world, e.g. OAM's own comparative operational matrices, as a style model).
- **Full Presentation Confidence, Including Full Nudity:** the reconfigured body presents as complete, convincing female anatomy in every context, including full nudity — locker rooms, saunas, nude beaches, changing rooms — with nothing to manage, hide, or worry about, ever. This is central to the device's whole purpose: presentation the wearer can live inside completely and unguardedly, not a costume that has to be carefully maintained. This includes intimacy: the reconfigured body functions authentically as a woman's in that context too, without anything to manage, translate, or hide — the same underlying principle as full nudity confidence, extended to the most personal context it applies to. A story references that this is true and moves on; the mechanics of any intimate scene are handled entirely in ordinary prose, the same as they would be for any character.
- **Menstrual Simulation:** see Menstrual & Fertility Function Module above — kept deliberately undetailed at the mechanical/anatomical level. The point of this function isn't the biology — it's giving the wearer the choice to share directly, personally, in that particular experience of womanhood alongside partners or others who go through it, if and when she wants that, rather than always remaining an outside observer to it.
- **Fertility & Family-Planning Function:** see above — reversible, wearer-controlled, and kept deliberately undetailed at the anatomical level. The mechanism exists to make family-building a real, available choice, not to be described in clinical or explicit terms; a story references that the capability exists and moves on, the same way it wouldn't dwell on the mechanics of any other body function.
- **Explicitly out of scope:** any age-specific targeting; any virginity or sexual-history simulation of any kind; any explicit or clinical description of genital anatomy or sexual function. A story wanting an intimate-history beat writes it in prose, full stop. The line here is anatomical/structural description (breasts, waist, hips, proportion, face) versus genital/sexual-function description — the former is the device's whole stated purpose and should be named plainly; the latter stays out entirely.
- **Tone:** installation is fast, painless, near-instant by design (matching the sci-fi register of the rest of this world) — the story doesn't need to dwell on procedure mechanics any more than it dwells on how the estate's elevators work.

## Omnitech Aesthetic & Body Modification Group (OAM)

**Corporate Mandate:** To deliver turnkey, parallelized physical reinventions through fully automated suites, entirely removing human technician error and standard sequential time constraints from high-end salon, piercing, and tattoo services. Operates as a completely separate, standalone corporate entity under the exact same parent holding structure as NAS.

**Origin — the rant behind the company:** Unlike NAS, OAM was born from one of the anchor's own recorded frustrations, the same pattern as Threadbourne and Evergreen Biofoundry (see below and `threadbourne-robotics.md`, `evergreen-biofoundry.md`). **Recommended default for any story using this:** the anchor never once set foot in a salon, had a piercing, or got a tattoo before her own realization — nothing here comes from personal experience of the world OAM serves. The origin instead comes from an obsessive, disproportionate research binge during unrelated due-diligence work, the kind of fixation that made no business sense at the time and makes a great deal of sense in hindsight. Filed in a late-night memo, ostensibly assessing a small, unrelated potential acquisition:

*"I've read four hundred complaint transcripts tonight about scheduling conflicts, botched color jobs, and appointments running two hours over, and I don't understand why an entire industry is built around hoping a person having a bad day doesn't also have shaky hands that day. This isn't a two-hundred-thousand-dollar acquisition question. I don't know why I've spent forty hours on it. I just kept reading."*

That memo, filed under due-diligence notes nobody thought to question, became the founding technical brief for OAM's entire zero-error automation philosophy.

- **Multi-Point Piercing Curation System:** *"I fell down a research hole tonight reading about piercing infections and asymmetric cartilage work, for reasons I can't actually justify to anyone, including myself. - **Multi-Needle Micro-Pigmentation Array:** *"I saw a photograph of a tattoo today — thin linework, a constellation, nothing special by professional standards probably — and I've been thinking about it for six hours. I looked up pigment migration rates and needle gauge standards until two in the morning like it mattered to my actual life in some way. It doesn't. I still couldn't stop."*
- **Olfactory Suite:** *"Every single one of these smells like a middle manager's idea of what confidence smells like. Cedar, leather, something the bottle calls 'ambition.' None of it smells like anything I'd actually want to smell like. I don't even know what that would smell like. I just know, with total certainty, that it isn't this."*
- **ChromaBlend Dispensary:** *"I have been looking at shade charts for three hours trying to buy a gift for someone I've never met in person. Twelve shades. An entire industry built around helping people look like themselves, and it offers twelve shades, for eight billion people, like that's a reasonable place to stop trying."*

**A pattern worth naming for any story's reveal scene:** every one of these came from research, complaint transcripts, a single photograph, or an unrelated home-renovation decision — never from the anchor personally using, wearing, or visiting anything OAM makes. That absence is itself part of the evidence Athena (or an equivalent) can point to: nobody spends this many unpaid, unexplainable hours obsessing over an industry they have zero personal stake in, unless some part of them already knew they would.

**Flagship System — the Aura-Matrix V-10 Treatment Pod:** An obsidian-trimmed sculptural treatment pod with biometric sensors and multi-axis micro-articulated nano-dispensers. When a subject steps onto the diagnostic platform, the internal environment configures itself to their target presentation profile within seconds.

**Consultation & Selection Process:** Before any execution, the wearer goes through a live consultation using the Aura-Matrix Preview Array (the same rendering technology behind AWS's mannequin preview and the household's vanity mirrors) — every option below is shown rendered on the wearer before anything is actually applied. Two paths through this consultation, consistent across all three modules below:
1. **Curated Packages** — pre-designed, named combinations at set intensity tiers (minimalist/moderate/maximalist), built to look cohesive together. Fast, low-decision-fatigue, the default most wearers are steered toward first.
2. **Full Custom Build** — the wearer selects individual items from the complete catalog one at a time, previewed live as they go, for full control.

The pod then executes across three synchronized phases, each taking roughly one hour under full automation regardless of how much or how little was selected within it (versus 9–11 hours of staggered manual labor, traditionally, for an equivalent full session):

1. **Salon, Hydrotherapy, & Hair Architecture (Hour 1):** the full hair, nail, skin, body, brow/lash, and makeup service catalog, selected via curated package or full custom build — see `oam-salon-manifest.md` for the complete option set and packages.
2. **Body Piercing Curation & Fine Jewelry Integration (Hour 2):** the full piercing catalog across ear, facial/nose, oral/lip, and torso/surface regions, selected the same way — see `oam-piercing-manifest.md` for the complete option set and packages. No genital/intimate placements are included, by deliberate design choice.
3. **Automated Dermal Tattooing & Fine-Line Pigmentation (Hour 3):** the full tattoo style, placement, size, and color catalog, selected the same way — see `oam-tattoo-manifest.md` for the complete option set and packages.

## A Reusable Story Pattern: What the Staff Already Suspected
Because this technology's presence at a private estate is itself unusual — nobody builds or acquires infrastructure this specifically suited to presentation transition without a reason — household staff who are aware of it (estate staff, salon staff, anyone with visibility into what NAS/OAM actually do) have a natural, quiet basis to have already suspected or assumed the anchor was trans, well before any on-page reveal or before the anchor herself consciously knew. This is a reusable emotional beat: the anchor discovering that people around her had already, gently, correctly intuited something about her that she hadn't yet found the words for herself — mirroring the pattern of the anchor's own build choices unconsciously revealing her long before she understood them.

For any character in a story who is herself trans and has personal history with loss in her own community, encountering this technology is likely to land as complicated rather than simple: not resentment that it exists, but grief that it didn't exist in time for people she's already lost. That reaction — wishing this had existed for someone specific, rather than begrudging the anchor for having it — is worth writing with real weight rather than glossing past. Specific character details belong in that story's own files, not here.
