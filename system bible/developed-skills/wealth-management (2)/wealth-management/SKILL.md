# Wealth Management

A reusable, tiered reference for how an anchor character's wealth is actually structured — trusts, holding companies, staffing — scaled from modest complexity up through deliberately absurd, empire-funding scale. Grounded in real-world family office practice at the lower/middle tiers; openly non-realistic at the top tier by design, matching the tone of `obsidian-vanguard-estate` and `fashion-empire`.

## What This Skill Covers (and What It Doesn't)

Covers: the core trust/holding-company entity structure, what financial/legal experts actually cost to retain, a tiered guidance table scaling from ~$1M up through empire-funding wealth, a standard illustrative asset-allocation model, governance norms, the **partner safety layer** (financial/legal protection for a polyamorous household's members), and the **shell-corporation/holding-company layer** that houses a story's corporate empire.

Does **not** cover:
- The estate itself (architecture, staffing) — see `obsidian-vanguard-estate`.
- The corporate empire's holdings, brands, or capital deployment — see `fashion-empire`. This skill covers *how* that empire's capital is legally organized and protected, not what it owns or costs.
- A specific story's actual net worth figure, or which tier applies — each story sets this independently; ask rather than assume.

## Files in This Skill

- `references/wealth-structure.md` — the full tiered reference: entity structure, expert costs, tiered guidance (including the top, empire-scale tier), asset allocation, governance, the partner safety layer, and the shell-corporation layer.

## Who Actually Administers This, at the Top Tier

At empire-scale wealth, the trust/holding-company architecture, the partner safety layer, and the shell-corporation layer described in `wealth-structure.md` are plausibly too complex for any human family office to administer entirely by hand day to day. If a story also uses `fashion-empire`, that skill's sentient capital-deployment system (see its `references/athena-the-algorithm.md`) is a natural fit as the entity actually building and maintaining these structures — not deciding their values independently, but faithfully executing protection directives the anchor herself set, the same logic that governs everything else that system does. This isn't required to use this skill; without `fashion-empire`, treat this file's structures as administered by the human financial/legal experts already described below.

## Workflow

1. **New story wants to use this skill:** read `references/wealth-structure.md`. Ask the user what tier applies (don't guess or reuse a previous story's figure) — tiers are illustrative reference points, not hard boundaries, so a story's actual number can sit between or above them.
2. **A story's empire (via `fashion-empire`) needs its capital structured or protected from regulatory/antitrust exposure:** that's the shell-corporation/holding-company layer in this file — read it alongside `fashion-empire`'s own "Regulatory Anti-Monopoly Bottlenecks" gap for the connective logic.
3. **A new partner joins the household (via `polyamory-family` or a story's own household structure):** the partner safety layer in this file is the reusable mechanism for how that person is actually protected, financially and legally, independent of the relationship's future. Story-specific details (which partner, what was actually set up for them, when) belong in that story's own state-of-world file, not here.
4. **Absurd top-tier wealth doesn't need re-justifying every time it comes up.** Once a story establishes its anchor is at the top tier, later scenes can treat that wealth as background texture — a detail mentioned in passing — rather than something that needs fresh explanation or justification each time it's referenced.
