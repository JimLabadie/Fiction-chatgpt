## Density result

With these two additions, the maintained named specialty-retail set reaches the established minimum target of 30 based on the last full audited baseline plus the explicitly categorized additions in 05C and this file.

Do not continue adding specialty shops merely to move farther into the 30–60 range. Thirty is a floor, not a quota to maximize. Additional shops should arise from a demonstrated missing function, a story need, or a future live audit showing that district growth requires more commercial mass.
