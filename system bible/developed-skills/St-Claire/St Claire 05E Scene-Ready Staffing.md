# St. Claire — Scene-Ready Staffing

Status: OPERATIONAL STRUCTURED DATA — NAMED STAFFING

This file instantiates the people required to make the ordinary-commerce businesses scene-ready. It supplements 05C/05D. A business is not considered scene-ready merely because an unnamed staffing slot exists: its normal operating coverage must resolve to named people with schedules and usable character facts.

## Governing rules

- Staff are created in small batches and checked against the demographic rules in `St Claire 00B Rules and Mechanics.md` and against existing names before promotion.
- Every named worker has a full date of birth, presentation/physical baseline, voice, occupation/role, work schedule, household/relationship state, arrival status, routine/haunt, specific trait, and connections sufficient for immediate scene use.
- A person may be off duty. Named staff are not permanently installed behind a counter.
- Relationship status and presentation are tracked to prevent staffing generation from drifting toward single/femme defaults.
- This file begins with one business completed end-to-end rather than partially naming staff across many businesses. Subsequent batches continue business by business.

# Batch 1–2 — Hearth & Home Service Cooperative

## Scene-ready operating model

Hearth & Home runs six two-person field crews plus dispatch. Normal public hours are 7:00 AM–7:00 PM Monday–Saturday. Three crews work 7:00 AM–3:30 PM and three work 10:30 AM–7:00 PM, with staggered lunches; the overlap handles the midday peak. The cooperative rotates one two-person after-hours emergency crew weekly. Sunday has no ordinary appointments, only the emergency pair. The dispatch desk is covered continuously during public hours by two overlapping coordinators. Major licensed electrical/plumbing/HVAC work is coordinated with St. Claire Builders & Co.; these crews handle ordinary repair, appliance, maintenance, diagnostic, and handyperson work.
