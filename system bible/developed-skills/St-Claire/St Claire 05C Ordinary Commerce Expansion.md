# St. Claire — Ordinary Commerce Expansion

Status: OPERATIONAL STRUCTURED DATA — FUNCTIONAL COMPLETENESS BUILD

This file adds the ordinary commercial functions identified by the closed-structure audit. It is deliberately mundane: these are the businesses that let roughly 14,500 residents live ordinary lives inside St. Claire. It supplements `St Claire 05 Organizations.md` and `St Claire 05A Recovered Organizations.md`.

Names, functions, scale, and locations below are established organization facts. Individual owners and staff remain unnamed unless already established elsewhere; per the Organizations template, unnamed staffing slots are not gaps and should be named only when a story needs them.

## Important correction from the audit

`St. Claire Builders & Co.` already contains a Master Electrician, Master Plumber, HVAC technician, finish carpenter, painter, roofer, mason, framer, and general contractor. Those trades were therefore not missing from the district. The gap was **household-service access and ordinary repair coverage**, not the existence of those professions. Builders remains the construction/renovation contractor; the new home-service organization below handles routine service calls, maintenance, and small repairs.

## Home, Repair & Daily Services

## Coverage result

This build intentionally closes the most obvious ordinary-year functional gaps identified in `St Claire 05B Organization Needs.md`: routine home/appliance repair, laundry/dry cleaning, moving/local delivery, funeral/end-of-life service, optometry, physical therapy, shoes/repair, electronics/phone/computer repair, furniture, appliances, office supplies/printing, housewares, insurance navigation, and additional fabrication and food density.

It does **not** duplicate St. Claire Builders & Co.'s established electrician/plumber/HVAC trades. It gives those trades a clear relationship to routine household service while preserving Builders as the construction/renovation organization.

Future expansion should be driven by a demonstrated missing function, live density count, or story need — not by the assumption that every plausible business deserves a named record.
