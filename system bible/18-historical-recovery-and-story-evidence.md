# Historical Recovery and Story Evidence

Status: ACTIVE EVIDENCE LEDGER
Authority: EVIDENCE NOT DIRECT CANON

This module proves what was said, accepted, rejected, copied, or superseded. It never overwrites current canon automatically.

## Archive principle

Duplication and disorder arose partly from repeated attempts to keep the project alive across unreliable AI contexts, exports, and tools. Preserve fragments and variants until their relationship is understood. Do not sanitize the comedy from repeated `final` filenames and escalating declarations of finality; they are both survival evidence and archive history.

## Evidence classes

- direct user canon decision;
- accepted story event;
- user correction to assistant drift;
- mixed conversation containing decisions and suggestions;
- unaccepted assistant proposal;
- current snapshot;
- derived consolidation/report;
- duplicate or near-duplicate;
- superseded version;
- explicitly rejected material;
- provenance-only artifact.

For each disputed fact record exact source, date if known, speaker/author, context, stated status, competing versions, controlling authority, and resolution. Filename, modification date, repetition, or detail cannot resolve a dispute alone.
