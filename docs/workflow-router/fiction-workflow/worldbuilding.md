# Worldbuilding

### Scope & Activation

Use for reusable setting material: worlds, places, institutions, cultures, social systems, technologies, companies, organizations, infrastructure, rules, and other setting facts that may support one or more stories.

### Authoritative Sources

* Governing durable world material lives under **Fiction Docs → Worlds**.
* Reusable fiction-wide material belongs under **Shared Fiction Framework**, not inside a story or world merely because it was first used there.
* Supporting source files and research may live in Emily Google when they are not themselves governing canon.

### Boundaries

Do not bury reusable world material inside a single story. Do not promote research, brainstorming, or a source document into canon without approval. Preserve uncertainty and contradictions until resolved.

### Procedure

1. Identify the world or reusable domain being changed.
2. Retrieve the live world record and only the story/shared-framework dependencies materially needed.
3. Separate established facts, proposals, open questions, and external research.
4. Develop or revise the material without silently changing dependent canon.
5. Persist approved durable changes to the governing world/shared-framework record and verify by read-back.

### Interaction

Use Canon & Continuity when the change could contradict existing material or affect multiple stories. Use Source & Research Management when source provenance or migration is involved.
