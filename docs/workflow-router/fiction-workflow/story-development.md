# Story Development

### Scope & Activation

Use for work owned by one story: story state, characters, relationships, chapters, scenes, outlines, development decisions, manuscript planning, handoffs, and story-specific setting material.

### Authoritative Sources

* Each story lives under **Fiction Docs → Stories →** .
* Retrieve the live Stories structure first rather than relying on a hardcoded story list.
* World or shared-framework material remains authoritative in its own container when the story references it.

### Boundaries

Keep story-specific material inside the story container. Do not copy reusable world/framework material into the story as a second governing version. Approved chapters, drafts, historical material, current state, and proposals must remain distinguishable.

### Procedure

1. Resolve the story container from the live Fiction Docs structure.
2. Retrieve the smallest set of current story records needed for the request.
3. Check world/shared-framework dependencies when materially relevant.
4. Perform the requested planning, writing, revision, or audit.
5. Treat approval as approval of the specific proposed story change, not unrelated canon.
6. Persist approved durable state to the story container and verify.

### Interaction

Use Canon & Continuity for contradictions, timeline issues, recovery, or canon promotion. Use Creative Working Style / Kate Partnership for brainstorming and writing collaboration.
